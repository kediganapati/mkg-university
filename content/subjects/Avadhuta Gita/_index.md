The Avadhuta Gita is presented here in its available chapters — 8 chapters comprising 289 shlokas, traditionally regarded as spoken directly by Bhagwan Dattatreya himself.

Interpretations of some shlokas are still pending because I have not yet gained sufficient personal experience to interpret them. As my experience and understanding grow, I will continue adding interpretations until all chapters are covered.

Numbering of shlokas is not my own — it comes from available sources. I do not agree with or support every shloka as absolute reality; some portions may have been shaped by later transmission across centuries. So I am only giving the interpretation and meaning of the shlokas, not writing a new Avadhuta Gita.
