<a id="shloka1"></a>

**Shloka 12:1**

*अर्जुन उवाच ।*
*एवं सततयुक्ता ये भक्तास्त्वां पर्युपासते ।*
*ये चाप्यक्षरमव्यक्तं तेषां के योगवित्तमाः ॥ १२:१ ॥*

*arjuna uvacha*
*evam satata-yukta ye bhaktas tvam paryupasate*
*ye capy aksaram avyaktam tesam ke yoga-vittamah*

**Translation**

Arjuna said: Those devotees who are always steadfast and worship You with devotion, and those who worship the imperishable, unmanifest Absolute—which of them is considered more perfect in yoga?

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka2"></a>

**Shloka 12:2**

*श्रीभगवानुवाच ।*
*मय्यावेश्य मनो ये मां नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ।*
*श्रद्धया परयोपेतास्ते मे युक्ततमा मताः ॥ १२:२ ॥*

*shri bhagavan uvacha*
*mayy avesya mano ye mam nitya-yukta upasate*
*sraddhaya parayopetas te me yuktatama matah*

**Translation**

The Supreme Lord said: Those who fix their minds on Me, worship Me with constant devotion, and are endowed with supreme faith are considered by Me to be the most perfect in yoga.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka3"></a>

**Shloka 12:3**

*ये त्वक्षरमनिर्देश्यमव्यक्तं पर्युपासते ।*
*सर्वत्रगमचिन्त्यं च कूटस्थमचलं ध्रुवम् ॥ १२:३ ॥*

*ye tv aksaram anirdesyam avyaktam paryupasate*
*sarvatra-gam acintyam ca kuta-stham acalam dhruvam*

**Translation**

But those who worship the imperishable, indefinable, unmanifest, all-pervading, inconceivable, unchanging, immovable, and eternal Reality also follow the path of spiritual realization.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka4"></a>

**Shloka 12:4**

*सन्नियम्येन्द्रियग्रामं सर्वत्र समबुद्धयः ।*
*ते प्राप्नुवन्ति मामेव सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥ १२:४ ॥*

*sanniyamyendriya-gramam sarvatra sama-buddhayah*
*te prapnuvanti mam eva sarva-bhuta-hite ratah*

**Translation**

Having controlled all the senses, remaining equal-minded everywhere, and delighting in the welfare of all beings, they too ultimately attain Me.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka5"></a>

**Shloka 12:5**

*क्लेशोऽधिकतरस्तेषामव्यक्तासक्तचेतसाम् ।*
*अव्यक्ता हि गतिर्दुःखं देहवद्भिरवाप्यते ॥ १२:५ ॥*

*kleso 'dhikataras tesam avyaktasakta-cetasam*
*avyakta hi gatir duhkham dehavadbhir avapyate*

**Translation**

For those whose minds are attached to the unmanifest, the path is more difficult, because the unmanifest goal is hard for embodied beings to realize.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka6"></a>

**Shloka 12:6**

*ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्पराः ।*
*अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते ॥ १२:६ ॥*

*ye tu sarvani karmani mayi sannyasya mat-parah*
*ananyenaiva yogena mam dhyayanta upasate*

**Translation**

But those who dedicate all their actions to Me, accept Me as the supreme goal, and worship Me with single-minded devotion while constantly meditating on Me—

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka7"></a>

**Shloka 12:7**

*तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात् ।*
*भवामि नचिरात्पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम् ॥ १२:७ ॥*

*tesam aham samuddharta mrtyu-samsara-sagarat*
*bhavami na cirat partha mayy avesita-cetasam*

**Translation**

O Arjuna, for those whose minds are fully absorbed in Me, I quickly become the deliverer from the ocean of birth and death.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka8"></a>

**Shloka 12:8**

*मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय ।*
*निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः ॥ १२:८ ॥*

*mayy eva mana adhatsva mayi buddhim nivesaya*
*nivasisyasi mayy eva ata urdhvam na samsayah*

**Translation**

Fix your mind on Me alone and place your intellect in Me. Then you shall certainly live in Me forever. There is no doubt about this.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka9"></a>

**Shloka 12:9**

*अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम् ।*
*अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनञ्जय ॥ १२:९ ॥*

*atha cittam samadhatum na saknosi mayi sthiram*
*abhyasa-yogena tato mam icchaptum dhananjaya*

**Translation**

If you are unable to steadily fix your mind on Me, then, O Dhananjaya, seek to attain Me through the practice of repeated spiritual discipline.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka10"></a>

**Shloka 12:10**

*अभ्यासेऽप्यसमर्थोऽसि मत्कर्मपरमो भव ।*
*मदर्थमपि कर्माणि कुर्वन्सिद्धिमवाप्स्यसि ॥ १२:१० ॥*

*abhyase 'py asamartho 'si mat-karma-paramo bhava*
*mad-artham api karmani kurvan siddhim avapsyasi*

**Translation**

If you are unable even to practice such discipline, then dedicate yourself to performing actions for My sake. By working for Me, you shall attain perfection.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka11"></a>

**Shloka 12:11**

*अथैतदप्यशक्तोऽसि कर्तुं मद्योगमाश्रितः ।*
*सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं ततः कुरु यतात्मवान् ॥ १२:११ ॥*

*athaitad apy asakto 'si kartum mad-yogam asritah*
*sarva-karma-phala-tyagam tatah kuru yatatmavan*

**Translation**

The Supreme Lord said: If you are unable to do even this, then take refuge in My yoga and, with self-control, renounce the fruits of all your actions.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka12"></a>

**Shloka 12:12**

*श्रेयो हि ज्ञानमभ्यासाज्ज्ञानाद्ध्यानं विशिष्यते ।*
*ध्यानात्कर्मफलत्यागस्त्यागाच्छान्तिरनन्तरम् ॥ १२:१२ ॥*

*sreyo hi jnanam abhyasaj jnanad dhyanam visisyate*
*dhyanat karma-phala-tyagas tyagac chantir anantaram*

**Translation**

Knowledge is superior to mere practice; meditation is superior to knowledge; and renunciation of the fruits of actions is superior to meditation, for peace immediately follows such renunciation.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka13"></a>

**Shloka 12:13**

*अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च ।*
*निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥ १२:१३ ॥*

*adveshta sarva-bhutanam maitrah karuna eva ca*
*nirmamo nirahankarah sama-duhkha-sukhah ksami*

**Translation**

One who has no hatred toward any living being, who is friendly and compassionate, free from possessiveness and ego, equal in happiness and distress, and forgiving—such a devotee is dear to Me.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka14"></a>

**Shloka 12:14**

*सन्तुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः ।*
*मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥ १२:१४ ॥*

*santustah satatam yogi yatatma drdha-niscayah*
*mayy arpita-mano-buddhir yo mad-bhaktah sa me priyah*

**Translation**

One who is always content, self-controlled, firmly determined, and has dedicated both mind and intellect to Me—such a devotee is dear to Me.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka15"></a>

**Shloka 12:15**

*यस्मान्नोद्विजते लोको लोकान्नोद्विजते च यः ।*
*हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः ॥ १२:१५ ॥*

*yasman nodvijate loko lokan nodvijate ca yah*
*harsa-marsa-bhayodvegair mukto yah sa ca me priyah*

**Translation**

One by whom the world is not disturbed, and who is not disturbed by the world, who is free from joy, envy, fear, and anxiety—such a devotee is dear to Me.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka16"></a>

**Shloka 12:16**

*अनपेक्षः शुचिर्दक्ष उदासीनो गतव्यथः ।*
*सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥ १२:१६ ॥*

*anapeksah sucir daksa udasino gata-vyathah*
*sarvarambha-parityagi yo mad-bhaktah sa me priyah*

**Translation**

One who is free from expectations, pure, skillful, impartial, free from anxiety, and who has renounced selfish undertakings—such a devotee is dear to Me.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka17"></a>

**Shloka 12:17**

*यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङ्क्षति ।*
*शुभाशुभपरित्यागी भक्तिमान्यः स मे प्रियः ॥ १२:१७ ॥*

*yo na hrsyati na dvesti na socati na kanksati*
*subhasubha-parityagi bhaktiman yah sa me priyah*

**Translation**

One who neither rejoices nor hates, neither grieves nor desires, and who has renounced attachment to both good and bad results—such a devoted person is dear to Me.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka18"></a>

**Shloka 12:18**

*समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च तथा मानापमानयोः ।*
*शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु समः सङ्गविवर्जितः ॥ १२:१८ ॥*

*samah satrau ca mitre ca tatha mana-apamanayoh*
*sitosna-sukha-duhkhesu samah sanga-vivarjitah*

**Translation**

One who remains equal toward friend and enemy, honor and dishonor, heat and cold, happiness and distress, and is free from attachment—is dear to Me.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka19"></a>

**Shloka 12:19**

*तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिर्मौनी सन्तुष्टो येन केनचित् ।*
*अनिकेतः स्थिरमतिर्भक्तिमान्मे प्रियो नरः ॥ १२:१९ ॥*

*tulya-ninda-stutir mauni santusto yena kenacit*
*aniketah sthira-matir bhaktiman me priyo narah*

**Translation**

One who is the same in praise and blame, restrained in speech, content with whatever comes, unattached to any residence, steady in mind, and full of devotion—such a person is dear to Me.

**Commentary**

---

<a id="shloka20"></a>

**Shloka 12:20**

*ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते ।*
*श्रद्दधाना मत्परमा भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः ॥ १२:२० ॥*

*ye tu dharmyamrtam idam yathoktam paryupasate*
*sraddadhana mat-parama bhaktas te 'tiva me priyah*

**Translation**

But those faithful devotees who follow this immortal path of righteousness as described above, accepting Me as their supreme goal, are exceedingly dear to Me.

**Commentary**
