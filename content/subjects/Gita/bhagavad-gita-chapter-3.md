<a id="shloka1"></a>

**Shloka 3:1**

*अर्जुन उवाच*  
*ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन ।*  
*तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव ॥ १ ॥*

*arjuna uvacha*  
*jyayasi chet karmanas te mata buddhir janardana*  
*tat kim karmani ghore mam niyojayasi keshava*

**Translation**

Arjuna said: O Janardana, if You consider knowledge to be superior to action, then why do You ask me, O Keshava, to engage in this terrible act of war?

**Conversation**

**Kedi:** What is the meaning of Bhagavad Gita, Verse 3:1?

**Ganesh:** Arjuna said to Lord Krishna, "O Janardana, if You consider knowledge to be superior to action, then O Keshava, why are You engaging me in such a terrible action as war?"

**Kedi:** How is knowledge superior to action?

**Ganesh:** When someone performs an action but has no understanding of why they are performing it, then it is not action—it is slavery. And when someone has knowledge, they know whether the action they are being told to perform is right or not. If it is not right, then they do not perform that action.

**Kedi:** Is it also right to not perform an action?

**Ganesh:** It is right to perform the right action, and it is right to refuse to perform the wrong action. The understanding of what is a right action and what is a wrong action comes from knowledge. That is why knowledge is superior to action.

**Kedi:** Arjuna is realizing that fighting the war is a terrible action and that he should not do it. So Arjuna is refusing to fight, using his knowledge to avoid performing a wrong action. Then why is Lord Krishna telling him to fight?

**Ganesh:** Knowing when it is right to fight and when it is wrong to fight is itself knowledge. At that time, fighting the war was the right thing to do. That is the knowledge Lord Krishna was giving to Arjuna.

<a id="shloka2"></a>

**Shloka 3:2**

*अर्जुन उवाच*  
*व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे ।*  
*तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्नुयाम् ॥ २ ॥*

*arjuna uvacha*  
*vyamishreneva vakyena buddhim mohayasiva me*  
*tad ekam vada nishchitya yena shreyo'ham apnuyam*

**Translation**

Arjuna said: Your words seem to present different paths, leaving my mind confused. Therefore, please tell me clearly the one path by which I can attain the highest good.

**Conversation**

**Kedi:** What is the meaning of Bhagavad Gita, Verse 3:2?

**Ganesh:** Arjuna said to Lord Krishna, "Your words seem to present different paths, which is confusing my mind. Therefore, please clearly tell me the one path by which I can attain the highest welfare."

**Kedi:** Which different paths did Lord Krishna explain? I forgot.

**Ganesh:** Earlier, Lord Krishna explained the path of Karma Yoga, and now He was explaining the path of Jnana Yoga. That is why Arjuna became confused about which path he should follow.

**Kedi:** Which path is the right one to follow?

**Ganesh:** When there is a book in your hand, it is Jnana Yoga, and when there is a broom in your hand, it is Karma Yoga. In life, we have to perform different physical and intellectual activities, and based on that, we should choose the appropriate path.

**Kedi:** If someone cannot read a book or does not want to read one, does that mean they are excluded from Jnana Yoga?

**Ganesh:** Reading books is not the only way to gain knowledge. Listening to the words of knowledge from others, understanding situations and circumstances, and knowing your own thoughts and nature are also forms of Jnana Yoga.

**Shloka 3:3**

*श्रीभगवानुवाच*  
*लोकेऽस्मिन्द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा प्रोक्ता मयानघ ।*  
*ज्ञानयोगेन सांख्यानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम् ॥ ३ ॥*

*shri bhagavan uvacha*  
*loke'smin dvividha nishtha pura prokta mayanagha*  
*jnanayogena sankhyanam karmayogena yoginam*

**Translation**

The Supreme Lord said: O sinless one, I have already explained that in this world there are two paths of spiritual practice: the path of knowledge for those inclined toward contemplation, and the path of selfless action for those devoted to action.

<a id="shloka4"></a>

**Shloka 3:4**

*न कर्मणामनारम्भान्नैष्कर्म्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते ।*  
*न च संन्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति ॥ ४ ॥*

*na karmanam anarambhan naishkarmyam purusho'shnute*  
*na cha sannyasanad eva siddhim samadhigacchati*

**Translation**

A person does not attain freedom from the effects of action simply by avoiding action, nor does one achieve perfection merely by renouncing work.

**Conversation**

**Kedi:** What is the meaning of Bhagavad Gita, Verse 3:4?

**Ganesh:** Lord Krishna said to Arjuna that merely avoiding action does not free a person from the effects of actions, nor does merely renouncing action enable a person to attain perfection.

**Kedi:** What kind of freedom is Lord Krishna talking about?

**Ganesh:** Human beings have been sent to Earth to perform actions. Until those actions are completed, freedom cannot be attained. One has to be born on Earth again and again to complete the remaining actions.

**Kedi:** What does renouncing action mean? If I renounce my work at the office, I'll lose my job.

**Ganesh:** If you do not perform the responsibilities for which you are doing your job, that is renouncing action. By doing so, perfection is not attained. A person attains perfection only by completing their actions.

<a id="shloka5"></a>

**Shloka 3:5**

*न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत् ।*  
*कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः ॥ ५ ॥*

*na hi kashchit kshanam api jatu tishthaty akarmakrit*  
*karyate hy avashah karma sarvah prakritijair gunaih*

**Translation**

No one can remain without performing action even for a single moment. Everyone is compelled to act according to the qualities born of material nature.

**Conversation**

**Kedi:** What is the meaning of Bhagavad Gita, Verse 3:5?

**Ganesh:** Lord Krishna said to Arjuna that no one can remain without performing action even for a single moment. Everyone is compelled to act according to the qualities born of material nature.

**Kedi:** How is that possible? I have seen some people sit without doing anything for 8–9 years.

**Ganesh:** Remaining bound to one place continuously is also an action, and not everyone can do that. You certainly can't.

**Kedi:** Which qualities of nature are being talked about?

**Ganesh:** Nature has given human beings the qualities of Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas. Based on those qualities, people perform different kinds of actions.

**Kedi:** What is the difference between the qualities of Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas?

**Ganesh:** When a person with Sattvic qualities wakes up, they first thank God and then get out of bed. A person with Rajasic qualities has tea and breakfast in bed first, and then gets up. A person with Tamasic qualities wakes up, then goes back to sleep, wakes up again, sleeps again, and remains confused until they figure out what to do after getting up.

**Kedi:** Which guna do I belong to?

**Ganesh:** Sometimes you're Sattvic—waking up early in the morning and hitting the gym. Sometimes you're Rajasic—getting out of bed only after having breakfast on the bed. And sometimes you're Tamasic—you wake up... just to go back to sleep, and you don't get up again until you find out what chaos you're going to create today. So, you belong to the category of Hybrid Gunas. You're an exception... and, in fact, the biggest exception of them all.

**Kedi:** So, does waking up early alone determine the three gunas of nature?

**Ganesh:** Waking up early is only one aspect of lifestyle. Nature can be understood through several categories—such as lifestyle, temperaments, eating habits, tendencies, and vision, among others. Each category can express itself in Sattvic, Rajasic, or Tamasic ways. When the right time comes, I'll explain each of them in detail.

<a id="shloka7"></a>

**Shloka 3:7**

*यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन ।*  
*कर्मेन्द्रियैः कर्मयोगमसक्तः स विशिष्यते ॥ ७ ॥*

*yas tv indriyani manasa niyamyarabate'rjuna*  
*karmendriyaih karmayogam asaktah sa vishishyate*

**Translation**

But O Arjuna, one who controls the senses with the mind and performs selfless action without attachment is truly superior.

**Conversation**

**Kedi:** What is the meaning of Bhagavad Gita, Verse 3:7?

**Ganesh:** Lord Krishna said to Arjuna, "But, O Arjuna, the person who controls the senses with the mind and performs selfless action without any attachment is truly superior."

**Kedi:** What does performing selfless action mean? Does it mean doing a job without a salary?

**Ganesh:** Actions are not performed only for money; they are also performed to fulfill one's duty. Therefore, when you perform an action to fulfill your duty, you should not look for additional self-interest in it. You should complete your action with a selfless attitude.

**Kedi:** Once I performed a selfless action, but instead of being called superior, I was called a fool. Which is correct?

**Ganesh:** Expecting praise in return for your actions is also not selfless action. If you had truly performed a selfless action, then perhaps being called a fool by them would not have made any difference to you. You would have already gained the satisfaction of completing your duty. You would have become truly superior, not just superior on paper.

**Kedi:** What does "superior on paper" mean?

**Ganesh:** Some people make others appear superior on paper for their own self-interest, while others use paper money to make themselves appear superior. The one who performs actions without expectation, desire, or attachment to the results is the one who is truly superior. Superiority is not something to be shown—it is something to be lived.

*joshayet sarva karmani vidvan yuktah samacharan*

**Translation**

The wise should not disturb the understanding of those who are attached to action. Instead, by performing their own duties properly, they should encourage others to do the same.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka27"></a>

**Shloka 3:27**

*प्रकृतेः क्रियमाणानि गुणैः कर्माणि सर्वशः ।*  
*अहङ्कारविमूढात्मा करताहमिति मन्यते ॥ २७ ॥*

*prakriteh kriyamanani gunaih karmani sarvashah*  
*ahankara vimudhatma kartaham iti manyate*

**Translation**

All actions are carried out by the qualities of material nature. But one whose mind is deluded by ego thinks, "I am the doer."

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka28"></a>

**Shloka 3:28**

*तत्त्ववित्तु महाबाहो गुणकर्मविभागयोः ।*  
*गुणा गुणेषु वर्तन्त इति मत्वा न सज्जते ॥ २८ ॥*

*tattvavit tu mahabaho guna karma vibhagayoh*  
*guna guneshu vartanta iti matva na sajjate*

**Translation**

But O mighty-armed one, a person who knows the truth understands that the qualities of nature alone interact with one another. Knowing this, such a person does not become attached.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka29"></a>

**Shloka 3:29**

*प्रकृतेर्गुणसम्मूढाः सज्जन्ते गुणकर्मसु ।*  
*तानकृत्स्नविदो मन्दान्कृत्स्नविन्न विचालयेत् ॥ २९ ॥*

*prakriter guna sammudhah sajjante guna karmasu*  
*tan akritsnavido mandan kritsnavin na vichalayet*

**Translation**

Those who are deluded by the qualities of material nature become attached to their actions. A person of true understanding should not unsettle such people, whose knowledge is still incomplete.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka30"></a>

**Shloka 3:30**

*मयि सर्वाणि कर्माणि संन्यस्याध्यात्मचेतसा ।*  
*निराशीर्निर्ममो भूत्वा युध्यस्व विगतज्वरः ॥ ३० ॥*

*mayi sarvani karmani sannyasyadhyatma chetasa*  
*nirashir nirmamo bhutva yudhyasva vigata jvarah*

**Translation**

Dedicate all your actions to Me with your mind centered on the Self. Free from expectations, free from possessiveness, and free from anxiety, arise and perform your duty.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka31"></a>

**Shloka 3:31**

*ये मे मतमिदं नित्यमनुतिष्ठन्ति मानवाः ।*  
*श्रद्दावन्तोऽनसूयन्तो मुच्यन्ते तेऽपि कर्मभिः ॥ ३१ ॥*

*ye me matam idam nityam anutishthanti manavah*  
*shraddhavanto'nasuyanto muchyante te'pi karmabhih*

**Translation**

Those who faithfully and sincerely follow this teaching of Mine, without finding fault in it, become free from the bondage of their actions.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka32"></a>

**Shloka 3:32**

*ये त्वेतदभ्यसूयन्तो नानुतिष्ठन्ति मे मतम् ।*  
*सर्वज्ञानविमूढांस्तान्विद्धि नष्टानचेतसः ॥ ३२ ॥*

*ye tv etad abhyasuyanto nanutishthanti me matam*  
*sarva jnana vimudhan tan viddhi nashtan achetasah*

**Translation**

But those who criticize this teaching and refuse to follow it, know them to be confused in all knowledge, lacking true understanding, and headed toward ruin.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka33"></a>

**Shloka 3:33**

*सदृशं चेष्टते स्वस्याः प्रकृतेर्ज्ञानवानपि ।*  
*प्रकृतिं यान्ति भूतानि निग्रहः किं करिष्यति ॥ ३३ ॥*

*sadrisham cheshtate svasyah prakriter jnanavan api*  
*prakritim yanti bhutani nigrahah kim karishyati*

**Translation**

Even a wise person acts according to their own nature. All living beings follow their natural tendencies. What can mere forceful suppression accomplish?

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka34"></a>

**Shloka 3:34**

*इन्द्रियस्येन्द्रियस्यार्थे रागद्वेषौ व्यवस्थितौ ।*  
*तयोर्न वशमागच्छेत्तौ ह्यस्य परिपन्थिनौ ॥ ३४ ॥*

*indriyasyendriyasyarthe raga dveshau vyavasthitau*  
*tayor na vasham agacchet tau hy asya paripanthinau*

**Translation**

Attachment and aversion naturally arise toward the objects of the senses. One should not come under their control, for they are obstacles on the path of spiritual progress.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka35"></a>

**Shloka 3:35**

*श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।*  
*स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः ॥ ३५ ॥*

*shreyan svadharmo vigunah paradharmat svanushthitat*  
*svadharme nidhanam shreyah paradharmo bhayavahah*

**Translation**

It is better to perform one's own duty imperfectly than to perform another's duty perfectly. Even death while following one's own duty is better, for following another's path is dangerous.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka36"></a>

**Shloka 3:36**

*अर्जुन उवाच*  
*अथ केन प्रयुक्तोऽयं पापं चरति पुरुषः ।*  
*अनिच्छन्नपि वार्ष्णेय बलादिव नियोजितः ॥ ३६ ॥*

*atha kena prayukto'yam papam charati purushah*  
*anichchhann api varshneya balad iva niyojitah*

**Translation**

Arjuna said: O Krishna, what force drives a person to commit sinful actions, even against their own will, as though compelled by some unseen power?

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka37"></a>

**Shloka 3:37**

*श्रीभगवानुवाच*  
*काम एष क्रोध एष रजोगुणसमुद्भवः ।*  
*महाशनो महापाप्मा विद्ध्येनमिह वैरिणम् ॥ ३७ ॥*

*shri bhagavan uvacha*  
*kama esha krodha esha rajoguna samudbhavah*  
*mahashano mahapapma viddhy enam iha vairinam*

**Translation**

The Supreme Lord said: It is desire, which later transforms into anger, born of the mode of passion. It is all-devouring, greatly sinful, and should be recognized as the enemy in this world.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka38"></a>

**Shloka 3:38**

**Shloka 3:38**

*धूमेनाव्रियते वह्निर्यथादर्शो मलेन च ।*  
*यथोल्बेनावृतो गर्भस्तथा तेनेदमावृतम् ॥ ३८ ॥*

*dhumenavriyate vahnir yathadarsho malena cha*  
*yatholbenavrito garbhas tatha tenedam avritam*

**Translation**

Just as fire is covered by smoke, a mirror by dust, and an embryo by the womb, so knowledge is covered by desire.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka39"></a>

**Shloka 3:39**

*आवृतं ज्ञानमेतेन ज्ञानिनो नित्यवैरिणा ।*  
*कामरूपेण कौन्तेय दुष्पूरेणानलेन च ॥ ३९ ॥*

*avritam jnanam etena jnanino nitya vairina*  
*kama rupena kaunteya dushpurena analena cha*

**Translation**

O Kaunteya, knowledge is covered by this eternal enemy of the wise—desire, which is never satisfied and burns like an unquenchable fire.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka40"></a>

**Shloka 3:40**

*इन्द्रियाणि मनो बुद्धिरस्याधिष्ठानमुच्यते ।*  
*एतैर्विमोहयत्येष ज्ञानमावृत्य देहिनम् ॥ ४० ॥*

*indriyani mano buddhir asyadhishthanam uchyate*  
*etair vimohayaty esha jnanam avritya dehinam*

**Translation**

The senses, the mind, and the intellect are said to be the dwelling places of desire. Through these, it covers knowledge and deludes the embodied being.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka41"></a>

**Shloka 3:41**

*तस्मात्त्वमिन्द्रियाण्यादौ नियम्य भरतर्षभ ।*  
*पाप्मानं प्रजहि ह्येनं ज्ञानविज्ञाननाशनम् ॥ ४१ ॥*

*tasmat tvam indriyany adau niyamya bharatarshabha*  
*papmanam prajahi hy enam jnana vijnana nashanam*

**Translation**

Therefore, O best of the Bharatas, first control your senses and destroy this sinful enemy, which destroys both knowledge and wisdom.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka42"></a>

**Shloka 3:42**

*इन्द्रियाणि पराण्याहुरिन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनः ।*  
*मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्यो बुद्धेः परतस्तु सः ॥ ४२ ॥*

*indriyani parany ahur indriyebhyah param manah*  
*manasas tu para buddhir yo buddheh paratas tu sah*

**Translation**

The senses are considered superior to the body, the mind is superior to the senses, the intellect is superior to the mind, and beyond the intellect is the true Self.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka43"></a>

**Shloka 3:43**

*एवं बुद्धेः परं बुद्ध्वा संस्तभ्यात्मानमात्मना ।*  
*जहि शत्रुं महाबाहो कामरूपं दुरासदम् ॥ ४३ ॥*

*evam buddheh param buddhva samstabhyatmanam atmana*  
*jahi shatrum mahabaho kama rupam durasadam*

**Translation**

Thus, knowing the Self to be beyond the intellect, strengthen your mind through that higher understanding. O mighty-armed one, conquer this formidable enemy in the form of desire.
