<a id="shloka1"></a>

**Shloka 15:1**

*श्रीभगवानुवाच -*  
*ऊर्ध्वमूलमधःशाखमश्वत्थं प्राहुरव्ययम् ।*  
*छन्दांसि यस्य पर्णानि यस्तं वेद स वेदवित् ॥ १५:१ ॥*

*shri-bhagavan uvacha*  
*urdhva-mulam adhah-shakham ashvattham prahur avyayam*  
*chandamsi yasya parnani yas tam veda sa veda-vit*

**Translation**

The Supreme Lord said: They speak of an eternal Ashvattha (banyan) tree with its roots above and branches below. Its leaves are the Vedic hymns. One who truly understands this tree is the knower of the Vedas.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka2"></a>

**Shloka 15:2**

*अधश्चोर्ध्वं प्रसृतास्तस्य शाखा गुणप्रवृद्धा विषयप्रवालाः ।*  
*अधश्च मूलान्यनुसन्ततानि कर्मानुबन्धीनि मनुष्यलोके ॥ १५:२ ॥*

*adhash cordhvam prasritas tasya shakha*  
*guna-pravriddha vishaya-pravalah*  
*adhash ca mulany anusantatani*  
*karmanubandhini manushya-loke*

**Translation**

Its branches extend both upward and downward, nourished by the three modes of material nature, with the objects of the senses as their tender shoots. Its roots also extend downward into the human world, binding the soul through the reactions of karma.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka3"></a>

**Shloka 15:3**

*न रूपमस्येह तथोपलभ्यते नान्तो न चादिर्न च सम्प्रतिष्ठा ।*  
*अश्वत्थमेनं सुविरूढमूलमसङ्गशस्त्रेण दृढेन छित्त्वा ॥ १५:३ ॥*

*na rupam asyeha tathopalabhyate*  
*nanto na cadir na ca sampratishtha*  
*ashvattham enam su-virudha-mulam*  
*asangga-shastrena dridhena chittva*

**Translation**

The true form of this tree cannot be perceived in this world, nor can its beginning, end, or foundation be understood. Having cut down this deeply rooted Ashvattha tree with the strong weapon of detachment, one should proceed further.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka4"></a>

**Shloka 15:4**

*ततः पदं तत्परिमार्गितव्यं यस्मिन्गता न निवर्तन्ति भूयः ।*  
*तमेव चाद्यं पुरुषं प्रपद्ये यतः प्रवृत्तिः प्रसृता पुराणी ॥ १५:४ ॥*

*tatah padam tat parimargitavyam*  
*yasmin gata na nivartanti bhuyah*  
*tam eva cadyam purusham prapadye*  
*yatah pravrittih prasrita purani*

**Translation**

Thereafter, one should seek that supreme abode from which, once attained, there is no return. I surrender unto that original Supreme Person from whom this ancient creation has expanded.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka5"></a>

**Shloka 15:5**

*निर्मानमोहा जितसङ्गदोषा अध्यात्मनित्या विनिवृत्तकामाः ।*  
*द्वन्द्वैर्विमुक्ताः सुखदुःखसंज्ञैर्गच्छन्त्यमूढाः पदमव्ययं तत् ॥ १५:५ ॥*

*nirmana-moha jita-sanga-dosha*  
*adhyatma-nitya vinivritta-kamah*  
*dvandvair vimuktah sukha-duhkha-samjnaih*  
*gacchanty amudhah padam avyayam tat*

**Translation**

Those who are free from pride and delusion, who have conquered attachment, who remain established in spiritual knowledge, who are free from material desires, and who have transcended the dualities of happiness and distress attain that eternal supreme abode.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka6"></a>

**Shloka 15:6**

*न तद्भासयते सूर्यो न शशाङ्को न पावकः ।*  
*यद्गत्वा न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥ १५:६ ॥*

*na tad bhasayate suryo*  
*na shashanko na pavakah*  
*yad gatva na nivartante*  
*tad dhama paramam mama*

**Translation**

Neither the sun, nor the moon, nor fire illumines My supreme abode. Having reached it, one never returns. That is My supreme eternal dwelling.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka7"></a>

**Shloka 15:7**

*ममैवांशो जीवलोके जीवभूतः सनातनः ।*  
*मनःषष्ठानीन्द्रियाणि प्रकृतिस्थानि कर्षति ॥ १५:७ ॥*

*mamaivamsho jiva-loke*  
*jiva-bhutah sanatanah*  
*manah-shashthanindriyani*  
*prakriti-sthani karshati*

**Translation**

The eternal living beings in this world are indeed My own fragmental parts. Bound by material nature, they struggle with the six senses, including the mind.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka8"></a>

**Shloka 15:8**

*शरीरं यदवाप्नोति यच्चाप्युत्क्रामतीश्वरः ।*  
*गृहीत्वैतानि संयाति वायुर्गन्धानिवाशयात् ॥ १५:८ ॥*

*shariram yad avapnoti*  
*yac capy utkramatishvarah*  
*grihitvaitani samyati*  
*vayur gandhan ivashayat*

**Translation**

When the embodied soul accepts a new body or leaves the old one, it carries the mind and senses along, just as the wind carries fragrances from their source.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka9"></a>

**Shloka 15:9**

*श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनं च रसनं घ्राणमेव च ।*  
*अधिष्ठाय मनश्चायं विषयानुपसेवते ॥ १५:९ ॥*

*shrotram cakshuh sparshanam ca*  
*rasanam ghranam eva ca*  
*adhishthaya manash cayam*  
*vishayan upasevate*

**Translation**

Residing in the ears, eyes, skin, tongue, nose, and mind, the embodied soul experiences and enjoys the objects of the senses.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka10"></a>

**Shloka 15:10**

*उत्क्रामन्तं स्थितं वापि भुञ्जानं वा गुणान्वितम् ।*  
*विमूढा नानुपश्यन्ति पश्यन्ति ज्ञानचक्षुषः ॥ १५:१० ॥*

*utkramantam sthitam vapi*  
*bhunjanam va gunanvitam*  
*vimudha nanupashyanti*  
*pashyanti jnana-cakshushah*

**Translation**

The ignorant cannot perceive the soul as it departs the body, resides within it, or experiences the objects of the senses under the influence of the material modes. But those who possess the eyes of wisdom can clearly perceive it.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka11"></a>

**Shloka 15:11**

*यतन्तो योगिनश्चैनं पश्यन्त्यात्मन्यवस्थितम् ।*  
*यतन्तोऽप्यकृतात्मानो नैनं पश्यन्त्यचेतसः ॥ १५:११ ॥*

*yatanto yoginash cainam*  
*pashyanty atmany avasthitam*  
*yatanto 'py akritatmano*  
*nainam pashyanty acetasah*

**Translation**

The striving yogis perceive the soul established within themselves. But those whose minds are impure and undisciplined, though they strive, cannot perceive it because they lack true understanding.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka12"></a>

**Shloka 15:12**

*यदादित्यगतं तेजो जगद्भासयतेऽखिलम् ।*  
*यच्चन्द्रमसि यच्चाग्नौ तत्तेजो विद्धि मामकम् ॥ १५:१२ ॥*

*yad aditya-gatam tejo*  
*jagad bhasayate 'khilam*  
*yac candramasi yac cagnau*  
*tat tejo viddhi mamakam*

**Translation**

The brilliance that resides in the sun and illuminates the entire world, the radiance of the moon, and the fire—all these should be understood as manifestations of My divine splendor.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka13"></a>

**Shloka 15:13**

*गामाविश्य च भूतानि धारयाम्यहमोजसा ।*  
*पुष्णामि चौषधीः सर्वाः सोमो भूत्वा रसात्मकः ॥ १५:१३ ॥*

*gam avishya ca bhutani*  
*dharayamy aham ojasā*  
*pushnami caushadhih sarvah*  
*somo bhutva rasatmakah*

**Translation**

Entering the earth, I sustain all living beings through My energy. Becoming the nourishing moon, I provide the vital essence by which all plants grow.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka14"></a>

**Shloka 15:14**

*अहं वैश्वानरो भूत्वा प्राणिनां देहमाश्रितः ।*  
*प्राणापानसमायुक्तः पचाम्यन्नं चतुर्विधम् ॥ १५:१४ ॥*

*aham vaishvanaro bhutva*  
*praninam deham ashritah*  
*pranapana-samayuktah*  
*pacamy annam catur-vidham*

**Translation**

Becoming the digestive fire within the bodies of all living beings and united with the incoming and outgoing breaths, I digest the four kinds of food.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka15"></a>

**Shloka 15:15**

*सर्वस्य चाहं हृदि सन्निविष्टो मत्तः स्मृतिर्ज्ञानमपोहनं च ।*  
*वेदैश्च सर्वैरहमेव वेद्यो वेदान्तकृद्वेदविदेव चाहम् ॥ १५:१५ ॥*

*sarvasya caham hridi sannivishto*  
*mattah smritir jnanam apohanam ca*  
*vedaish ca sarvair aham eva vedyo*  
*vedanta-krid veda-vid eva caham*

**Translation**

I dwell in the hearts of all beings. From Me arise memory, knowledge, and forgetfulness. By all the Vedas, I alone am to be known. Indeed, I am the author of Vedanta and the knower of the Vedas.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka16"></a>

**Shloka 15:16**

*द्वाविमौ पुरुषौ लोके क्षरश्चाक्षर एव च ।*  
*क्षरः सर्वाणि भूतानि कूटस्थोऽक्षर उच्यते ॥ १५:१६ ॥*

*dvav imau purushau loke*  
*ksharash cakshara eva ca*  
*ksharah sarvani bhutani*  
*kutastho 'kshara ucyate*

**Translation**

There are two kinds of beings in this world: the perishable and the imperishable. All embodied beings are perishable, while the unchanging spiritual self is called imperishable.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka17"></a>

**Shloka 15:17**

*उत्तमः पुरुषस्त्वन्यः परमात्मेत्युदाहृतः ।*  
*यो लोकत्रयमाविश्य बिभर्त्यव्यय ईश्वरः ॥ १५:१७ ॥*

*uttamah purushas tv anyah*  
*paramatmety udahritah*  
*yo loka-trayam avishya*  
*bibharty avyaya ishvarah*

**Translation**

But beyond both is the Supreme Person, known as the Paramatma, the eternal Lord who enters the three worlds and sustains them all.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka18"></a>

**Shloka 15:18**

*यस्मात्क्षरमतीतोऽहमक्षरादपि चोत्तमः ।*  
*अतोऽस्मि लोके वेदे च प्रथितः पुरुषोत्तमः ॥ १५:१८ ॥*

*yasmat ksharam atito 'ham*  
*aksharad api cottamah*  
*ato 'smi loke vede ca*  
*prathitah purushottamah*

**Translation**

Because I transcend both the perishable and the imperishable, I am celebrated in the world and in the Vedas as the Supreme Person (Purushottama).

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka19"></a>

**Shloka 15:19**

*यो मामेवमसम्मूढो जानाति पुरुषोत्तमम् ।*  
*स सर्वविद्भजति मां सर्वभावेन भारत ॥ १५:१९ ॥*

*yo mam evam asammudho*  
*janati purushottamam*  
*sa sarva-vid bhajati mam*  
*sarva-bhavena bharata*

**Translation**

Whoever knows Me as the Supreme Person without delusion is the knower of all wisdom. Such a person worships Me with complete devotion and with all his heart, O Bharata.

**Conversation**

---

<a id="shloka20"></a>

**Shloka 15:20**

*इति गुह्यतमं शास्त्रमिदमुक्तं मयानघ ।*  
*एतद्बुद्ध्वा बुद्धिमान्स्यात्कृतकृत्यश्च भारत ॥ १५:२० ॥*

*iti guhyatamam shastram*  
*idam uktam mayanagha*  
*etad buddhva buddhiman syat*  
*krita-krityash ca bharata*

**Translation**

Thus, O sinless Bharata, I have revealed this most confidential teaching. By understanding it, a person becomes truly wise and fulfills the highest purpose of life.

**Conversation**
