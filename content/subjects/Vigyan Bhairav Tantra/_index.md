---
title: "Vigyan Bhairav Tantra"
---

The Vigyan Bhairav Tantra is presented here in its available verses — 163 verses containing 112 meditation techniques, structured as a dialogue in which Devi (Parvati) asks Bhagwan Shankar (as Bhairava) who he truly is, and Shankar answers not through definition but through these techniques for realising the true nature of consciousness.

Interpretations of some techniques are still pending because I have not yet gained sufficient personal experience to interpret them. As my experience and understanding grow, I will continue adding interpretations until all verses are covered.

Numbering of verses is not my own — it comes from available sources. I do not agree with or support every verse as absolute reality; some portions may have been shaped by later transmission and commentary across centuries. So I am only giving the interpretation and meaning of the verses, not writing a new Vigyan Bhairav Tantra.
