Selected Upanishads are available here, along with my interpretations and reflections.

The Upanishads contain profound philosophical questions and insights from ancient sages of the Sanatan tradition.

I do not necessarily agree with or endorse every idea presented in them. However, I offer my 100% independent interpretation of each verse, based on my own reasoning, understanding, and experience.
