## Verse 1

विश्वामित्रवचः श्रुत्वा हर्षितो मुनिपुङ्गवः ।

शतानन्दो महातेजा रामं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥

Hearing the words of Vishvamitra, the great sage Shatananda, greatly radiant and filled with joy, spoke to Rama.

## Verse 2

अहो ते कथितं सर्वं यथावृत्तं महात्मना ।

विश्वामित्रेण काकुत्स्थ वसिष्ठस्य च धीमतः ॥

“O Kakutstha, the great-souled Vishvamitra has narrated everything exactly as it happened, concerning himself and the wise Vasistha.”

## Verse 3

राजर्षिरभवद्राजा विश्वामित्रो महाबलः ।

तस्याश्रमं समागम्य वसिष्ठो मुनिसत्तमः ॥

“Vishvamitra was a royal sage and a mighty king. The excellent sage Vasistha came to his hermitage.”

## Verse 4

तौ तत्र समवेतौ तु मुनिराजर्षिसत्तमौ ।

परस्परमभिवाद्याथ कुशलं पर्यपृच्छताम् ॥

The two excellent sages, the great royal sage and the great sage, met there. They respectfully greeted each other and enquired about each other's welfare.

## Verse 5

अथ राजा महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिम् ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं वसिष्ठं मुनिपुङ्गवम् ॥

Then the greatly radiant King Vishvamitra spoke sweet words to Vasistha, the foremost of sages.

## Verse 6

स्वागतं ते मुनिश्रेष्ठ कच्चित्ते कुशलं वने ।

तपस्यसि महाभाग कुशलं चाश्रमे तव ॥

“O foremost of sages, welcome to you. I hope you are well in the forest. O greatly fortunate one, I hope your austerities are progressing well and everything is well in your hermitage.”

## Verse 7

वसिष्ठस्तु तदा राजन् प्रत्युवाच महातपाः ।

कुशलं ते महाबाहो कुशलं चाश्रमे मम ॥

Then Vasistha, the great ascetic, replied to the king, “O mighty-armed one, I am well, and everything is well in my hermitage.”

## Verse 8

ततः परमसत्कारं कृतवान् मुनिसत्तमः ।

विश्वामित्राय धर्मात्मा वसिष्ठो मुनिपुङ्गवः ॥

Then the righteous Vasistha, the foremost of sages, offered the greatest hospitality to Vishvamitra, the excellent sage.

## Verse 9

अर्घ्यं पाद्यं च गां चैव मधुपर्कं तथैव च ।

निवेदयामास विधिवद्विश्वामित्राय धीमते ॥

He duly offered the wise Vishvamitra arghya, water for washing the feet, a cow and madhuparka.

## Verse 10

प्रतिगृह्य तु तां पूजां विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

कृतार्थोऽभून्महातेजा वसिष्ठेन महात्मना ॥

Having accepted that honour, the great and greatly radiant sage Vishvamitra felt fulfilled by the hospitality of the great-souled Vasistha.

## Verse 11

ततः कुशलमव्यग्रं वसिष्ठो मुनिपुङ्गवः ।

पप्रच्छ मधुरं वाक्यं विश्वामित्रं महामुनिम् ॥

Then Vasistha, the foremost of sages, calmly enquired about the welfare of the great sage Vishvamitra in sweet words.

## Verse 12

कच्चित्ते कुशलं राजन् राज्ये च बले च ते ।

मित्रेषु च सुहृच्चैव कच्चित्सुखमवाप्स्यसि ॥

“O king, I hope you are well, and that your kingdom and army are also well. I hope your friends and well-wishers are well and that you are living happily.”

## Verse 13

कच्चित्ते कुशलं राम सर्वत्र च नरेश्वर ।

कच्चिद्भृत्याः प्रसन्नास्ते कच्चित्तेऽस्ति धनागमः ॥

“O Rama, I hope everything is well with you everywhere, O lord of men. I hope your servants are content and that wealth is coming to you.”

## Verse 14

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं वसिष्ठं मुनिसत्तमम् ॥

Hearing those words, the great sage Vishvamitra spoke sweetly to the excellent sage Vasistha.

## Verse 15

कुशलं मे महाभाग सर्वत्र कुशलं मम ।

राज्यं च कुशलं सर्वं बलं च कुशलं तथा ॥

“O greatly fortunate one, I am well, and everything is well with me. My kingdom is prosperous and my army too is in good condition.”

## Verse 16

मित्राणि च सुहृच्चैव भृत्याश्च कुशलाः सदा ।

धनागमश्च मे ब्रह्मन् कुशलं च यथातथम् ॥

“My friends and well-wishers are well, and my servants are also always well. O Brahman, wealth is coming to me, and everything is as it should be.”

## Verse 17

एवमुक्त्वा महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

वसिष्ठं मुनिशार्दूलं पुनरेवाभ्यभाषत ॥

Having spoken thus, the greatly radiant great sage Vishvamitra again addressed Vasistha, the tiger among sages.

## Verse 18

इमौ कुमारौ भद्रं ते देवतुल्यपराक्रमौ ।

रामो दशरथस्यायं लक्ष्मणश्चापि वीर्यवान् ॥

“These two princes possess prowess equal to that of the gods. May you be blessed. This is Rama, the son of Dasharatha, and this is the mighty Lakshmana.”

## Verse 19

इमौ मुनिवरश्रेष्ठं मां शुश्रूषापरायणौ ।

आगतौ सहिता वीरौ यज्ञस्य परिरक्षणे ॥

“These two heroic princes have come with me, devoted to serving me and protecting my sacrifice.”

## Verse 20

अहं वेद्मि महात्मानौ रामलक्ष्मणयोर्बलम् ।

न वेत्ति हि भवान् सर्वं तयोर्वीर्यं महामुने ॥

“O great sage, I know the strength of these two great-souled princes, Rama and Lakshmana. You do not yet know the full extent of their valour.”

## Verse 21

ततः शतानन्दवचः श्रुत्वा रामो महायशाः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं विश्वामित्रं महामुनिम् ॥

Then Rama, greatly renowned, hearing the words of Shatananda, spoke sweetly to the great sage Vishvamitra.

## Verse 22

भगवन् कौशिकश्रेष्ठ वसिष्ठस्य महात्मनः ।

चरितं श्रोतुमिच्छामि विश्वामित्रस्य धीमतः ॥

“O Bhagavan, O foremost among the descendants of Kusha, I wish to hear the history of the wise Vishvamitra and the great-souled Vasistha.”

## Verse 23

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं रामं रघुकुलोद्वहम् ॥

Hearing those words, the great sage Vishvamitra spoke sweetly to Rama, the descendant of the Raghu dynasty.

## Verse 24

महर्षेस्तस्य काकुत्स्थ कौशिकस्य महात्मनः ।

यथावृत्तं पुरावृत्तं कथयिष्यामि विस्तरात् ॥

“O Kakutstha, I shall narrate in detail the ancient history of that great sage Kaushika exactly as it happened.”

## Verse 25

कुशिकस्य च दायादो गाधिर्नाम महायशाः ।

गाधेः पुत्रो महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ॥

“Gadhi, greatly renowned, was the descendant of Kushika. The greatly radiant Vishvamitra, the great sage, was the son of Gadhi.”

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे त्रिपञ्चाशः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the fifty-third Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
