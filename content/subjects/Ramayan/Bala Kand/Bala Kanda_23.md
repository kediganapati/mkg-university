## Verse 1

प्रभातायां तु शर्वर्यां विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।
अभ्यभाषत काकुत्स्थौ शयानौ पर्णसंस्तरे ॥

When the night had passed and dawn appeared, the great sage Vishvamitra spoke to the two descendants of Kakutstha, Rama and Lakshmana, who were sleeping on a bed of leaves.

## Verse 2

कौसल्या सुप्रजा राम पूर्वा सन्ध्या प्रवर्तते ।
उत्तिष्ठ नरशार्दूल कर्तव्यं दैवमाह्निकम् ॥

"O Rama, Kausalya is blessed to have you as her son. The eastern dawn is appearing. Arise, O tiger among men; the morning duties to the gods are to be performed."

## Verse 3

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा ऋषेर्वचनमुत्तमम् ।
स्नात्वा तौ विधिवद् वीरौ जपेतां परमं जपम् ॥

Hearing the excellent words of the sage, the two heroic princes bathed according to the prescribed rule and recited the sacred prayer.

## Verse 4

कृतोदकौ तु तौ वीरौ कृतसन्ध्यौ महात्मनौ ।
उपेत्य मुनिशार्दूलं तस्थतुश्चारुदर्शनौ ॥

Having offered water and completed their morning twilight prayers, the two great-souled and handsome heroes approached the tiger among sages and stood before him.

## Verse 5

तौ दृष्ट्वा तु महात्मानौ कुशिकस्यात्मजस्तदा ।
उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं गङ्गासरयुसङ्गमे ॥

Seeing the two great-souled princes, the son of Kushika spoke sweet words to them at the confluence of the Ganga and Sarayu.

## Verse 6

इयं सरयू नदी पुण्या गङ्गायाः सङ्गता शुभा ।
तस्यास्तीरे महाभागाः पुण्यं देशमुपाश्रिताः ॥

"This holy and auspicious river Sarayu has joined the Ganga. O greatly blessed ones, we have reached a sacred place on its bank."

## Verse 7

अत्र पूर्वं महादेवः कृत्वा क्रोधं महाबलः ।
दग्धवान् कामदेवं तु त्रैलोक्ये चरितं यशः ॥

"Here, in ancient times, the mighty Mahadeva, becoming enraged, burned Kama, the god of desire. His fame from that deed is renowned throughout the three worlds."

## Verse 8

तस्य देशस्य महिमा ब्रह्मर्षिभिरुदाहृतः ।
तं देशमाश्रिताः सर्वे पुण्यकर्माणि कुर्वते ॥

"The greatness of this place has been described by the Brahmarshis. Those who reside in this sacred region perform meritorious deeds."

## Verse 9

अत्रैव रात्रिं वत्स्यामः प्रभाते यास्यते पुनः ।
इत्युक्त्वा मुनिशार्दूलस्तत्रैवोपविवेश ह ॥

"We shall spend this night here, my children, and depart again in the morning." Having spoken thus, the tiger among sages sat down there.

## Verse 10

ततो रामो महातेजा लक्ष्मणश्च महाबलः ।
विश्वामित्रं पुरस्कृत्य तस्थतुः सहिता उभौ ॥

Then the greatly radiant Rama and the mighty Lakshmana stood together, with Vishvamitra leading them.

## Verse 11

तौ दृष्ट्वा मुनयः सर्वे हृष्टाः परममानसाः ।
अर्घ्यपाद्यादिभिस्तत्र पूजयामासुरादरात् ॥

Seeing them, all the sages became exceedingly pleased and respectfully honored them with offerings of water for the feet and other customary hospitality.

## Verse 12

विश्वामित्रं पुरस्कृत्य रामलक्ष्मणयोस्तदा ।
सर्वे ते मुनयः पुण्याः समागम्याभ्यपूजयन् ॥

The holy sages gathered together and, with Vishvamitra foremost, respectfully honored Rama and Lakshmana.

## Verse 13

ततो रामः स लक्ष्मणः कौशिकेन महात्मना ।
सह तत्र निवासं तु सुखमासादयत् प्रभुः ॥

Then Rama, together with Lakshmana, comfortably stayed there with the great-souled Kaushika.

## Verse 14

तत्राश्रमपदे पुण्ये बहवो मुनिसत्तमाः ।
न्यवसन् नियताहारा जपहोमपरायणाः ॥

Many excellent sages lived in that sacred hermitage, controlled in their food and devoted to recitation and sacrificial offerings.

## Verse 15

तेषां मध्ये महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।
रराज राजसिंहस्य पुत्राभ्यामिव पर्वतः ॥

Among them, the greatly radiant sage Vishvamitra shone together with the two sons of the lion among kings, like a mountain surrounded by peaks.

## Verse 16

ततो मुनिगणाः सर्वे समागम्य रघूत्तमम् ।
रामं च लक्ष्मणं चैव पूजयामासुरादरात् ॥

Then all the groups of sages gathered and respectfully honored Rama, the foremost of the Raghus, and Lakshmana.

## Verse 17

तत्र रामस्य महतः पुण्यस्य च महात्मनः ।
कथां चक्रुर्मुनिगणाः पुराणां धर्मसंहिताम् ॥

There the groups of sages spoke to Rama about ancient accounts connected with righteousness and about the great sacred traditions of former times.

## Verse 18

तत्र रामो महातेजा लक्ष्मणेन समन्वितः ।
शुश्राव मुनिभिः प्रोक्ताः कथाः परममङ्गलाः ॥

There, the greatly radiant Rama, accompanied by Lakshmana, listened to the highly auspicious stories narrated by the sages.

## Verse 19

ततः सन्ध्यामुपासित्वा मुनयः संशितव्रताः ।
समागम्य च ते सर्वे रामलक्ष्मणमन्तिके ॥

Then the sages, firm in their vows, performed the evening twilight prayers and gathered together near Rama and Lakshmana.

## Verse 20

रामलक्ष्मणयोः पश्चादकुर्वन्नतिथिक्रियाम् ।
सत्कारं समनुप्राप्य कथाभिरभिरञ्जयन् ॥

Afterwards they performed the rites of hospitality for Rama and Lakshmana and, having honored them, delighted them with various stories.

## Verse 21

यथार्हमजपन् सन्ध्यामृषयस्ते समाहिताः ।
तत्र वासिभिरानीता मुनिभिः सुव्रतैः सह ॥

Those sages, with concentrated minds, performed their evening prayers according to the prescribed manner. Then, together with the sages of excellent vows who lived there, they remained in that hermitage.

## Verse 22

न्यवसन् सुसुखं तत्र कामाश्रमपदे तथा ।
कथाभिरभिरामाभिरभिरामौ नृपात्मजौ ।
रमयामास धर्मात्मा कौशिको मुनिपुङ्गवः ॥

They stayed there very comfortably in the sacred precincts of the Kama hermitage. The righteous-souled Kaushika, the foremost of sages, delighted the two charming princes, the sons of the king, with delightful stories.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे त्रयोविंशः सर्गः ॥

Thus ends the twenty-third Sarga of Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the first epic composed by Sage Valmiki.
