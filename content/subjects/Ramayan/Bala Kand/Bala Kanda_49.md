## Verse 1

अफलस्तु ततश्शक्रो देवानग्निपुरोगमान् ।

अब्रवीत्त्रस्तनयनः सिद्धगन्धर्वचारणान् ॥

Then Indra, deprived of his testicles and with frightened eyes, spoke to the gods, Siddhas, Gandharvas and Charanas, with Agni at their head.

## Verse 2

कुर्वता तपसो विघ्नं गौतमस्य महात्मनः ।

क्रोधमुत्पाद्य हि मया सुरकार्यमिदं कृतम् ॥

“By creating an obstacle to the austerities of the great-souled Gautama and arousing his anger, I have indeed accomplished this task for the gods.”

## Verse 3

न च मेऽत्र विचारोऽस्ति यदिदं तपसः फलम् ।

गौतमस्य प्रभावेण नष्टं मे बलमुत्तमम् ॥

“I have no doubt that this has happened as the result of Gautama's austerities. Through the power of Gautama, my great strength has been destroyed.”

## Verse 4

तन्मां सुरवराः सर्वे सर्षिसङ्घाः सचारणाः ।

सुरकार्यकरं यूयं सफलं कर्तुमर्हथ ॥

“Therefore, O best of gods, together with the assemblies of sages and Charanas, you should make me whole again, since I have acted for the benefit of the gods.”

## Verse 5

शतक्रतोर्वचः श्रुत्वा देवा अग्निपुरोगमाः ।

पितृदेवानुपेत्याहुः सह सर्वैर्मरुद्गणैः ॥

Hearing the words of Shatakratu, the gods, with Agni at their head, together with all the Maruts, approached the ancestral gods and spoke to them.

## Verse 6

अयं शक्रो महाभागो गौतमेन महात्मना ।

शप्तो विनष्टवृषणः कथं शक्रो भविष्यति ॥

“This greatly fortunate Shakra has been cursed by the great-souled Gautama and has lost his virility. How shall Shakra become whole again?”

## Verse 7

तेषां तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा पितृदेवा महात्मनः ।

ऊचुस्तान् देवसङ्घांश्च सह सर्वैर्मरुद्गणैः ॥

Hearing their words, the great ancestral gods spoke to the hosts of gods together with all the Maruts.

## Verse 8

अजा मेषस्य वृषणावुत्पाट्य सुरसत्तमाः ।

शक्रस्य वृषणौ योज्यं तेन शक्रो भविष्यति ॥

“O best of gods, take the testicles of a ram and attach them to Shakra. In this way Shakra shall become whole again.”

## Verse 9

ततः शक्रस्य वृषणौ मेषस्योत्पाट्य चोभयौ ।

योजयामासतुर्देवाः शक्रस्य परमात्मनः ॥

Then the gods removed the two testicles of the ram and attached them to Shakra, the great-souled Lord of the gods.

## Verse 10

स तु शक्रो महातेजा वृषणाभ्यां समन्वितः ।

पुनः स्वं रूपमास्थाय देवानामग्रतोऽभवत् ॥

Then the greatly radiant Shakra, having been endowed with testicles, regained his former form and appeared before the gods.

## Verse 11

ततः सर्वे सुरगणाः शक्रमभ्यर्च्य सादरम् ।

तुष्टुवुर्मुनयश्चैव सिद्धाश्च परमर्षयः ॥

Then all the hosts of gods respectfully worshipped Shakra, and the sages, Siddhas and supreme Rishis praised him.

## Verse 12

अथ शक्रो महातेजा गौतमं समुपागमत् ।

प्रसादयितुमारेभे तपसा दग्धकिल्बिषम् ॥

Then the greatly radiant Shakra approached Gautama and began to seek his forgiveness, for Gautama had burned away his sins through austerity.

## Verse 13

तं दृष्ट्वा देवसङ्घाश्च ऋषयश्च तपोधनाः ।

सर्वे समेत्य मुनिना गौतमेन महात्मना ॥

Seeing him, the hosts of gods and the sages whose wealth was austerity assembled together with the great-souled sage Gautama.

## Verse 14

ऊचुस्तं मुनिशार्दूलं प्रसादं कुरु शक्रतः ।

देवकार्यं कृतं तेन त्वया चापि महात्मना ॥

They said to that tiger among sages, “Be gracious toward Shakra. He has performed a task for the gods, and you too are a great-souled one.”

## Verse 15

ततः प्रसन्नो भगवान् गौतमो मुनिसत्तमः ।

शक्रमामन्त्र्य धर्मात्मा पुनरेवाभ्यभाषत ॥

Then the venerable and excellent sage Gautama became pleased. The righteous sage addressed Shakra once again.

## Verse 16

अहल्यां शापमोक्षार्थं रामस्यागमनं प्रति ।

उवाच परमं वाक्यं गौतमो मुनिसत्तमः ॥

Gautama, the excellent sage, spoke of the coming of Rama, by which Ahalya would be released from her curse.

## Verse 17

यदा रामो महातेजा आश्रमं ते गमिष्यति ।

तदा त्वं शापनिर्मुक्ता भविष्यसि न संशयः ॥

“When the greatly radiant Rama comes to your hermitage, then you shall certainly be freed from the curse. There is no doubt about this.”

## Verse 18

राघवौ तु ततस्तस्याः पादौ जगृहतुस्तदा ।

स्मरन्ती गौतमवचः प्रतिजग्राह सा च तौ ॥

Then Rama and Lakshmana grasped her feet. Remembering Gautama's words, she received them as guests.

## Verse 19

पाद्यमर्घ्यं तथातिथ्यं चकार सुसमाहिता ।

प्रतिजग्राह काकुत्स्थो विधिदृष्टेन कर्मणा ॥

She offered them water for washing their feet, the customary offering and hospitality. The Kakutstha Rama accepted them according to the prescribed rites.

## Verse 20

पुष्पवृष्टिर्महत्यासीद्देवदुन्दुभिनिःस्वनैः ।

गन्धर्वाप्सरसां चैव महानासीत् समागमः ॥

There was a great shower of flowers, accompanied by the sound of divine drums. The Gandharvas and Apsaras also assembled in great numbers.

## Verse 21

साधु साध्विति देवास्तामहल्यां समपूजयन् ।

तपोबलविशुद्धाङ्गीं गौतमस्य वशानुगाम् ॥

The gods praised Ahalya, saying, “Well done! Well done!” Her limbs had been purified by the power of austerity, and she had returned to Gautama's way of life.

## Verse 22

गौतमोऽपि महातेजा अहल्यासहितः सुखी ।

रामं सम्पूज्य विधिवत् तपस्तेपे महातपाः ॥

The greatly radiant Gautama, reunited with Ahalya and filled with happiness, duly honoured Rama and continued his great austerities.

## Verse 23

रामोऽपि परमां पूजां गौतमस्य महामुनेः ।

सकाशाद् विधिवत् प्राप्य जगाम मिथिलां ततः ॥

Rama too, having duly received the highest honour from the great sage Gautama, then proceeded to Mithila.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे एकोनपञ्चाशः सर्गः ॥

Thus ends the forty-ninth Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
