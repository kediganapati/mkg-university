## Verse 1

विश्वामित्रवचः श्रुत्वा राघवः सह लक्ष्मणः ।

उवाच परमप्रीतो विश्वामित्रं महामुनिम् ॥

Hearing the words of Vishvamitra, Rama, together with Lakshmana, became exceedingly pleased and spoke to the great sage Vishvamitra.

## Verse 2

भगवन् श्रोतुमिच्छामि देवासुरसमागमम् ।

यथा वृत्तं पुरा युद्धं तन्मे कथय सुव्रत ॥

“O Bhagavan, I wish to hear about the encounter between the gods and the Asuras. O righteous one, tell me how that ancient battle took place.”

## Verse 3

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं रामं रघुकुलोद्वहम् ॥

Hearing those words, the great sage Vishvamitra spoke sweetly to Rama, the descendant of the Raghu dynasty.

## Verse 4

पूर्वं कृतयुगे राम देवासुरमहारणम् ।

बभूव परमं घोरं त्रैलोक्यभयवर्धनम् ॥

“O Rama, in the ancient Krita Yuga there was a terrible and great battle between the gods and the Asuras, causing fear throughout the three worlds.”

## Verse 5

तस्मिन् युद्धे महाघोरे देवासुरसमागमे ।

दितेः पुत्रा महावीर्या अदितेः पुत्रकैः सह ॥

In that dreadful battle between the gods and Asuras, the mighty sons of Diti fought against the sons of Aditi.

## Verse 6

युद्धं परमसंरब्धं बभूव सुमहाबलम् ।

तत्र देवाः सुरश्रेष्ठा असुरांश्च महाबलान् ॥

A fierce and immensely powerful battle took place there between the foremost gods and the mighty Asuras.

## Verse 7

जघ्नुः परस्परं सर्वे देवासुरगणास्तदा ।

ततः परमसंहृष्टा देवाः सर्वे महाबलाः ॥

At that time, all the hosts of gods and Asuras struck one another down. Then all the mighty gods became greatly delighted.

## Verse 8

असुराणां तु वीराणां युद्धे ह्यपराजिताः ।

देवा विजित्य तान् सर्वान् त्रिदिवं समुपागताः ॥

Having defeated the heroic Asuras in battle, the gods, who had been victorious, returned to heaven.

## Verse 9

ततो देवगणाः सर्वे ब्रह्माणमुपचक्रमुः ।

विजयं कथयन्तस्ते युद्धे दानवसंयुगे ॥

Then all the hosts of gods approached Brahma and narrated their victory in the battle against the Danavas.

## Verse 10

ब्रह्मा तु तान् महाभागान् देवांश्चैवाभ्यनन्दत ।

उवाच परमं वाक्यं लोकानां हितकाम्यया ॥

Brahma praised those greatly blessed gods and spoke these important words for the welfare of the worlds.

## Verse 11

धर्मेण जयता युद्धं नाधर्मेण कदाचन ।

धर्मो हि परमं बलं देवानां च सनातनम् ॥

“Win battles through righteousness, never through unrighteousness. Righteousness is indeed the supreme and eternal strength of the gods.”

## Verse 12

एवमुक्तास्तु देवास्ते ब्रह्मणा परमात्मना ।

प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं त्रिदिवं पुनरागमन् ॥

Thus addressed by Brahma, the Supreme Being, the gods bowed their heads before him and returned again to heaven.

## Verse 13

ततः कालेन महता दितिः पुत्रार्थिनी शुभा ।

कश्यपं मुनिशार्दूलं तपसा पर्यतोषयत् ॥

After a long time, the auspicious Diti, desiring a son, pleased the tiger among sages, Kashyapa, through austerities.

## Verse 14

तस्याः तुष्टो महातेजा मुनिः कश्यपसत्तमः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं दितिं पुत्रार्थिनीं तदा ॥

The greatly radiant and excellent sage Kashyapa became pleased with her and spoke sweet words to Diti, who desired a son.

## Verse 15

वरं वृणीष्व भद्रं ते यत्ते मनसि वर्तते ।

ददामि ते महाभागे तपसा तोषितो ह्यहम् ॥

“May you be blessed. Choose the boon that is in your mind. O greatly fortunate one, I shall grant it to you, for I have been pleased by your austerities.”

## Verse 16

सा तु पुत्रं महातेजा दितिर्वव्रे महाबलम् ।

देवानां चापि सर्वेषां जेतारं बलवत्तरम् ॥

Diti asked for a son of immense radiance and great strength, one who would be more powerful than all the gods and capable of defeating them.

## Verse 17

तस्याः तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा कश्यपो मुनिसत्तमः ।

उवाच परमं वाक्यं दितिं धर्मार्थसंहितम् ॥

Hearing her words, the excellent sage Kashyapa spoke to Diti words that were in accordance with righteousness and duty.

## Verse 18

शुचिर्भूत्वा महाभागे शतं वर्षाणि संयता ।

पुत्रं जनय कल्याणि देवानामपि दर्पहन् ॥

“O greatly fortunate one, remain pure and disciplined for a hundred years, and then you will give birth to a son who will humble even the pride of the gods.”

## Verse 19

इत्युक्त्वा मुनिशार्दूलः कश्यपो मुनिसत्तमः ।

तपस्यन्तीं तदा दितिं पुनरेवाभ्यभाषत ॥

Having spoken thus, the excellent sage Kashyapa again addressed Diti, who was engaged in austerities.

## Verse 20

शुचिर्भव महाभागे नियमेन समन्विता ।

न ते गर्भं विनश्येत् वै यदि सत्यं त्वमाचरेः ॥

“O greatly fortunate one, remain pure and observe the prescribed discipline. Your pregnancy will not be destroyed if you faithfully follow these rules.”

## Verse 21

सा तु तस्य वचः श्रुत्वा तथेति प्रत्युवाच ह ।

नियमं च समास्थाय तपस्तेपे सुदारुणम् ॥

Hearing his words, she replied, “So be it,” and observing the prescribed discipline, she performed severe austerities.

## Verse 22

तस्या वर्षशतं पूर्णं तपसः पर्यवर्तत ।

ततः पुत्रमसूतैषा दितिः परमदुर्जयम् ॥

When the hundred years of her austerity were complete, Diti gave birth to a son who was extremely difficult to defeat.

## Verse 23

तं तु पुत्रं महातेजा दितिः परमदुर्जयम् ।

जज्ञे परमसंहृष्टा कश्यपस्य महात्मनः ॥

Diti became exceedingly delighted upon giving birth to that greatly radiant and extremely powerful son of the great-souled Kashyapa.

## Verse 24

ततः पुत्रस्य जन्मान्ते देवाः सर्वे भयार्दिताः ।

इन्द्रं समुपगम्याथ ऊचुः परमदुःखिताः ॥

After the birth of that son, all the gods, afflicted with fear, approached Indra and spoke to him in great distress.

## Verse 25

भगवन् देवदेवेश दितेः पुत्रो महाबलः ।

जातोऽयं नः परित्राणं त्वं कर्तुमर्हसि प्रभो ॥

“O Bhagavan, Lord of the gods, this immensely powerful son of Diti has been born. O Lord, you should protect us.”

## Verse 26

ततः शक्रो महातेजा देवैः सह समागतः ।

दितिं च पुत्रसहितां ददर्श परमाद्भुताम् ॥

Then the greatly radiant Shakra, together with the gods, approached and saw Diti with her son, a wondrous sight.

## Verse 27

स तु दृष्ट्वा महातेजा दितिं पुत्रसमन्विताम् ।

विचिन्त्य मनसा सर्वं तत्रैव समुपाविशत् ॥

Seeing Diti with her son, the greatly radiant Indra reflected upon the matter in his mind and remained there.

## Verse 28

ततो वज्रधरः शक्रो दितिं वचनमब्रवीत् ।

मातस्तव सुतो वीरः कथं नाम भविष्यति ॥

Then Indra, the wielder of the thunderbolt, spoke to Diti: “Mother, what will be the name of your heroic son?”

## Verse 29

दितिस्तु परमप्रीता पुत्रनाम चकार ह ।

मारुतो नाम मे पुत्रो भविष्यति महाबलः ॥

Diti, greatly pleased, gave her son the name Maruta and said, “My son shall be named Maruta and shall possess great strength.”

## Verse 30

ततः शक्रो महातेजा तस्य पुत्रस्य रक्षणे ।

नियुक्तो देवसंघेन दित्या चापि महात्मना ॥

Then the greatly radiant Shakra was appointed by the hosts of gods, together with the great-souled Diti, to protect that son.

## Verse 31

स तु पुत्रं महातेजा दितिं चापि महाबलाम् ।

ररक्ष देवदेवेशो वज्रहस्तः पुरंदरः ॥

The Lord of the gods, Purandara, wielder of the thunderbolt, protected the greatly radiant son and the mighty Diti.

## Verse 32

कस्यचित्त्वथ कालस्य दितिः पुत्रमथाब्रवीत् ।

शक्रं त्वं सततं पुत्र रक्षस्व नियतेन्द्रियः ॥

After some time, Diti said to her son, “My child, always protect Shakra and remain disciplined in your senses.”

## Verse 33

तस्यास्तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा पुत्रो मातुर्वचः सदा ।

शक्रस्य च हितं कृत्वा तत्रैव न्यवसत् सुखम् ॥

Hearing his mother's words, the son always acted for the welfare of Shakra and lived there happily.

## Verse 34

ततो देवगणाः सर्वे तं पुत्रं दितिनन्दनम् ।

मारुतं नाम चक्रुस्ते देवा इन्द्रसमप्रभम् ॥

Then all the hosts of gods gave that son of Diti the name Maruta, as radiant as Indra himself.

## Verse 35

ततः शक्रः स तं पुत्रं भ्रातरं समुपागमत् ।

सख्यं चकार तेनैव देवदेवः शचीपतिः ॥

Then Shakra approached that son as his brother, and the Lord of the gods, the husband of Shachi, formed a friendship with him.

## Verse 36

तेषां तु तत्र संहृष्टं बभूव परमं सुखम् ।

भ्रातृभावेन संयुक्तानां देवानां च महात्मनाम् ॥

There was great happiness among them there, as the gods became united with him in the spirit of brotherhood.

## Verse 37

ततः प्रभृति ते सर्वे मरुतो नाम विश्रुताः ।

इन्द्रस्य सहया भूत्वा त्रिषु लोकेषु विश्रुताः ॥

From then onward, they became renowned by the name Marutas. Becoming companions of Indra, they became famous throughout the three worlds.

## Verse 38

एतदाख्यानमाख्यातं मया ते रघुनन्दन ।

देवानां चासुराणां च यथावृत्तं पुरातनम् ॥

O delight of the Raghu dynasty, I have narrated to you this ancient account of the gods and Asuras exactly as it occurred.

## Verse 39

यः पठेत् शृणुयाद् वापि देवासुरसमागमम् ।

सर्वपापविनिर्मुक्तः स्वर्गलोके महीयते ॥

Whoever reads or hears this account of the encounter between the gods and Asuras becomes free from all sins and is honoured in the heavenly world.

## Verse 40

पुत्रपौत्रसमायुक्तः श्रीमान् दीर्घमायुर्भवेत् ।

सर्वान् कामानवाप्नोति यशश्चाप्नोति पुष्कलम् ॥

Such a person becomes blessed with sons and grandsons, prosperity and long life, obtains all desired things, and gains abundant fame.

## Verse 41

इति ते कथितं राम देवानामसुरैः सह ।

चरितं पुण्यदं घोरं सर्वलोकहितावहम् ॥

Thus, O Rama, I have narrated to you the sacred and terrible account of the gods together with the Asuras, which brings welfare to all the worlds.

## Verse 42

श्रुत्वा तु परमं पुण्यं देवासुरसमागमम् ।

रामः परमसंहृष्टो विश्वामित्रमथाब्रवीत् ॥

Hearing this highly sacred account of the encounter between the gods and Asuras, Rama became exceedingly delighted and spoke to Vishvamitra.

## Verse 43

भगवन् विस्तरेणैव श्रोतुमिच्छामि सुव्रत ।

कथयस्व महाभाग सर्वं मे नात्र संशयः ॥

“O Bhagavan, O righteous one, I wish to hear everything in detail. O greatly fortunate sage, tell me all of it; I have no doubt.”

## Verse 44

विश्वामित्रस्तु तच्छ्रुत्वा रामस्य वचनं शुभम् ।

उवाच परमप्रीतो राघवं रघुनन्दनम् ॥

Hearing these auspicious words of Rama, Vishvamitra became exceedingly pleased and spoke to Rama, the delight of the Raghu dynasty.

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदि काव्ये बाल काण्डे पञ्चचत्वारिंशः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the forty-fifth Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
