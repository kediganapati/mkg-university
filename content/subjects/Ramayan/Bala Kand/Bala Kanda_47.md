## Verse 1

विश्वामित्रवचः श्रुत्वा राघवः सह लक्ष्मणः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं मुनिं रघुकुलोद्वहम् ॥

Hearing the words of Vishvamitra, Rama, together with Lakshmana, spoke these sweet words to the sage, the descendant of the Raghu dynasty.

## Verse 2

भगवन् श्रोतुमिच्छामि कथां परमपावनीम् ।

ययातेर्नहुषस्यैव चरितं मे वद प्रभो ॥

“O Bhagavan, I wish to hear the most sacred story. O Lord, tell me the story of Yayati and Nahusha.”

## Verse 3

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं रामं रघुकुलोद्वहम् ॥

Hearing those words, the great sage Vishvamitra spoke sweetly to Rama, the descendant of the Raghu dynasty.

## Verse 4

पुरा कृतयुगे राम ययातिर्नाम पार्थिवः ।

आसीत् परमधर्मात्मा सत्यसन्धो जितेन्द्रियः ॥

“O Rama, in the ancient Krita Yuga there was a king named Yayati. He was exceedingly righteous, truthful in his resolve, and master of his senses.

## Verse 5

स राजा राजसिंहश्च प्रजाः सम्यगनुपालयन् ।

यज्ञैरिष्ट्वा महाभागो देवांश्चाप्यतितोषयत् ॥

That lion among kings properly protected his subjects. The greatly fortunate king performed sacrifices and greatly pleased the gods.

## Verse 6

तस्य तुष्टो महातेजा देवराजः शतक्रतुः ।

वरं प्रादाद् महाराज ययातेरमितौजसः ॥

The greatly radiant king of the gods, Shatakratu, became pleased with the immensely powerful King Yayati and granted him a boon.

## Verse 7

स राजा परमप्रीतो देवराजमथाब्रवीत् ।

स्वर्गलोकं ममाप्यस्तु देवदेव नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

The king, greatly pleased, said to the Lord of the gods, “O Lord of the gods, may heaven also become mine. My salutations to you.”

## Verse 8

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा देवराजः पुरंदरः ।

उवाच परमं वाक्यं ययातिं नृपसत्तमम् ॥

Hearing those words, Purandara, the king of the gods, spoke these words to Yayati, the excellent king.

## Verse 9

स्वर्गलोकं गमिष्यसि पुण्येन स्वेन कर्मणा ।

इत्युक्त्वा तं नृपश्रेष्ठं देवराजो जगाम ह ॥

“You shall attain heaven through your own meritorious deeds.” Having spoken thus to that excellent king, the Lord of the gods departed.

## Verse 10

ततो ययातिः स राजर्षिर्देवलोकमुपागमत् ।

तत्र देवगणैः सार्धं मुमुदे नृपसत्तमः ॥

Then that royal sage Yayati went to the world of the gods. There the excellent king rejoiced together with the hosts of gods.

## Verse 11

तस्य तत्र महाभागस्य कालेन महता ततः ।

क्षीणे पुण्ये महातेजाः पुनर्मर्त्यं जगाम ह ॥

After a long time, when the merit of that greatly fortunate and radiant king had been exhausted, he returned again to the mortal world.

## Verse 12

स च राजा महातेजाः स्वर्गलोकात् परिभ्रष्टः ।

पुनराप्याययामास तपसा स्वेन तेजसा ॥

That greatly radiant king, having fallen from heaven, again restored his spiritual power through his own austerities.

## Verse 13

ततः स पुनरागत्य पृथिवीं पृथिवीपतिः ।

प्रजाः सम्यगुपस्थाय यज्ञैश्चाप्यतितोषयत् ॥

Then the lord of the earth returned to the earth, properly cared for his subjects, and greatly pleased the gods through sacrifices.

## Verse 14

तस्य पुत्रो महातेजा नहुषो नाम वीर्यवान् ।

बभूव परमधर्मात्मा सत्यसन्धो जितेन्द्रियः ॥

He had a greatly radiant and powerful son named Nahusha, who was exceedingly righteous, truthful in his resolve, and master of his senses.

## Verse 15

नहुषस्तु महातेजा पितुराज्ञामनुव्रतः ।

धर्मेण पृथिवीं सर्वां शशास नृपसत्तमः ॥

The greatly radiant Nahusha, obedient to his father's command, ruled the entire earth righteously as an excellent king.

## Verse 16

तस्य तु पृथिवीं सर्वां प्रशासतो महात्मनः ।

बभूव परमं तेजो देवानामपि दर्पहा ॥

As that great-souled king ruled the entire earth, his extraordinary power became capable even of humbling the pride of the gods.

## Verse 17

ततः स देवैः सहितो नहुषः परमद्युतिः ।

इन्द्रत्वं समुपागम्य त्रिदिवे समपूज्यत ॥

Then the greatly radiant Nahusha, together with the gods, attained the position of Indra and was honoured in heaven.

## Verse 18

स तु देवैः समागम्य स्वर्गलोकमुपागतः ।

देवराजोऽभवद् राजा नहुषो नृपसत्तमः ॥

Having gone to the celestial world and associating with the gods, the excellent king Nahusha became the king of the gods.

## Verse 19

ततः स देवगन्धर्वैरप्सरोभिश्च सेवितः ।

मुमुदे नहुषो राजा देवराज्ये प्रतिष्ठितः ॥

Then King Nahusha, established in the sovereignty of the gods, enjoyed himself, attended by gods, Gandharvas and Apsaras.

## Verse 20

तस्य तत्र महातेजा नहुषस्य महात्मनः ।

अहंकारोऽभवत् तीव्रो देवानामपि दर्पहा ॥

There, the greatly radiant and great-souled Nahusha became intensely proud, even to the point of humbling the gods.

## Verse 21

स तु देवगणान् सर्वान् ऋषींश्चाप्यवमन्यत ।

तेन दोषेण महता स्वर्गाद् भ्रष्टोऽभवत् तदा ॥

He then showed disrespect to all the hosts of gods and even to the sages. Because of that great fault, he fell from heaven.

## Verse 22

ततः स देवगणैः सर्वैः शप्तोऽथ नृपसत्तमः ।

अजगरत्वमापन्नो भूमौ निपतितस्तदा ॥

Then that excellent king was cursed by all the hosts of gods and became a python, falling down upon the earth.

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदि काव्ये बाल काण्डे सप्तचत्वारिंशः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the forty-seventh Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
