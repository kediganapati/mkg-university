# Bala Kanda — Sarga 05

## Verse 1

सर्वा पूर्वम् इयम् येषाम् आसीत् कृत्स्ना वसुंधरा |
प्रजपतिम् उपादाय नृपाणम् जय शालिनाम् ||

Formerly, this entire earth belonged to those victorious kings, beginning with Prajapati.

## Verse 2

येषाम् स सगरो नाम सागरो येन खानितः |
षष्टिः पुत्र सहस्राणि यम् यान्तम् पर्यवारयन् ||

Among them was King Sagara, after whom the ocean was named, and whom sixty thousand sons accompanied when he set out.

## Verse 3

इक्ष्वाकूणाम् इदम् तेषाम् राज्ञाम् वंशे महात्मनाम् |
महद् उत्पन्नम् आख्यनम् रामायणम् इति श्रुतम् ||

In the lineage of those great-souled kings of the Ikshvaku dynasty arose this great narrative, known as the Ramayana.

## Verse 4

तदिदम् वर्तयिष्यावः सर्वम् निखिलम् आदितः |
धर्म काम अर्थ सहितम् श्रोतव्यम् अनसूयता ||

We shall narrate this entire account from the beginning, containing Dharma, Kama, and Artha. It should be heard without envy or fault-finding.

## Verse 5

कोसलो नाम मुदितः स्फीतो जनपदो महान् |
निविष्ट सरयू तीरे प्रभूत धन धान्यवान् ||

There was a great and prosperous kingdom named Kosala, joyful and flourishing, situated on the banks of the Sarayu and abundant in wealth and grain.

## Verse 6

अयोध्या नाम नगरी तत्र आसीत् लोक विश्रुता |
मनुना मानव इन्द्रेण या पुरी निर्मिता स्वयम् ||

There was a world-renowned city named Ayodhya, which had been built by Manu, the foremost among human rulers.

## Verse 7

आयता दश च द्वे च योजनानि महापुरी |
श्रीमती त्रीणि विस्तीर्णा सु विभक्ता महापथा ||

That glorious great city extended twelve yojanas in length and three yojanas in breadth, with well-planned great roads.

## Verse 8

राज मार्गेण महता सुविभक्तेन शोभिता |
मुक्ता पुष्प अवकीर्णेन जल सिक्तेन नित्यशः ||

It was adorned with broad and well-laid royal roads, constantly sprinkled with water and covered with scattered flowers.

## Verse 9

ताम् तु राजा दशरथो महाराष्ट्र विवर्धनः |
पुरीम् आवासयामास दिवि देवपतिः यथा ||

King Dasharatha, who increased the greatness of his kingdom, made that city his residence just as the lord of the gods resides in heaven.

## Verse 10

कपाट तोरणवर्ती सु विभक्त अन्तरापणाम् |
सर्व यंत्र अयुधवतीम् उषिताम् सर्व शिल्पिभिः ||

It was surrounded by gates and archways, with well-arranged marketplaces, equipped with various machines and weapons, and inhabited by skilled craftsmen.

## Verse 11

सूत मागध संबाधाम् श्रीमतीम् अतुल प्रभाम् |
उच्चाट्टाल ध्वजवतीम् शतघ्नी शत संकुलाम् ||

It was filled with bards and heralds, prosperous and of incomparable splendour, with high towers, banners, and many defensive weapons.

## Verse 12

वधू नाटक सन्घैः च संयुक्ताम् सर्वतः पुरीम् |
उद्यान आम्र वणोपेताम् महतीम् साल मेखलाम् ||

The city was filled with groups of women and performers, surrounded by gardens and mango groves, and enclosed by a great protective wall.

## Verse 13

दुर्ग गंभीर परिखाम् दुर्गाम् अन्यैः दुरासदम् |
वाजीवारण संपूर्णाम् गोभिः उष्ट्रैः खरैः तथा ||

It had deep and difficult moats and was difficult for enemies to approach. It was filled with horses and elephants, as well as cows, camels, and donkeys.

## Verse 14

सामंत राज सन्घैः च बलि कर्मभिः आवृतम् |
नाना देश निवासैः च वणिग्भिः उपशोभिताम् ||

It was surrounded by groups of subordinate kings who paid tribute and was beautified by merchants and people from many different lands.

## Verse 15

प्रासादै रत्न विकृतैः पर्वतैः इव शोभिताम् |
कूटागारैः च संपूर्णाम् इन्द्रस्य इव अमरावतीम् ||

It was adorned with palaces decorated with precious jewels, resembling mountains, and filled with lofty buildings like Indra's Amaravati.

## Verse 16

चित्रम् अष्टापद आकाराम् वर नारी गणैर् युताम् |
सर्व रत्न समाकीर्णाम् विमान गृह शोभिताम् ||

It was wonderfully laid out, filled with beautiful women and abundant precious gems, and adorned with magnificent multi-storeyed buildings.

## Verse 17

गृह गाढाम् अविच्छिद्राम् सम भूमौ निवेशिताम् |
शालि तण्डुल संपूर्णाम् इक्षु काण्ड रसः उदकाम् ||

Its houses stood closely together without gaps upon level ground. It was abundant in rice and grain, and its water was sweet like the juice of sugarcane.

## Verse 18

दुन्दुभीभिः मृदन्गैः च वीणाभिः पणवैः तथा |
नादिताम् भृशम् अत्यर्थम् पृथिव्याम् ताम् अनुत्तमाम् ||

It resounded greatly with dundubhi drums, mridangas, veenas, and other musical instruments. Upon the earth, it was an incomparable city.

## Verse 19

विमानम् इव सिद्धानाम् तपस अधिगतम् दिवि |
सु निवेशित वेश्मान्ताम् नरोत्तम समावृताम् ||

It resembled the heavenly abode attained by perfected beings through austerity, with well-arranged buildings and filled with excellent people.

## Verse 20

ये च बाणैः न विध्यन्ति विविक्तम् अपरा परम् |
शब्द वेध्यम् च विततम् लघु हस्ता विशारदाः ||

Its skilled archers did not strike a solitary person, one without protection or support, or a fleeing person, and they did not shoot merely by following sound.

## Verse 21

सिंह व्याघ्र वराहाणाम् मत्तानाम् नदताम् वने |
हन्तारो निशितैः शस्त्रैः बलात् बाहु बलैर् अपि ||

They could kill powerful and roaring lions, tigers, and wild boars in the forest with sharp weapons and even through the strength of their own arms.

## Verse 22

तादृशानाम् सहस्रैः ताम् अभि पूर्णाम् महारथैः |
पुरीम् आवसयमास राजा दशरथः तदा ||

King Dasharatha dwelt in that city, which was filled with thousands of such great warriors and mighty chariot fighters.

## Verse 23

ताम् अग्निमद्भिः गुणवद्भिः आवृताम् |
द्विजोत्तमैः वेद षडङ्ग पारगैः |
सहस्रदैः सत्य रतैः महात्मभिः |
महर्षि कल्पैः ऋषिभिः च केवलैः ||

That city was surrounded by virtuous and excellent learned people, accomplished in the Vedas and their six auxiliary disciplines, generous and devoted to truth, great-souled and comparable to great sages.
