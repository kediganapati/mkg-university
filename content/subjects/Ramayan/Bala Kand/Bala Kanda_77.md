## Verse 1

ततः प्रहृष्टः सुमन्त्रस्त्वरितो राजशासनात् ।

रामं प्रस्थापयामास वनवासाय राघवम् ॥

Then Sumantra, delighted and acting swiftly according to the king's command, prepared Raghava Rama for departure to the forest.

## Verse 2

रामस्तु पितुरादेशं शिरसा प्रतिपद्य च ।

वनं गन्तुं व्यवस्याथ सीतामामन्त्रयत्तदा ॥

Rama accepted his father's command with bowed head and then resolved to go to the forest. He then spoke to Sita.

## Verse 3

सीते वनं गमिष्यामि पितुराज्ञां न लङ्घये ।

त्वमत्रैव निवर्तस्व मम दुःखं न वर्धय ॥

“O Sita, I shall go to the forest and shall not violate my father's command. You should remain here and not increase my sorrow.”

## Verse 4

सा तु रामस्य वचनं श्रुत्वा सीता पतिव्रता ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं धर्मयुक्तं पतिव्रता ॥

Hearing Rama's words, Sita, devoted to her husband, spoke sweet words consistent with righteousness.

## Verse 5

नाहं गच्छामि ते राम विना वनमिह प्रभो ।

त्वया सह गमिष्यामि वनं सत्यपराक्रम ॥

“O Rama, my lord, I shall not remain here without you. I shall go to the forest with you, O truly valiant one.”

## Verse 6

तथा लक्ष्मणमासाद्य रामो वाक्यमथाब्रवीत् ।

त्वमप्यनुचरस्वाद्य भ्रातृभावेन सुव्रत ॥

Then Rama approached Lakshmana and said: “You too should accompany me today, O righteous one, out of brotherly affection.”

## 7

लक्ष्मणस्तु तदा रामं प्रणम्य च कृताञ्जलिः ।

उवाच वचनं वीरः सर्वथा त्वामनुव्रजम् ॥

Then the heroic Lakshmana bowed to Rama with folded hands and said: “I shall follow you in every way.”

## 8

ततः सीता च रामश्च लक्ष्मणश्च महाबलः ।

त्रयस्ते रथमारुह्य वनाय प्रस्थितास्तदा ॥

Then Sita, Rama and the mighty Lakshmana mounted the chariot and departed for the forest.

## 9

ततः पुरजनाः सर्वे रामस्य प्रियकाम्यया ।

अन्वगच्छन् महात्मानं सीतां च लक्ष्मणं तथा ॥

Then all the citizens, desiring the welfare of the great-souled Rama, followed him, Sita and Lakshmana.

## 10

न तत्र कश्चिद् दुःखार्तो न च कश्चिद् हतप्रभः ।

सर्वे ते हृष्टमनसः रामं दृष्ट्वा विनिर्ययुः ॥

There was no one there who was not sorrowful, and no one whose radiance had not faded. Seeing Rama, they all followed him with troubled minds.

## 11

ततो दशरथो राजा रामं दृष्ट्वा वनं गतम् ।

दुःखार्तो विललापाथ हा रामेति पुनः पुनः ॥

Then King Dasharatha, seeing Rama depart for the forest, was overcome with grief and lamented repeatedly, saying, “O Rama! O Rama!”

## 12

कौसल्या च सुमित्रा च कैकेयी च यशस्विनी ।

रामस्य गमनं श्रुत्वा रुरुदुः शोककर्शिताः ॥

Kausalya, Sumitra and the illustrious Kaikeyi, hearing of Rama's departure, wept, afflicted with sorrow.

## 13

ततः स्त्रियः समासाद्य राजानं शोककर्शितम् ।

पर्यवारयदुः सर्वा दुःखार्ता रुदतीस्तदा ॥

Then all the women, afflicted with grief and weeping, surrounded the sorrow-stricken king.

## 14

ततः पुरजनाः सर्वे दुःखशोकसमन्विताः ।

राममेवानुगच्छन्तो न जहुस्तं नरर्षभम् ॥

Then all the citizens, filled with sorrow and grief, followed Rama and did not abandon that bull among men.

## 15

ततः सुमन्त्रः प्रययौ रथेन रघुनन्दनम् ।

सीतां रामं च लक्ष्मणं वनं प्रति ॥

Then Sumantra proceeded by chariot, carrying Rama, Sita and Lakshmana toward the forest.

## 16

ततः पन्थानमाश्रित्य दूरमध्वानमागतम् ।

रथस्तेषां तु वेगेन प्रययौ रघुनन्दन ॥

Then their chariot travelled swiftly along the road for a great distance, carrying the descendants of Raghu.

## 17

ततः पुरं समासाद्य रामो दशरथात्मजः ।

अब्रवीत् सुमन्त्रं सारथे रथं वाहय शीघ्रतः ॥

After reaching the outskirts of the city, Rama, the son of Dasharatha, said to Sumantra, the charioteer: “Drive the chariot swiftly.”

## 18

निवर्तय रथं शीघ्रं नगरं प्रति सारथे ।

जनस्यास्य च दुःखं मे निवारयितुमर्हसि ॥

“Turn the chariot back quickly toward the city, O charioteer. You should prevent these people from continuing to suffer because of me.”

## 19

ततः सुमन्त्रः संहृष्टो रामस्य वचनाद् रथम् ।

निवर्तयामास त्वरितो नगरं प्रति ॥

Then Sumantra, according to Rama's command, swiftly turned the chariot back toward the city.

## 20

ततः पुरजनाः सर्वे रथं दृष्ट्वा निवर्तितम् ।

रामं विना निराशास्ते पुनरेव गृहान् ययुः ॥

Then all the citizens, seeing the chariot return without Rama, became hopeless and returned to their homes.

## 21

ततः स रामो धर्मात्मा सीतया लक्ष्मणेन च ।

वनं प्रविश्य धर्मेण तपस्वीव समाहितः ॥

Then the righteous Rama entered the forest with Sita and Lakshmana, living there with the discipline of an ascetic.

## 22

ततः ते रघुशार्दूलाः सर्वे वनमुपागताः ।

मुनिवेषधराः सर्वे तपोवनमुपाश्रिताः ॥

Then the tigers among the Raghus, all wearing the garments of ascetics, entered the forest and took shelter in a hermitage.

## 23

ततो रामस्य धर्मात्मा भरतो नाम विश्रुतः ।

आगतो मातुलं द्रष्टुं कैकेयस्य महाबलः ॥

Then the righteous and renowned Bharata, the mighty son of Kaikeyi, came to see his maternal uncle.

## 24

तत्र तं मातुलं दृष्ट्वा भरतः प्रियकाम्यया ।

अभ्यनन्दत धर्मात्मा स्नेहाद् भ्रातरमात्मनः ॥

There Bharata, the righteous one, having seen his maternal uncle, joyfully greeted him with affection, while thinking of his brother.

## 25

ततः स मातुलं दृष्ट्वा हृष्टो भरतसत्तमः ।

उवाच वचनं वीरः कुशलं तत्र पृच्छति ॥

Then the excellent and heroic Bharata, delighted to see his maternal uncle, spoke to him and asked about his welfare.

## 26

ततः पितुर्नियोगेन कैकेय्या च यशस्विनी ।

भरतः सह शत्रुघ्नो मातुलस्य गृहेऽवसत् ॥

Then, by his father's command and at the request of the illustrious Kaikeyi, Bharata stayed at his maternal uncle's residence together with Shatrughna.

## 27

ततः काले गते तत्र रामस्य वनवासिनः ।

दशरथस्य च निधनं भरतोऽश्रौष तद् वचः ॥

After some time had passed, Bharata heard that Rama was living in the forest and that Dasharatha had died.

## 28

श्रुत्वा तु पितरं मृतं रामं च वनमाश्रितम् ।

भरतः शोकसंतप्तो विललापाकुलेन्द्रियः ॥

Hearing that his father had died and that Rama had gone to the forest, Bharata was overwhelmed with grief and lamented with his senses distressed.

## 29

हा राम हा महाबाहो हा सीते लक्ष्मण प्रभो ।

इति शोकसमाविष्टो भरतः पर्यदेवयत् ॥

“Alas, Rama! Alas, mighty-armed one! Alas, Sita! Alas, Lakshmana!” Thus overwhelmed with grief, Bharata lamented.

## 30

ततो भरतशत्रुघ्नौ रामस्य प्रियकाम्यया ।

अयोध्यां पुनरागम्य पितरं द्रष्टुमर्हतः ॥

Then Bharata and Shatrughna, desiring the welfare of Rama, returned to Ayodhya, intending to see their father.

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे सप्तसप्ततितमः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the seventy-seventh Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
