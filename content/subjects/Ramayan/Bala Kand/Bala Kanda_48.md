## Verse 1

पृष्ट्वा तु कुशलं तत्र परस्पर समागमे ।

कथान्ते सुमतिः वाक्यं व्याजहार महामुनिम् ॥

After meeting one another there and enquiring about each other's welfare, King Sumati spoke to the great sage Vishvamitra.

## Verse 2

इमौ कुमारौ भद्रं ते देव तुल्य पराक्रमौ ।

गज सिंह गती वीरौ शार्दूल वृषभ उपमौ ॥

These two young princes are heroic, possessing strength equal to that of the gods. Their movements are like elephants and lions, and they resemble tigers and bulls in valour.

## Verse 3

पद्म पत्र विशालाक्षौ खड्ग तूणी धनुर् धरौ ।

अश्विनौ इव रूपेण समुपस्थित यौवनौ ॥

Their eyes are broad like lotus petals, and they carry swords, quivers and bows. In appearance and youthful beauty, they resemble the Ashvin twins.

## Verse 4

यदृच्छया एव गां प्राप्तौ देवलोकात् इव अमरौ ।

कथं पद्भ्याम् इह प्राप्तौ किम् अर्थं कस्य वा मुने ॥

They appear like immortals who have come to earth from the celestial world. O sage, how have they come here on foot? For what purpose have they come, and whose sons are they?

## Verse 5

भूषयन्तौ इमं देशं चन्द्र सूर्यौ इवांबरम् ।

परस्परेण सदृशौ प्रमाण इङ्गित चेष्टितैः ॥

These two, alike in bodily form, gestures and movements, beautify this land just as the Moon and Sun beautify the sky.

## Verse 6

किम् अर्थं च नर श्रेष्ठौ संप्राप्तौ दुर्गमे पथि ।

वर आयुध धरौ वीरौ श्रोतुम् इच्छामि तत्त्वतः ॥

Why have these two excellent and heroic men, bearing magnificent weapons, come along this difficult path? I wish to know the truth.

## Verse 7

तस्य तद् वचनं श्रुत्वा यथा वृत्तं न्यवेदयत् ।

सिद्ध आश्रम निवासं च राक्षसानां वधं तथा ॥

Hearing those words, Vishvamitra narrated what had happened, including their stay at Siddhashrama and the destruction of the Rakshasas.

## Verse 8

विश्वामित्र वचः श्रुत्वा राजा परम विस्मितः ।

अतिथी परमौ प्राप्तौ पुत्रौ दशरथस्य तौ ॥

Hearing the words of Vishvamitra, the king was greatly astonished that the two sons of Dasharatha had arrived as distinguished guests.

## Verse 9

पूजयामास विधिवत् सत्कार अर्हौ महाबलौ ।

ततः परम सत्कारं सुमतेः प्राप्य राघवौ ॥

He properly honoured those two mighty princes, who were worthy of hospitality. Having received great honour from King Sumati, the two descendants of Raghu...

## Verse 10

उष्य तत्र निशाम् एकाम् जग्मतुः मिथिलां ततः ।

तां दृष्ट्वा मुनयः सर्वे जनकस्य पुरीं शुभाम् ॥

...spent one night there and then proceeded toward Mithila. Seeing the auspicious city of King Janaka, all the sages...

## Verse 11

साधु साधु इति शंसन्तो मिथिलां समपूजयन् ।

मिथिल उपवने तत्र आश्रमं दृश्य राघवः ॥

...praised Mithila, saying, “Excellent! Excellent!” In the woodland near Mithila, Rama saw an hermitage...

## Verse 12

पुराणं निर्जनं रम्यं पप्रच्छ मुनि पुंगवम् ।

इदम् आश्रम संकाशं किम् नु इदम् मुनि वर्जितम् ॥

...ancient, beautiful and deserted, and asked the foremost of sages: “What is this place that resembles an hermitage but has been abandoned by sages?”

## Verse 13

श्रोतुम् इच्छामि भगवन् कस्य अयं पूर्व आश्रमः ।

तत् श्रुत्वा राघवेण उक्तं वाक्यं वाक्य विशारदः ॥

“O Bhagavan, I wish to hear whose hermitage this formerly was.” Hearing Rama's words, the eloquent sage...

## Verse 14

प्रति उवाच महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि शृणु तत्त्वेन राघव ॥

...the greatly radiant great sage Vishvamitra, replied: “O Raghava, I shall tell you. Listen to the truth.”

## Verse 15

यस्य एतत् आश्रम पदं शप्तं कोपान् महात्मना ।

गौतमस्य नरश्रेष्ठ पूर्वम् आसीत् महात्मनः ॥

“O best among men, this hermitage, which was cursed in anger by a great-souled one, formerly belonged to the great sage Gautama.

## Verse 16

आश्रमो दिव्य संकाशः सुरैः अपि सुपूजितः ।

स च अत्र तप आतिष्ठत् अहल्या सहितः पुरा ॥

This hermitage was heavenly in appearance and highly revered even by the gods. Long ago, Gautama performed austerities here together with Ahalya.

## Verse 17

वर्ष पूगानि अनेकानि राजपुत्र महायशः ।

तस्य अन्तरं विदित्वा तु सहस्राक्षः शची पतिः ॥

O greatly renowned prince, they remained there for many years. Knowing the opportunity when Gautama was away, Indra, the husband of Shachi and the thousand-eyed one...

## Verse 18

मुनि वेष धरो भूत्वा अहल्याम् इदम् अब्रवीत् ।

ऋतु कालं प्रतीक्षन्ते न अर्थिनः सुसमाहिते ॥

...assuming the guise of a sage, spoke to Ahalya: “Those who seek pleasure do not wait for the proper season.

## Verse 19

संगमं तु अहम् इच्छामि त्वया सह सुमध्यमे ।

मुनि वेषं सहस्राक्षं विज्ञाय रघुनंदन ॥

“O slender-waisted one, I desire union with you.” O delight of the Raghu dynasty, Ahalya recognized the thousand-eyed Indra in the guise of the sage...

## Verse 20

मतिं चकार दुर्मेधा देवराज कुतूहलात् ।

अथ अब्रवीत् सुरश्रेष्ठं कृतार्थेन अंतरात्मना ॥

...but, overcome by curiosity about the king of the gods, she consented. Then, feeling satisfied within herself, she spoke to the foremost of the gods:

## Verse 21

कृतार्था अस्मि सुरश्रेष्ठ गच्छ शीघ्रम् इतः प्रभो ।

आत्मानं मां च देवेश सर्वदा रक्ष गौतमात् ॥

“O foremost of the gods, I am satisfied. O Lord, go quickly from here. O Lord of the gods, always protect yourself and me from Gautama.”

## Verse 22

इन्द्रः तु प्रहसन् वाक्यम् अहल्याम् इदम् अब्रवीत् ।

सुश्रोणि परितुष्टोऽस्मि गमिष्यामि यथागतम् ॥

Indra smiled and said to Ahalya, “O beautiful-hipped lady, I am completely satisfied. I shall depart by the way I came.”

## Verse 23

एवं संगम्य तु तया निश्चक्राम उटजात् ततः ।

स संभ्रमात् त्वरन् राम शङ्कितो गौतमं प्रति ॥

Having thus united with her, Indra came out of the hut. O Rama, he hurried away in confusion, fearing the arrival of Gautama.

## Verse 24

गौतमं स ददर्शाथ प्रविशन्तं महामुनिम् ।

देव दानव दुर्धर्षं तपो बल समन्वितम् ॥

He then saw the great sage Gautama entering. Gautama was formidable to gods and Danavas and endowed with the power of austerity.

## Verse 25

तीर्थ उदक परिक्लिन्नं दीप्यमानम् इवानलम् ।

गृहीत समिधं तत्र स कुशं मुनि पुंगवम् ॥

The foremost of sages was wet from the sacred waters and shone like fire, carrying sacrificial fuel and kusha grass.

## Verse 26

दृष्ट्वा सुर पतिः त्रस्तो विषण्ण वदनोऽभवत् ।

अथ दृष्ट्वा सहस्राक्षं मुनि वेष धरं मुनिः ॥

Seeing him, Indra, the lord of the gods, became frightened and his face became pale. The sage then saw the thousand-eyed Indra disguised as a sage...

## Verse 27

दुर्वृत्तं वृत्त संपन्नो रोषाद् वचनम् अब्रवीत् ।

मम रूपं समास्थाय कृतवान् असि दुर्मते ॥

Gautama, righteous in conduct, angrily spoke to the wicked Indra: “O evil-minded one, taking my form, you have committed this improper act.

## Verse 28

अकर्तव्यम् इदं यस्मात् विफलः त्वं भविष्यति ।

गौतमेन एवम् उक्तस्य स रोषेण महात्मना ॥

“Because you have done what should not be done, you shall become without virility.” Thus, when the great-souled Gautama spoke in anger...

## Verse 29

पेततुर् वृषणौ भूमौ सहस्राक्षस्य तत्क्षणात् ।

तथा शप्त्वा च वै शक्रं भार्याम् अपि च शप्तवान् ॥

...the testicles of the thousand-eyed Indra fell to the ground at that very moment. Having thus cursed Shakra, Gautama also cursed his wife.

## Verse 30

इह वर्ष सहस्राणि बहूनि निवत्स्यसि ।

वायु भक्षा निराहारा तप्यन्ती भस्म शायिनी ॥

“You shall remain here for many thousands of years, living on air and without food, performing austerities and lying upon ashes.

## Verse 31

अदृश्या सर्व भूतानाम् आश्रमेऽस्मिन् वत्स्यसि ।

यदा त्वेतद् वनं घोरं रामो दशरथात्मजः ॥

“You shall remain in this hermitage unseen by all beings. When Rama, the son of Dasharatha, comes to this dreadful forest...

## Verse 32

आगमिष्यति दुर्धर्षस् तदा पूता भविष्यसि ।

तस्य आतिथ्येन दुर्वृत्ते लोभ मोह विवर्जिता ॥

...the unassailable Rama will come, and then you shall be purified. O ill-behaved woman, by offering him hospitality and becoming free from greed and delusion...

## Verse 33

मत् सकाशे मुदा युक्ता स्वं वपुर्धारयिष्यसि ।

एवम् उक्त्वा महातेजा गौतमो दुष्ट चारिणीम् ॥

...you shall joyfully regain your own form in my presence.” Having spoken thus to the wicked Ahalya...

## Verse 34

इमम् आश्रमम् उत्सृज्य सिद्ध चारण सेविते ।

हिमवत् शिखरे रम्ये तपः तेपे महातपाः ॥

...the greatly radiant Gautama abandoned this hermitage and performed austerities upon a beautiful peak of the Himalayas, frequented by Siddhas and Charanas.

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे अष्टचत्वारिंशः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the forty-eighth Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
