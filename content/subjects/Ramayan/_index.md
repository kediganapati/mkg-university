title: "Ramayan"
---

All sarga of the 6+1 chapters (Bala Kand, Ayodhya Kand, Aranya Kand, Kishkindha Kand, Sundara Kand, Yuddha Kand, and Uttara Kand) are available here.

The 7th chapter, Uttara Kand, is not accepted by MKG as part of the original composition, but interpretation is still provided based on the text as it exists today.

Interpretations of some shlokas are still pending because I have not yet gained sufficient personal experience to interpret them. As my experience and understanding grow, I will continue adding interpretations until all kands are covered.

Numbering of shlokas in each sarga is not my own — it comes from available sources. I do not agree with or support every shloka as absolute reality; some shlokas may have been included to hide the truth, and some may have been added to divert from it. So I am only giving the interpretation and meaning of the shlokas, not writing a new Ramayan.
