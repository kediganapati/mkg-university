## Verse 1

अथ तां रजनीं तत्र कृतार्थौ रामलक्ष्मणौ ।
ऊषतुर्मुदितौ वीरौ प्रहृष्टेनान्तरात्मना ॥

There, having accomplished their purpose, the heroic Rama and Lakshmana spent that night with joyful hearts.

## Verse 2

प्रभातायां तु शर्वर्यां कृतपौर्वाह्णिकक्रियौ ।
विश्वामित्रमृषिं चान्यांसहितावभिजग्मतुः ॥

When the night had passed and dawn appeared, the two brothers completed their morning duties and approached the sage Vishvamitra together with the other sages.

## Verse 3

अभिवाद्य मुनिश्रेष्ठं ज्वलन्तमिव पावकम् ।
ऊचतुर्मधुरोदर्कं वचनं मधुराक्षरम् ॥

Having bowed to the foremost of sages, who shone like fire, they spoke words that were sweet and auspicious.

## Verse 4

इमौ स्म मुनिशार्दूल कृतार्थौ सह लक्ष्मणौ ।
यज्ञस्य च प्रसादेन गन्तुमिच्छाव मैथिलम् ॥

"O tiger among sages, together with Lakshmana we have accomplished our purpose. By the grace of the sacrifice, we wish to go to Mithila."

## Verse 5

तत्र यज्ञसमाप्तिं च धनुषश्च महात्मनः ।
द्रष्टुमिच्छावहे ब्रह्मन् धनुषोऽद्भुतदर्शनम् ॥

"O Brahman, we wish to see the completion of the sacrifice there and the great bow, whose appearance is wondrous."

## Verse 6

इत्युक्ते वचने रामे विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।
अब्रवीन्मधुरं वाक्यं हृष्टो दशरथात्मजौ ॥

When Rama spoke these words, the great sage Vishvamitra, delighted, spoke sweetly to the sons of Dasharatha.

## Verse 7

गच्छावहे महाबाहू मिथिलां जनकस्य च ।
यज्ञं द्रक्ष्यथ तत्रैव धनुश्चाद्भुतदर्शनम् ॥

"Let us go, O mighty-armed ones, to Mithila and to the kingdom of Janaka. There you shall see the sacrifice and the wondrous bow."

## Verse 8

ततो मुनिवरः श्रीमान् कौशिको रघुनन्दनौ ।
आमन्त्र्य प्रययौ तत्र सिद्धाश्रमनिवासिनः ॥

Then the illustrious sage Kaushika took leave of the inhabitants of Siddhashrama and set out with the delight of the Raghu dynasty.

## Verse 9

ते सर्वे मुनयः सिद्धा विश्वामित्रपुरोगमाः ।
प्रययुर्मिथिलां रम्यां रामलक्ष्मणसंयुताः ॥

All those accomplished sages, led by Vishvamitra and accompanied by Rama and Lakshmana, proceeded toward beautiful Mithila.

## Verse 10

गच्छतां तु महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।
अथ शिष्यगणान् सर्वान् उवाच वचनं मुनिः ॥

As they journeyed, the greatly radiant great sage Vishvamitra spoke to all his disciples.

## Verse 11

शीघ्रं गच्छत भद्रं वो नदीं शोणां शुभोदकाम् ।
तस्यास्तीरे निवत्स्यामो रात्रिं यामोऽद्य मा चिरम् ॥

"Go quickly, may you be blessed, to the beautiful river Shona with its auspicious waters. We shall spend the night on its bank. Let us go there without delay."

## Verse 12

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा मुनयः शीघ्रगामिनः ।
जग्मुः शोणस्य तीरं ते समागम्य महर्षयः ॥

Hearing his words, the sages, moving swiftly, went to the bank of the Shona and gathered there.

## Verse 13

ते तत्र हृष्टमनसो रामलक्ष्मणसंयुताः ।
कथाश्चक्रुर्महाभागा मुनयः पुण्यकर्मणः ॥

There, together with Rama and Lakshmana, the greatly blessed and righteous sages spent the time in joyful conversation.

## Verse 14

अथ रामो महातेजा विश्वामित्रमृषिं तदा ।
पप्रच्छ मुनिशार्दूलं कौतूहलसमन्वितः ॥

Then the greatly radiant Rama, filled with curiosity, asked the tiger among sages, Vishvamitra.

## Verse 15

भगवन् किमिदं देशः समृद्धवनशोभितः ।
श्रोतुमिच्छामि भद्रं ते वक्तुमर्हसि तत्त्वतः ॥

"O Bhagavan, what is this region, adorned with flourishing forests? May you be blessed. I wish to hear about it; you should tell me the truth concerning it."

## Verse 16

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।
उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं रामं सत्यपराक्रमम् ॥

Hearing those words, the great sage Vishvamitra spoke sweetly to Rama of true valor.

## Verse 17

अयं देशः पुरा राम सुमहान् समृद्धिमान् ।
अङ्गदेश इति ख्यातो लोकेषु नृपसत्तम ॥

"O Rama, this was formerly a great and prosperous region, known throughout the world as Anga."

## Verse 18

तत्र राजा पुरा राम कामदेव इति स्मृतः ।
तस्य पुत्रोऽभवत् श्रीमान् लोमपाद इति श्रुतः ॥

"O Rama, formerly there was a king known as Kamadeva. His illustrious son was known as Lomapada."

## Verse 19

तस्य देशस्य महतीं कथां शृणु नरर्षभ ।
यथा चायं प्रदेशोऽभूत् पुण्यः पुण्यतरस्तव ॥

"O best among men, listen to the great history of this land and how this region became sacred and even more sacred."

## Verse 20

तत्र राजा महातेजा लोमपादो महाबलः ।
राज्यं प्रशासति श्रीमान् धर्मेण सुसमाहितः ॥

There the greatly radiant and mighty king Lomapada ruled his kingdom, firmly established in righteousness.

## Verse 21

अथ काले गते तस्मिन् रामो धर्मपरायणः ।
तत्रागच्छत् महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ॥

After some time, the greatly radiant and righteous king came there, while the great sage Vishvamitra also arrived in that region.

## Verse 22

अथ रामो महातेजा विश्वामित्रं तपोधनम् ।
पप्रच्छ मुनिशार्दूलं कौतूहलसमन्वितः ॥

Then the greatly radiant Rama, filled with curiosity, asked the tiger among sages, Vishvamitra, whose wealth was austerity.

## Verse 23

भगवन् कः न्वयं देशः समृद्धवनशोभितः ।
श्रोतुमिच्छामि भद्रं ते वक्तुमर्हसि तत्त्वतः ॥

"O Bhagavan, what indeed is this region, shining with flourishing forests? May you be blessed. I wish to hear about it; you should tell me its true history."

## Verse 24

चोदितो रामवाक्येन कथयामास सुव्रतः ।
तस्य देशस्य निखिलमृषिमध्ये महातपाः ॥

Urged by Rama's words, that great ascetic, steadfast in his vows, began to recount the entire history of that region in the midst of the sages.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे एकत्रिंशः सर्गः ॥

Thus ends the thirty-first Sarga of Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the first epic composed by Sage Valmiki.
