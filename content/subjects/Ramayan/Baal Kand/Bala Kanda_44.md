## Verse 1

ततो रामः स लक्ष्मणो विश्वामित्रेण धीमता ।

सहितो मुनिशार्दूलं गङ्गां पप्रच्छ राघवः ॥

Then Rama, together with Lakshmana and the wise Vishvamitra, asked the tiger among sages about Ganga.

## Verse 2

भगवन् श्रोतुमिच्छामि गङ्गायाः सरितां वराम् ।

त्रिपथां कथमाख्यातां कथं वा जाह्नवी शुभा ॥

“O Bhagavan, I wish to hear about Ganga, the foremost of rivers. Why is she known as Tripatha, and how did the auspicious Jahnavi come to be?”

## Verse 3

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं राघवं रघुनन्दनम् ॥

Hearing those words, the great sage Vishvamitra spoke sweetly to Rama, the delight of the Raghu dynasty.

## Verse 4

शृणु राम महाभाग गङ्गायाः सम्भवं शुभम् ।

यथा त्रिपथगा जाता जाह्नवी च यथा शुभा ॥

“O greatly fortunate Rama, listen to the auspicious origin of Ganga, how she became Tripathaga and how she came to be known as the auspicious Jahnavi.”

## Verse 5

हिमवांश्चैव विन्ध्यश्च मेरुश्चैव महागिरिः ।

तेषां मध्ये तु गङ्गा वै प्रवृत्ता सरितां वरा ॥

Himalaya, Vindhya and the great mountain Meru stood among the great mountains. Among them flowed Ganga, the foremost of rivers.

## Verse 6

सर्वलोकहितार्थाय देवैः सा निर्मिता नदी ।

त्रिषु लोकेषु विख्याता पुण्या पापप्रणाशिनी ॥

That river was created for the welfare of all the worlds. She became renowned throughout the three worlds as sacred and destroyer of sins.

## Verse 7

तां तु देवा महाभागां गङ्गां त्रिपथगां नदीम् ।

स्वर्गे मन्दाकिनीं प्राहुर्भुवि भागीरथीं तथा ॥

The gods call that greatly blessed river Ganga Mandakini in heaven, while upon earth she is known as Bhagirathi.

## Verse 8

पाताले भोगवती नाम गङ्गा त्रिपथगा स्मृता ।

एवं त्रिषु पथेष्वेषा गङ्गा लोकान् पुनाति वै ॥

In the subterranean world Ganga is known as Bhogavati. Thus, flowing through three paths, Ganga purifies the worlds.

## Verse 9

पूर्वं तु ब्रह्मलोकस्था गङ्गा लोकपवित्रिणी ।

देवानां हितकामाय पृथिव्यामवतारिता ॥

Originally dwelling in the world of Brahma, Ganga, the purifier of the worlds, descended upon earth for the welfare of the gods.

## Verse 10

भगीरथेन राजर्षिणा तपसा भावितात्मना ।

आनीता पृथिवीं देवी गङ्गा त्रिपथगा शुभा ॥

The auspicious divine Ganga, flowing through the three paths, was brought to earth by the royal sage Bhagiratha, whose mind had been purified through austerities.

## Verse 11

तस्याः प्रभावं देवेशो न शक्नोति कथञ्चन ।

वक्तुं वर्षशतैरपि सर्वलोकहितैषिणः ॥

Even the Lord of the gods could not fully describe her greatness in hundreds of years, for she works for the welfare of all the worlds.

## Verse 12

गङ्गायाः पतनं राम शृणु विस्तरतो मम ।

यथा सा पतिता देवी शम्भोर्मूर्ध्नि महात्मनः ॥

O Rama, listen in detail to the descent of Ganga and how the goddess fell upon the head of the great-souled Shambhu.

## Verse 13

स तु राजा महातेजा भगीरथ इति स्मृतः ।

तपसा तामनुप्राप्य गङ्गामानयदव्ययाम् ॥

That greatly radiant king, known as Bhagiratha, obtained Ganga through his austerities and brought the eternal river to earth.

## Verse 14

तस्य तु प्रयतो राज्ञो गङ्गामन्वेषतोऽभवत् ।

पितॄणां च हितार्थाय ययौ गङ्गा महापगा ॥

As the king devoted himself to bringing Ganga for the welfare of his ancestors, the great river followed him.

## Verse 15

गङ्गायाः पतनं दृष्ट्वा देवा ऋषिगणास्तथा ।

साधु साध्विति तं राजन् भगीरथमपूजयन् ॥

Seeing the descent of Ganga, the gods and hosts of sages praised King Bhagiratha, saying, “Well done! Well done!”

## Verse 16

ततो गङ्गा महावेगा भगीरथमनुव्रता ।

जगाम सरितां श्रेष्ठा समुद्रस्य निवेशनम् ॥

Then Ganga, possessing tremendous speed and following Bhagiratha, proceeded toward the ocean, the abode of the foremost waters.

## Verse 17

ततोऽनु गच्छती गङ्गा जह्नुं मुनिवरं ययौ ।

तस्य क्रोधसमुद्भूतं वारि सर्वमपाहरत् ॥

As Ganga continued on her course, she reached the excellent sage Jahnu and carried away all the water that had arisen through his anger.

## Verse 18

ततः स मुनिशार्दूलो जह्नुर्नाम महातपाः ।

अपिबत् सलिलं सर्वं गङ्गायाः सरितां वराम् ॥

Then the great ascetic Jahnu, the tiger among sages, drank all the water of Ganga, the foremost of rivers.

## Verse 19

ततः सुरगणाः सर्वे ऋषयश्च तपोधनाः ।

जाह्नवीं सरितां श्रेष्ठां तं मुनिं पर्यवारयन् ॥

Then all the hosts of gods and the sages whose wealth was austerity surrounded that sage and requested him to release Jahnavi, the foremost of rivers.

## Verse 20

तुष्टावुः परमप्रीता जह्नुं मुनिवरं तदा ।

गङ्गां चापि महाभागां मोक्तुमर्हसि सुव्रत ॥

They greatly pleased and praised the excellent sage Jahnu and said, “O righteous one, you should release the greatly blessed Ganga.”

## Verse 21

ततः स मुनिशार्दूलो जह्नुर्नाम महातपाः ।

उत्ससर्ज महावेगां गङ्गां लोकनमस्कृताम् ॥

Then the great ascetic Jahnu, the tiger among sages, released the immensely swift Ganga, who is revered by all the worlds.

## Verse 22

तस्मात् तु जाह्नवी नाम गङ्गा लोकप्रविश्रुता ।

जाह्नोः कन्येति चाख्याता त्रिषु लोकेषु विश्रुता ॥

Therefore Ganga became widely known throughout the worlds as Jahnavi and was also known as the daughter of Jahnu.

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदि काव्ये बाल काण्डे चतुश्चत्वारिंशः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the forty-fourth Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
