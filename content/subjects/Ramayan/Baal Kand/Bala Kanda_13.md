## Verse 1

अथ संवत्सरे पूर्णे तस्मिन् प्राप्ते तुरङ्गमे |
सरय्वाश्चोत्तरे तीरे राज्ञो यज्ञोऽभ्यवर्तत || १-१३-१

After a full year had passed and the sacrificial horse had returned, the king's sacrifice commenced on the northern bank of the Sarayu.

## Verse 2

ऋष्यशृङ्गं पुरस्कृत्य वसिष्ठं च द्विजोत्तमम् |
अश्वमेधेन यक्ष्येऽहमिति राजा व्यवस्थितः || १-१३-२

With Rishyasringa and the foremost Brahmana Vasishtha at the head, the king resolved to perform the Ashvamedha sacrifice.

## Verse 3

ऋत्विजस्तु यथान्यायं यथाशास्त्रं यथाक्रमम् |
चक्रिरे तं महायज्ञं राज्ञो दशरथस्य ह || १-१३-३

The priests performed that great sacrifice of King Dasharatha according to proper procedure, the scriptures, and the prescribed order.

## Verse 4

अश्वमेधे महायज्ञे विधिवत् सम्प्रवर्तिते |
तत्र यज्ञस्य नियमान् कल्पयामासुरेव ते || १-१३-४

When the great Ashvamedha sacrifice had properly commenced, they established all the rules and arrangements required for the sacrifice.

## Verse 5

सर्वकामफलप्राप्तिर्यस्य यज्ञस्य यज्वनः |
तस्य यज्ञस्य सम्पत्तिं सर्वे विप्रा उपासते || १-१३-५

All the Brahmanas attended to the successful completion of that sacrifice, which grants the performer the fulfillment of his desires.

## Verse 6

ततोऽश्वं यज्ञियं हत्वा शास्त्रदृष्टेन कर्मणा |
ऋत्विजस्तस्य राजर्षेः कर्म चक्रुर्यथाविधि || १-१३-६

Then, having ritually sacrificed the sacrificial horse according to the prescribed procedure, the priests performed the required rites for that royal sage.

## Verse 7

ततो यज्ञस्य यूपान् विधिवत् स्थापयन्ति ते |
सुवर्णरजतादीनि रत्नानि विविधानि च || १-१३-७

Then they properly erected the sacrificial posts and arranged various ornaments and precious materials of gold, silver, and other kinds.

## Verse 8

एकविंशतियूपास्तु तत्रासन् मुनिपुङ्गवाः |
बिल्ववृक्षैश्च खदिरैः पलाशैश्चैव निर्मिताः || १-१३-८

O foremost among sages, there were twenty-one sacrificial posts there. They were made from Bilva, Khadira, Palasha, and other prescribed woods.

## Verse 9

तद् यथाशास्त्रं विधिवत् सर्वं सम्पादितं तदा |
यूपेषु च नियोज्यन्ते पशवः शास्त्रचोदिताः || १-१३-९

Everything was then prepared according to the scriptures and proper procedure, and the animals prescribed by the sacred rules were assigned to the sacrificial posts.

## Verse 10

तत्र पशूनां नियतं संख्यातं शास्त्रवित्तमाः |
उपाकुर्वन्ति विधिवत् यज्ञस्यार्थे द्विजोत्तमाः || १-१३-१०

The foremost Brahmanas, learned in the sacred rules, properly prepared the prescribed number of animals for the purpose of the sacrifice.

## Verse 11

अश्वमेधे तु यज्ञेऽस्मिन् पशूनां नियते विधौ |
उरगाः पक्षिणश्चैव पशवश्च तथा नराः || १-१३-११

In that Ashvamedha sacrifice, according to the prescribed rules concerning the sacrificial victims, there were serpents, birds, animals, and human beings.

## Verse 12

यूपेषु तान् यथान्यायं नियोज्य मुनिपुङ्गवाः |
सर्वं विधिवदाचक्रुर्यज्ञकर्म द्विजोत्तमाः || १-१३-१२

The foremost sages properly assigned them to the sacrificial posts according to the prescribed procedure, and the Brahmanas carried out all the sacrificial rites according to the rules.

## Verse 13

शामित्रे तु हयस्याथ वपामुद्धृत्य शास्त्रतः |
ऋत्विजः प्राशयन् सर्वे यथाशास्त्रं यथाविधि || १-१३-१३

At the prescribed place, the priests extracted the sacrificial portion of the horse according to the sacred rules and partook of it as prescribed.

## Verse 14

पत्न्यश्च तस्य राजर्षेः शास्त्रदृष्टेन कर्मणा |
अश्वस्य वपयास्यन्तु वाससाऽऽच्छाद्य चाभवत् || १-१३-१४

The wives of the royal sage then participated in the prescribed rite involving the horse, according to the rules laid down in the sacred texts.

## Verse 15

ततः पत्नीसमायुक्तं त्रिरात्रं यज्ञमेव च |
अश्वमेधस्य विधिना राज्ञो यज्ञोऽभ्यवर्तत || १-१३-१५

Then, together with the king's wives, the sacrifice continued for three nights according to the prescribed procedure of the Ashvamedha.

## Verse 16

अश्वमेधे महायज्ञे दशरथस्य धीमतः |
ऋत्विजः कर्म चक्रुस्ते यथाशास्त्रं यथाक्रमम् || १-१३-१६

In the great Ashvamedha sacrifice of the wise Dasharatha, the priests performed all the rites according to the scriptures and in proper order.

## Verse 17

अहल्यां चापि धर्मज्ञो गौतमोऽथ महातपाः |
यथाशास्त्रं समानीय तां चक्रे यज्ञसंहिताम् || १-१३-१७

The highly ascetic and Dharma-knowing Gautama also performed the prescribed sacrificial rites according to the sacred procedure.

## Verse 18

सर्वं हि विधिवद् यज्ञं तत्र चक्रुर्द्विजोत्तमाः |
न्यायेन विधिना चैव राज्ञो यज्ञस्य यज्वनः || १-१३-१८

The foremost Brahmanas performed the entire sacrifice according to the proper rules and procedure for the king who was its performer.

## Verse 19

ऋत्विजस्तु ततः सर्वे यथाशास्त्रं यथाविधि |
राज्ञो दशरथस्यैव यज्ञं निर्वर्तयन्ति ते || १-१३-१९

Then all the priests completed King Dasharatha's sacrifice according to the scriptures and prescribed procedure.

## Verse 20

ततः स राजा दशरथः कृतयज्ञो महाबलः |
ऋत्विग्भ्यो दक्षिणां दत्त्वा तान् सर्वान् विससर्ज ह || १-१३-२०

Then the mighty King Dasharatha, having completed the sacrifice, gave the priests their prescribed gifts and dismissed them all.

## Verse 21

ततः कृतार्थाः स ऋत्विजः सर्वे द्विजोत्तमाः |
यथार्हं पूजितास्तेन राज्ञा यज्ञस्य कर्मणि || १-१३-२१

All those foremost Brahmanas and priests, their duties fulfilled, were properly honoured by the king for their participation in the sacrifice.

## Verse 22

ततोऽब्रवीद् दशरथो वसिष्ठं मुनिपुङ्गवम् |
यज्ञस्य फलमिच्छामि पुत्रान् मे दीयतां प्रभो || १-१३-२२

Then Dasharatha said to the foremost sage Vasishtha:

“O revered one, I desire the fruit of this sacrifice. May sons be granted to me.”

## Verse 23

ततो वसिष्ठो भगवान् ऋष्यशृङ्गं च वीर्यवान् |
सम्पूज्य विधिवत् राजा पुत्रकामोऽभ्यभाषत || १-१३-२३

Then the powerful king properly honoured the divine Vasishtha and Rishyasringa and spoke to them, expressing his desire for sons.

## Verse 24

तौ च तं राजशार्दूलं पुत्रकामं नृपोत्तमम् |
ऊचतुस्ते महात्मानौ पुत्रेष्टिं ते करिष्यतः || १-१३-२४

Those two great-souled sages said to that foremost king, who desired sons:

“We shall perform the Putreshti sacrifice for you.”

## Verse 25

इत्युक्त्वा तौ महात्मानौ राजानं पुत्रकामुकम् |
यज्ञार्थं संनियोज्याथ सर्वकार्याण्यकारयताम् || १-१३-२५

Having spoken thus, those two great-souled sages engaged the king, who desired sons, in the sacrifice and arranged all the necessary preparations.

## Verse 26

ततो यज्ञसमाप्त्यर्थं पुत्रेष्टिं विधिवत् तदा |
ऋष्यशृङ्गो महातेजाः समारभत वीर्यवान् || १-१३-२६

Then the greatly radiant and powerful Rishyasringa commenced the Putreshti sacrifice according to the prescribed procedure for the completion of the king's sacrificial purpose.

## Verse 27

ततः सर्वे महात्मानः ऋत्विजः समुपस्थिताः |
यज्ञकर्म समारभ्य विधिवत् सम्प्रचक्रिरे || १-१३-२७

Then all the great-souled priests assembled and commenced the sacrificial rites, performing them properly according to the prescribed procedure.
