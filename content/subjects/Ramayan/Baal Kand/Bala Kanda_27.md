## Verse 1

अथ तां रजनीमुष्य विश्वामित्रो महायशाः ।
प्रहस्य राघवं वाक्यमुवाच मधुराक्षरम् ॥

After spending that night there, the greatly renowned Vishvamitra smiled and spoke sweet words to Rama.

## Verse 2

परितुष्टोऽस्मि भद्रं ते राजपुत्र महायशः ।
प्रीत्या परमया युक्तो ददाम्यस्त्राणि सर्वशः ॥

I am greatly pleased with you. May you be blessed, O illustrious prince. Filled with the greatest affection, I shall give you all the celestial weapons.

## Verse 3

देवासुरगणान् वापि सगन्धर्वोरगान्भुवि ।
यैरमित्रान् प्रसह्याजौ वशीकृत्य जयिष्यसि ॥

With these weapons you shall be able to overpower and conquer in battle the hosts of gods and Asuras, Gandharvas and Nagas, and other enemies upon the earth.

## Verse 4

तानि दिव्यानि भद्रं ते ददाम्यस्त्राणि सर्वशः ।
दण्डचक्रं महद्दिव्यं तव दास्यामि राघव ॥

May you be blessed. I shall give you all these divine weapons. O Raghava, I shall give you the great celestial Danda Chakra.

## Verse 5

धर्मचक्रं ततो वीर कालचक्रं तथैव च ।
विष्णुचक्रं तथा चोग्रमैन्द्रं चक्रमथैव च ॥

Then, O hero, I shall give you the Dharma Chakra and likewise the Kala Chakra, the Vishnu Chakra, the extremely powerful Chakra of Indra—

## Verse 6

वज्रमस्त्रं नरश्रेष्ठ शैवं शूलवरं तथा ।
अस्त्रं ब्रह्मशिरश्चैव ऐषीकमपि राघव ॥

O best of men, the Vajra weapon, the excellent Shaiva Trident, the Brahmashira weapon, and the Aishika weapon, O Raghava.

## Verse 7

ददामि ते महाबाहो ब्राह्ममस्त्रमनुत्तमम् ।
गदे द्वे चैव काकुत्स्थ मोदकीशिखरी शुभे ॥

O mighty-armed Kakutstha, I give you the unsurpassed Brahma weapon and two beautiful maces named Modaki and Shikhari.

## Verse 8

धर्मपाशमहं राम कालपाशं तथैव च ।
वारुणं पाशमस्त्रं च ददाम्यहमनुत्तमम् ॥

O Rama, I shall give you the Dharma Pasha, the Kala Pasha, and the unsurpassed Varuna Pasha weapon.

## Verse 9

अशनी द्वे प्रयच्छामि शुष्कार्द्रे रघुनन्दन ।
ददामि चास्त्रं पैनाकमस्त्रं नारायणं तथा ॥

O delight of the Raghus, I give you two Ashanis, named Shushka and Ardra. I also give you the Pinaaka weapon and the Narayana weapon.

## Verse 10

आग्नेयमस्त्रं दयितं शिखरं नाम नामतः ।
वायव्यं प्रथमं नाम ददामि तव चानघ ॥

O sinless one, I give you the beloved Agneya weapon named Shikhara and the first Vayavya weapon.

## Verse 11

अस्त्रं हयशिरो नाम क्रौञ्चमस्त्रं तथैव च ।
शक्तिद्वयं च काकुत्स्थ ददामि तव राघव ॥

O Raghava, O Kakutstha, I give you the weapon named Hayashira, the Krauncha weapon, and two Shaktis.

## Verse 12

कङ्कालं मुसलं घोरं कापालमथ किङ्किणीम् ।
वधार्थं राक्षसान् यानि ददाम्येतानि सर्वशः ॥

I give you the dreadful Kankala, Musala, Kapala and Kinkini weapons, all of which are used for destroying Rakshasas.

## Verse 13

वैद्याधरं महास्त्रं च नन्दनं नाम नामतः ।
असिरत्नं महाबाहो ददामि नृवरात्मज ॥

O mighty-armed son of the best of kings, I give you the great Vaidyadhara weapon and the excellent sword named Nandana.

## Verse 14

गान्धर्वमस्त्रं दयितं मोहनं नाम नामतः ।
प्रस्वापनं प्रशमनं दद्मि सौम्यं च राघव ॥

O Raghava, I give you the beloved Gandharva weapon named Mohana, the Prasvapana weapon, the Prashamana weapon, and the gentle Saumya weapon.

## Verse 15

वर्षणं शोषणं चैव सन्तापनविलापने ।
मादनं चैव दुर्धर्षं कन्दर्पदयितं तथा ॥

I give you the Varshana, Shoshana, Santapana, Vilapana and Madana weapons, the latter being dear to Kandarpa and difficult to resist.

## Verse 16

गान्धर्वमस्त्रं दयितं मानवन्नाम नामतः ।
पैशाचमस्त्रं दयितं मोहनं नाम नामतः ॥

I give you the Gandharva weapon named Manava and the Paishacha weapon named Mohana.

## Verse 17

प्रतीच्छ नरशार्दूल राजपुत्र महायशः ।
एतानि राम महाबाहो कामरूपाणि सर्वशः ॥

Receive these, O tiger among men, O illustrious prince. O mighty-armed Rama, these weapons can assume forms according to their will.

## Verse 18

गृहाण परमोदारान् क्षिप्रम् एव नृपात्मज ।
तामसं नरशार्दूल सौमनं च महाबलम् ॥

O son of the king, quickly receive these most excellent weapons: the Tamasa and the greatly powerful Saumana.

## Verse 19

संवर्तं चैव दुर्धर्षं मौसलं च नृपात्मज ।
सत्यं चास्त्रं महाबाहो तथा मायामयं परम् ॥

O prince, receive the invincible Samvarta and Mausala weapons. O mighty-armed one, receive the Satya weapon and the supreme Mayamaya weapon.

## Verse 20

सौरं तेजःप्रभं नाम परतेजोऽपकर्षणम् ।
सोमास्त्रं शिशिरं नाम त्वाष्ट्रमस्त्रं सुदारुणम् ॥

I give you the Solar weapon named Tejahprabha, which draws away the brilliance of others; the lunar weapon named Shishira; and the extremely dreadful Tvashta weapon.

## Verse 21

दारुणं च भगस्यापि शितेषुमथ मानवम् ।
एतान् राम महाबाहो कामरूपान् महाबलान् ॥

I also give you the dreadful weapon of Bhaga named Shiteshu and the Manava weapon. O mighty-armed Rama, these weapons possess great power and can assume forms according to their will.

## Verse 22

गृहाण परमोदारान् क्षिप्रम् एव नृपात्मज ।
स्थितस्तु प्राङ्मुखो भूत्वा शुचिर्मुनिवरस्तदा ॥

Receive these most excellent weapons quickly, O son of the king. Then the foremost of sages, having purified himself, stood facing east.

## Verse 23

ददौ रामाय सुप्रीतो मन्त्रग्राममनुत्तमम् ।
सर्वसंग्रहणमेषां दैवतैरपि दुर्लभम् ॥

Being greatly pleased, he gave Rama the unsurpassed collection of mantras for these weapons, the complete knowledge of which is difficult even for the gods to obtain.

## Verse 24

तानि सर्वाणि दिव्यानि ददौ रामाय धीमते ।
जपतस्तु मुनेस्तस्य विश्वामित्रस्य धीमतः ॥

He gave all those divine weapons to the intelligent Rama. While the wise sage Vishvamitra was reciting the mantras—

## Verse 25

उपतस्थुर्महार्हाणि सर्वाण्यस्त्राणि राघवम् ।
ऊचुश्च मुदिता रामं सर्वे प्राञ्जलयस्तदा ॥

—all the highly revered weapons appeared before Raghava. Then, delighted and with folded hands, they spoke to Rama:

इमे च परमोदार किंकरास्तव राघव ।
यद्यदिच्छसि भद्रं ते तत्सर्वं करवाम वै ॥

"Here we are, O Raghava, your most devoted servants. May you be blessed. Whatever you desire, we shall do."

## Verse 26

ततो रामः प्रसन्नात्मा विश्वामित्रं महामुनिम् ।
अभिवाद्य महातेजा गमनायोपचक्रमे ॥

Then Rama, with a pleased heart, bowed to the great sage Vishvamitra and, greatly radiant, prepared to continue the journey.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे सप्तविंशः सर्गः ॥

Thus ends the twenty-seventh Sarga of Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the first epic composed by Sage Valmiki.
