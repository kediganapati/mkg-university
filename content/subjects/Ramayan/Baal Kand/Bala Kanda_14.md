## Verse 1

अथ संवत्सरे पूर्णे तस्मिन् प्राप्ते तुरङ्गमे |

सरय्वाः च उत्तरे तीरे राज्ञो यज्ञो ऽभ्यवर्तत || १-१४-१

After one year had been completed and the sacrificial horse had returned, the king's sacrifice commenced on the northern bank of the Sarayu.

## Verse 2

ऋश्यशृङ्गं पुरस्कृत्य कर्म चक्रुर्द्विजर्षभाः |

अश्वमेधे महायज्ञे राज्ञो ऽस्य सुमहात्मनः || १-१४-२

Placing Rishyasringa at the forefront, the foremost Brahmanas performed the rites in the great Ashvamedha sacrifice of that noble king.

## Verse 3

कर्म कुर्वन्ति विधिवद्याजका वेदपारगाः |

यथाविधि यथान्यायं परिक्रामन्ति शास्त्रतः || १-१४-३

The priests, learned in the Vedas, performed the rites according to the prescribed procedure and proceeded according to the rules of scripture.

## Verse 4

प्रवर्ग्यं शास्त्रतः कृत्वा तथैवोपसदं द्विजाः |

चक्रुश्च विधिवत्सर्वमधिकं कर्म शास्त्रतः || १-१४-४

The Brahmanas performed the Pravargya and likewise the Upasad rites according to scripture, and they performed all the additional rites as prescribed.

## Verse 5

अभिपूज्य ततो हृष्टाः सर्वे चक्रुर्यथाविधि |

प्रातःसवनपूर्वाणि कर्माणि मुनिपुङ्गवाः || १-१४-५

Having duly worshipped the deities, all those foremost sages, rejoicing, performed the rites beginning with the morning pressing according to the prescribed procedure.

## Verse 6

ऐन्द्रश्च विधिवद्दत्तो राजा चाभिष्टुतो ऽनघः |

माध्यन्दिनं च सवनं प्रावर्तत यथाक्रमम् || १-१४-६

The offering belonging to Indra was duly given, and the sinless king was praised. Then the midday pressing proceeded in its proper order.

## Verse 7

तृतीयसवनं चैव राज्ञो ऽस्य सुमहात्मनः |

चक्रुस्ते शास्त्रतो दृष्ट्वा तथा ब्राह्मणपुङ्गवाः || १-१४-७

The foremost Brahmanas likewise performed the third pressing of that great-souled king according to the prescriptions of scripture.

## Verse 8

न चाहुतमभूत्तत्र स्खलितं वापि किञ्चन |

दृश्यते ब्रह्मवत्सर्वं क्षेमयुक्तं हि चक्रिरे || १-१४-८

There was nothing there that remained unoffered, nor was there any mistake. Everything was performed with the mantras, and they carried out the rites without hindrance.

## Verse 9

न तेष्वहस्सु श्रान्तो वा क्षुधितो वापि दृश्यते |

नाविद्वान् ब्राह्मणस्तत्र नाशतानुचरस्तथा || १-१४-९

During those days no one was seen to be weary or hungry. There was no Brahmana who was unlearned, nor was there any Brahmana without a hundred attendants.

## Verse 10

ब्राह्मणा भुञ्जते नित्यं नाथवन्तश्च भुञ्जते |

तापसा भुञ्जते चापि श्रमणा भुञ्जते तथा || १-१४-१०

The Brahmanas were constantly fed, as were those who had attendants. The ascetics were also fed, and the wandering religious mendicants were likewise fed.

## Verse 11

वृद्धाश्च व्याधिताश्चैव स्त्रियो बालास्तथैव च |

अनिशं भुञ्जमानानां न तृप्तिरुपलभ्यते || १-१४-११

The elderly, the sick, women, and children, though eating continually, found no lack of satisfaction.

## Verse 12

दीयतां दीयतामन्नं वासांसि विविधानि च |

इति सञ्चोदितास्तत्र तथा चक्रुरनेकशः || १-१४-१२

"Let food be given, let food be given, and various garments as well." Thus instructed, they distributed them there repeatedly.

## Verse 13

अन्नकूटाश्च बहवो दृश्यन्ते पर्वतोपमाः |

दिवसे दिवसे तत्र सिद्धस्य विधिवत्तदा || १-१४-१३

Many heaps of prepared food, resembling mountains, were seen there, being properly prepared day after day.

## Verse 14

नानादेशादनुप्राप्ताः पुरुषाः स्त्रीगणास्तथा |

अन्नपानैः सुविहितास्तस्मिन् यज्ञे महात्मनः || १-१४-१४

Men and groups of women who had arrived from many different countries were well supplied with food and drink at that sacrifice of the great-souled king.

## Verse 15

अन्नं हि विधिवत्स्वादु प्रशंसन्ति द्विजर्षभाः |

अहो तृप्ताः स्म भद्रं त इति शुश्राव राघवः || १-१४-१५

The foremost Brahmanas praised the food, saying that it was delicious and properly prepared. "Ah, we are satisfied; may good fortune be yours." Raghava heard these words.

## Verse 16

स्वलङ्कृताश्च पुरुषा ब्राह्मणान् पर्यवेषयन् |

उपासते च तानन्ये सुमृष्टमणिकुण्डलाः || १-१४-१६

Well-adorned men served the Brahmanas, while others, wearing finely polished jewel earrings, attended upon them.

## Verse 17

कर्मान्तरे तदा विप्रा हेतुवादान् बहूनपि |

प्राहुः स्म वाग्ग्मिनो धीराः परस्परजिगीषया || १-१४-१७

During the intervals between the rites, the learned and eloquent Brahmanas engaged in many discussions of reasoning, each wishing to prevail over the others.

## Verse 18

दिवसे दिवसे तत्र संस्तरे कुशला द्विजाः |

सर्वकर्माणि चक्रुस्ते यथाशास्त्रं प्रचोदिताः || १-१४-१८

Day after day, those skilled Brahmanas performed all the rites upon the sacrificial ground as instructed according to scripture.

## Verse 19

नाषडङ्गविदत्रासीन्नाव्रतो नाबहुश्रुतः |

सदस्यास्तस्य वै राज्ञो नावादकुशला द्विजाः || १-१४-१९

There was no one there who was not learned in the six branches of the Veda, no one without observance of the prescribed vows, and no one who had learned only a little. None of the Brahmanas serving as members of the king's sacrifice was unskilled in debate.

## Verse 20

प्राप्ते यूपोच्छ्रये तस्मिन् षड्बैल्वाः खादिरास्तथा |

तावन्तो बिल्वसहिताः पर्णिनश्च तथा ऽपरे || १-१४-२०

When the time came to erect the sacrificial posts, six posts of Bilva wood and six of Khadira wood were erected, and likewise six others of Parna wood, together with the Bilva posts.

## Verse 21

श्लेष्मातकमयस्त्वेको देवदारुमयस्तथा |

द्वावेव तत्र विहितौ बाहुव्यस्तपरिग्रहौ || १-१४-२१

One post was made of Shleshmātaka wood and likewise two were made of Devadāru wood. These were arranged there with the prescribed spacing measured by the span of the arms.

## Verse 22

कारिताः सर्व एवैते शास्त्रज्ञैर्यज्ञकोविदैः |

शोभार्थं तस्य यज्ञस्य काञ्चनालङ्कृता भवन् || १-१४-२२

All these posts were made by those learned in scripture and skilled in sacrifices, and they were adorned with gold for the beauty of the sacrifice.

## Verse 23

एकविंशति यूपास्ते एकविंशत्यरत्नयः |

वासोभिरेकविंशद्भिरेकैकं समलङ्कृताः || १-१४-२३

There were twenty-one sacrificial posts, each twenty-one aratnis in height, and each one was adorned with one of twenty-one garments.

## Verse 24

विन्यस्ता विधिवत्सर्वे शिल्पिभिः सुकृता दृढाः |

अष्टाश्रयः सर्व एव श्लक्ष्णरूपसमन्विताः || १-१४-२४

All of them were properly erected, well-made and strong, fashioned by craftsmen, with eight-sided surfaces and smooth forms.

## Verse 25

आच्छादितास्ते वासोभिः पुष्पैर्गन्धैश्च पूजिताः |

सप्तर्षयो दीप्तिमन्तो विराजन्ते यथा दिवि || १-१४-२५

Covered with garments and worshipped with flowers and fragrances, they shone brilliantly like the Seven Sages in the sky.

## Verse 26

इष्टकाश्च यथान्यायं कारिताश्च प्रमाणतः |

चितो ऽग्निर्ब्राह्मणैस्तत्र कुशलैः शुल्बकर्मणि || १-१४-२६

The bricks were made according to the prescribed rules and measurements, and the fire altar was constructed there by Brahmanas skilled in the work of the sacrificial measurements.

## Verse 27

स चित्यो राजसिंहस्य सञ्चितः कुशलैर्द्विजैः |

गरुडो रुक्मपक्षो वै त्रिगुणो ऽष्टादशात्मकः || १-१४-२७

The fire altar of that lion among kings was constructed by skilled Brahmanas. It was in the form of Garuda, with golden wings, three times the prescribed extent and consisting of eighteen sections.

## Verse 28

नियुक्तास्तत्र पशवस्तत्तदुद्दिश्य दैवतम् |

उरगाः पक्षिणश्चैव यथाशास्त्रं प्रचोदिताः || १-१४-२८

Animals, serpents, and birds were assigned there to their respective deities according to the prescriptions of scripture.

## Verse 29

शामित्रे तु हयस्तत्र तथा जलचराश्च ये |

ऋत्विग्भिः सर्वमेवैतन्नियुक्तं शास्त्रतस्तदा || १-१४-२९

The horse and the aquatic creatures were assigned there to the place of sacrifice, and all these were arranged by the officiating priests according to scripture.

## Verse 30

पशूनां त्रिशतं तत्र यूपेषु नियतं तदा |

अश्वरत्नोत्तमं तत्र राज्ञो दशरथस्य च || १-१४-३०

Three hundred animals were then bound to the sacrificial posts there, along with the finest and most excellent horse of King Dasharatha.

## Verse 31

कौसल्या तं हयं तत्र परिचर्य समन्ततः |

कृपाणैर्विशशासैनं त्रिभिः परमया मुदा || १-१४-३१

Kausalya, having attended upon that horse and circumambulated it there, performed the prescribed act upon it with three knives, with great joy.

## Verse 32

पतत्रिणा तदा सार्द्धं सुस्थितेन च चेतसा |

अवसद्रजनीमेकां कौसल्या धर्मकाम्यया || १-१४-३२

Then Kausalya, with a composed mind and desiring the fulfillment of the rite, remained for one night together with the horse.

## Verse 33

होताध्वर्युस्तथोद्गाता हस्तेन समयोजयन् |

महिष्या परिवृत्त्या च वावातामपरां तथा || १-१४-३३

The Hotri, Adhvaryu and Udgatri priests, likewise, joined by hand the chief queen, the neglected queen, and another woman, the Vavata, as prescribed in the rite.

## Verse 34

पतत्रिणस्तस्य वपामुद्धृत्य नियतेन्द्रियः |

ऋत्विक् परमसम्पन्नः श्रपयामास शास्त्रतः || १-१४-३४

The highly accomplished officiating priest, with controlled senses, removed the fat of that horse and cooked it according to scripture.

## Verse 35

धूमगन्धं वपायास्तु जिघ्रति स्म नराधिपः |

यथाकालं यथान्यायं निर्णुदन् पापमात्मनः || १-१४-३५

The king inhaled the fragrance of the smoke arising from the offering of the horse's fat, thereby removing his own sin according to the proper time and prescribed procedure.

## Verse 36

हयस्य यानि चाङ्गानि तानि सर्वाणि ब्राह्मणाः |

अग्नौ प्रास्यन्ति विधिवत्समन्त्राः षोडशर्त्विजः || १-१४-३६

The Brahmanas, the sixteen officiating priests, placed all the limbs of the horse into the fire according to the prescribed procedure and with the recitation of mantras.

## Verse 37

प्लक्षशाखासु यज्ञानामन्येषां क्रियते हविः |

अश्वमेधस्य यज्ञस्य वैतसो भाग इष्यते || १-१४-३७

For other sacrifices the offering is made upon branches of the Plaksha tree; for the Ashvamedha sacrifice, the portion is prescribed to be placed upon the Vaitasa.

## Verse 38

त्र्यहो ऽश्वमेधः सङ्ख्यातः कल्पसूत्रेण ब्राह्मणैः |

चतुष्टोममहस्तस्य प्रथमं परिकल्पितम् |

उक्थ्यं द्वितीयं सङ्ख्यातमतिरात्रं तथोत्तरम् || १-१४-३८

According to the Kalpa Sutra and the Brahmanas, the Ashvamedha is counted as lasting three days. The first day was arranged as Chatushtoma, the second as Ukthya, and the following day as Atiratra.

## Verse 39

कारितास्तत्र बहवो विहिताः शास्त्रदर्शनात् || १-१४-३९

Many other prescribed sacrifices were also performed there according to what is laid down in scripture.

## Verse 40

ज्योतिष्टोमायुषी चैवमतिरात्रौ विनिर्मितौ |

अभिजिद्विश्वजिच्चैवमाप्तोर्यामो महाक्रतुः || १-१४-४०

The Jyotishtoma, Ayushi and Atiratra rites were performed, as well as the great sacrifices Abhijit, Vishvajit and Aptoryama.

## Verse 41

प्राचीं होत्रे ददौ राजा दिशं स्वकुलवर्द्धनः |

अध्वर्यवे प्रतीचीं तु ब्रह्मणे दक्षिणां दिशम् || १-१४-४१

The king, desirous of increasing his lineage, gave the eastern direction to the Hotri, the western direction to the Adhvaryu, and the southern direction to the Brahma priest.

## Verse 42

उद्गात्रे वै तथोदीचीं दक्षिणैषा विनिर्मिता |

हयमेधे महायज्ञे स्वयम्भूविहिते पुरा || १-१४-४२

He likewise gave the northern direction to the Udgatri. Such was the prescribed sacrificial gift in the great Ashvamedha sacrifice established in ancient times by the Self-born.

## Verse 43

क्रतुं समाप्य तु तथा न्यायतः पुरुषर्षभः |

ऋत्विग्भ्यो हि ददौ राजा धरां तां क्रतुवर्द्धनः || १-१४-४३

Having completed the sacrifice according to the prescribed procedure, that foremost of men, the king, gave that land to the officiating priests.

## Verse 44

ऋत्विजश्चाब्रुवन् सर्वे राजानं गतकल्मषम् |

भवानेव महीं कृत्स्नामेको रक्षितुमर्हति || १-१४-४४

All the officiating priests then said to the king, who was freed from sin, "You alone are capable of protecting the entire earth."

## Verse 45

न भूम्या कार्यमस्माकं न हि शक्ताः स्म पालने |

रताः स्वाध्यायकरणे वयं नित्यं हि भूमिप |

निष्क्रयं किञ्चिदेवेह प्रयच्छतु भवानिति || १-१४-४५

"We have no use for the land, nor are we capable of governing it. O king, we are always engaged in the study and recitation of scripture. Therefore, give us something else here as its equivalent."

## Verse 46

मणिरत्नं सुवर्णं वा गावो यद्वा समुद्यतम् |

तत्प्रयच्छ नरश्रेष्ठ धरण्या न प्रयोजनम् || १-१४-४६

"Give us jewels, precious stones, gold, cows, or whatever else is available, O best of men. We have no use for the land."

## Verse 47

एवमुक्तो नरपतिर्ब्राह्मणैर्वेदपारगैः |

गवां शतसहस्राणि दश तेभ्यो ददौ नृपः || १-१४-४७

Thus addressed by the Brahmanas learned in the Vedas, the king gave them ten hundred thousand cows.

## Verse 48

दशकोटीस्सुवर्णस्य रजतस्य चतुर्गुणम् || १-१४-४८

He gave ten crores of gold and four times that amount in silver.

## Verse 49

ऋत्विजस्तु ततः सर्वे प्रददुः सहिता वसु |

ऋश्यशृङ्गाय मुनये वसिष्ठाय च धीमते || १-१४-४९

Then all the officiating priests together gave the wealth to the sage Rishyasringa and to the wise Vashishta.

## Verse 50

ततस्ते न्यायतः कृत्वा प्रविभागं द्विजोत्तमाः |

सुप्रीतमनसस्सर्वे प्रत्यूचुर्मुदिता भृशम् || १-१४-५०

Then those foremost Brahmanas divided the wealth among themselves according to the proper share and, greatly delighted, all replied with satisfied minds.

## Verse 51

ततः प्रसर्पकेभ्यस्तु हिरण्यं सुसमाहितः |

जाम्बूनदं कोटिसङ्ख्यं ब्राह्मणेभ्यो ददौ तदा || १-१४-५१

Then, with a composed mind, he gave one crore of Jambunada gold to the other Brahmanas who had come there.

## Verse 52

दरिद्राय द्विजायाथ हस्ताभरणमुत्तमम् |

कस्मैचिद्याचमानाय ददौ राघवनन्दनः || १-१४-५२

Then the son of the Raghu lineage gave an excellent hand ornament to a poor Brahmana who was asking for something.

## Verse 53

ततः प्रीतेषु नृपतिर्द्विजेषु द्विजवत्सलः |

प्रणाममकरोत्तेषां हर्षपर्याकुलेक्षणः || १-१४-५३

Then, when the Brahmanas were pleased, the king, devoted to the Brahmanas, bowed before them, his eyes filled with joy.

## Verse 54

तस्याशिषो ऽथ विधिवद्ब्राह्मणैः समुदाहृताः |

उदारस्य नृवीरस्य धरण्यां प्रणतस्य च || १-१४-५४

Then the Brahmanas properly pronounced various blessings upon that generous and heroic king who had bowed down upon the ground.

## Verse 55

ततः प्रीतमना राजा प्राप्य यज्ञमनुत्तमम् |

पापापहं स्वर्नयनं दुष्करं पार्थिवर्षभैः || १-१४-५५

Then the king became joyful after attaining that excellent sacrifice, which removes sin, leads to heaven, and is difficult for the foremost of kings to perform.

## Verse 56

ततो ऽब्रवीदृश्यशृङ्गं राजा दशरथस्तदा |

कुलस्य वर्द्धनं त्वं तु कर्तुमर्हसि सुव्रत || १-१४-५६

Then King Dasharatha said to Rishyasringa, "O you of excellent vows, you should perform the rite for the increase of my lineage."

## Verse 57

तथेति च स राजानमुवाच द्विजसत्तमः |

भविष्यन्ति सुता राजंश्चत्वारस्ते कुलोद्वहाः || १-१४-५७

The best of Brahmanas, Rishyasringa, said to the king, "So be it. O King, four sons who will uphold your lineage will be born to you."
