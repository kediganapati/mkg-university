## Verse 1

ततः संतप्तहृदयः स्मरन्निग्रहमात्मनः ।

विनिःश्वस्य विनिःश्वस्य कृतवैरः महात्मना ॥

Then Vishvamitra, remembering his humiliation by the great-souled Vasistha, became distressed at heart and repeatedly sighed, filled with resentment.

## Verse 2

स दक्षिणां दिशं गत्वा महिष्या सह राघव ।

तताप परमं घोरं विश्वामित्रो महातपाः ॥

O Raghava, that great ascetic Vishvamitra went to the southern direction with his chief queen and performed extremely severe austerities.

## Verse 3

फलमूलाशनो दान्तश्चचार महतं तपः ।

अथास्य जज्ञिरे पुत्राः सत्यधर्मपरायणाः ॥

Living on fruits and roots and controlling his senses, he performed great austerities. Then sons devoted to truth and righteousness were born to him.

## Verse 4

हविष्पन्दो मधुष्पन्दो दृढनेत्रो महारथः ।

पूर्णे वर्षसहस्रे तु ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ॥

His sons were Havishpanda, Madhushpanda, Dridhanetra and Maharatha. When a thousand years had been completed, Brahma, the grandsire of the worlds, appeared.

## Verse 5

अब्रवीन्मधुरं वाक्यं विश्वामित्रं तपोधनम् ।

जिता राजर्षिलोकास्ते तपसा कुशिकात्मज ॥

Brahma, seeing Vishvamitra, whose wealth was austerity, spoke sweet words: “O son of Kushika, through your austerities you have attained the worlds of the royal sages.”

## Verse 6

अनेन तपसा त्वां हि राजर्षिरिति विद्महे ।

एवमुक्त्वा महातेजा जगाम सह दैवतैः ॥

त्रिविष्टपं ब्रह्मलोकं लोकानां परमेश्वरः ॥

“We recognize you through this austerity as a Rajarshi.” Having spoken thus, the greatly radiant Brahma, the supreme Lord of the worlds, departed with the gods to the heavenly and Brahma worlds.

## Verse 7

विश्वामित्रोऽपि तच्छ्रुत्वा ह्रिया किंचिदवाङ्मुखः ।

दुःखेन महताविष्टः स मन्युरिदमब्रवीत् ॥

Hearing this, Vishvamitra became somewhat downcast with shame. Overcome by great sorrow and anger, he spoke these words.

## Verse 8

तपश्च सुमहत् तप्तं राजर्षिरिति मां विदुः ।

देवाः सर्षिगणाः सर्वे नास्ति मन्ये तपःफलम् ॥

“I have performed extremely great austerities, yet all the gods and sages still regard me only as a Rajarshi. I therefore think that my austerities have borne no fruit.”

## Verse 9

एवं निश्चित्य मनसा भूय एव महातपाः ।

तपश्चचार काकुत्स्थ परमं परमात्मवान् ॥

O Kakutstha, having firmly resolved this in his mind, the great ascetic Vishvamitra again undertook supreme austerities with great determination.

## Verse 10

एतस्मिन्नेव काले तु सत्यवादी जितेन्द्रियः ।

त्रिशङ्कुरिति विख्यात इक्ष्वाकुकुलवर्धनः ॥

At this very time there was a truthful king named Trishanku, renowned as an enhancer of the Ikshvaku dynasty and one who had conquered his senses.

## Verse 11

तस्य बुद्धिः समुत्पन्ना यजेयमिति राघव ।

गच्छेयं स्वशरीरेण देवानां परमां गतिम् ॥

O Raghava, an idea arose in his mind: “I shall perform a sacrifice by which I may go with my own body to the highest abode of the gods.”

## Verse 12

वसिष्ठं स समाहूय कथयामास चिन्तितम् ।

अशक्यमिति चाप्युक्तो वसिष्ठेन महात्मना ॥

He summoned Vasistha and told him what he had conceived. But the great-souled Vasistha told him that it was impossible.

## Verse 13

प्रत्याख्यातो वसिष्ठेन स ययौ दक्षिणां दिशम् ।

ततः तत्कर्मसिद्ध्यर्थं पुत्रांस्तस्य गतो नृपः ॥

Rejected by Vasistha, the king went to the southern direction. Then, for the purpose of accomplishing that undertaking, he went to the sons of Vasistha.

## Verse 14

वासिष्ठा दीर्घतपसस्तपो यत्र हि तेपिरे ।

त्रिशङ्कुः सुमहातेजाः शतम् परमभास्वरम् ॥

Trishanku, the greatly radiant king, went to the place where the sons of Vasistha were performing prolonged austerities.

## Verse 15

वसिष्ठपुत्रान् ददृशे तप्यमानान् यशस्विनः ।

He saw the renowned sons of Vasistha engaged in austerities.

## Verse 16

सोऽभिगम्य महात्मानः सर्वानेव गुरोः सुतान् ।

अभिवाद्यानुपूर्व्येण ह्रिया किंचिदवाङ्मुखः ॥

Trishanku approached all those great-souled sons of his teacher and respectfully greeted them according to seniority, somewhat downcast with embarrassment.

## Verse 17

अब्रवीत् स महात्मानः सर्वानेव कृताञ्जलिः ।

शरणं वः प्रपद्येऽहं शरण्यान् शरणागतः ॥

He spoke to all those great-souled sages with folded hands: “I seek refuge in you. I have come to you who are protectors of those seeking refuge.”

## Verse 18

प्रत्याख्यातोऽस्मि भद्रं वो वसिष्ठेन महात्मना ।

यष्टुकामो महायज्ञं तदनुज्ञातुमर्हथ ॥

“May you be blessed. The great-souled Vasistha has rejected me. I desire to perform a great sacrifice, and you should grant me permission to undertake it.”

## Verse 19

गुरुपुत्रानहं सर्वान् नमस्कृत्य प्रसादये ।

शिरसा प्रणतो याचे ब्राह्मणांस्तपसि स्थितान् ॥

“I bow to all the sons of my teacher and seek your favour. With my head bowed, I request you, O Brahmins established in austerity.”

## Verse 20

ते मां भवन्तः सिद्ध्यर्थं याजयन्तु समाहिताः ।

सशरीरो यथाहं वै देवलोकमवाप्नुयाम् ॥

“Please, all of you, with concentrated minds, perform the sacrifice for my purpose, so that I may attain the world of the gods with my physical body.”

## Verse 21

प्रत्याख्यातो वसिष्ठेन गतिमन्यां तपोधनाः ।

गुरुपुत्रानृते सर्वान्नाहं पश्यामि काञ्चन ॥

“O Brahmins whose wealth is austerity, having been rejected by Vasistha, I do not see any other means except you, the sons of my teacher.”

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे सप्तपञ्चाशः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the fifty-seventh Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
