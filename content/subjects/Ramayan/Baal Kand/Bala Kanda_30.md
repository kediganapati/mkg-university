## Verse 1

अथ तौ देशकालज्ञौ राजपुत्रौ अरिंदमौ |
देशे काले च वाक्यज्ञौ अब्रूताम् कौशिकम् वचः ||

Then the two princes, Rama and Lakshmana, destroyers of enemies and knowing the proper place and time for action, spoke these words to Vishvamitra.

## Verse 2

भगवन् श्रोतुम् इच्छावो यस्मिन् काले निशाचरौ |
संरक्षणीयौ तौ ब्रूहि न अतिवर्तेत तत् क्षणम् ||

"O Bhagavan, we wish to know at what time those night-roaming Rakshasas will come, so that we may protect the sacrifice. Tell us, so that the appointed moment does not pass."

## Verse 3

एवम् ब्रुवाणौ काकुत्स्थौ त्वरमाणौ युयुत्सया |
सर्वे ते मुनयः प्रीताः प्रशशंसुर् नृपात्मजौ ||

Thus speaking and eager for battle, the two Kakutsthas were praised with delight by all the sages present there.

## Verse 4

अद्य प्रभृति षट् रात्रम् रक्षतम् राघवौ युवाम् |
दीक्षाम् गतो हि येष मुनिर् मौनित्वम् च गमिष्यति ||

"O Raghavas, from today you two must protect this sacrifice for six nights. This sage has undertaken the vow and will observe silence."

## Verse 5

तौ तु तद् वचनम् श्रुत्वा राजपुत्रौ यशस्विनौ |
अनिद्रौ षट् अहोरात्रम् तपोवनम् अरक्षताम् ||

Hearing those words, the two illustrious princes protected the sacred forest of the sacrifice for six days and nights without sleep.

## Verse 6

उपासाम् चक्रतुर् वीरौ यत्तौ परम धन्विनौ |
ररक्षतुर् मुनिवरम् विश्वामित्रम् अरिंदमौ ||

The two heroic and mighty bowmen, destroyers of enemies, remained alert and protected the foremost sage, Vishvamitra.

## Verse 7

अथ काले गते तस्मिन् षष्ठे अहनि तदा आगते |
सौमित्रम् अब्रवीद् रामो यत्तो भव समाहितः ||

When that period had passed and the sixth day arrived, Rama said to Saumitra, "Be alert and attentive."

## Verse 8

रामस्य एवम् ब्रुवाणस्य त्वरितस्य युयुत्सया |
प्रजज्वाल ततो वेदिः स उपाध्याय पुरोहिता ||

While Rama was speaking thus and hastening for battle, the sacrificial altar suddenly blazed brightly together with the teacher and the priests.

## Verse 9

स दर्भ चमस स्रुक्का स समित् कुसुमोच्चया |
विश्वामित्रेण सहिता वेदिः जज्वाल स ऋत्विजा ||

The altar, furnished with sacred Darbha grass, vessels, offering spoons, sacrificial wood and heaps of flowers, blazed brightly together with Vishvamitra and the Ritviks.

## Verse 10

मंत्रवत् च यथा न्यायम् यज्ञो असौ संप्रवर्तते |
आकाशे च महान् शब्दः प्रादुर् आसीत् भयानकः ||

As that sacrifice proceeded properly according to the prescribed mantras and rites, a great and terrifying sound arose in the sky.

## Verse 11

आवार्य गगनम् मेघो यथा प्रावृषि दृश्यते |
तथा मायाम् विकुर्वाणौ राक्षसौ अभ्यधावताम् ||

As a great cloud covers the sky during the rainy season, so the two Rakshasas appeared, employing their magical powers, and rushed toward the sacrifice.

## Verse 12

मारीचः च सुबाहुः च तयोर् अनुचराः तथा |
आगंय भीम संकाशा रुधिर ओघान् अवासृजन् ||

Maricha and Subahu, together with their followers, terrible in appearance, arrived and began pouring down streams of blood.

## Verse 13

ताम् तेन रुधिर ओघेण वेदीम् वीक्ष्य समुक्षिताम् |
सहसा अभिद्रुतो रामः तान् अपश्यत् ततो दिवि ||

Seeing the sacrificial altar drenched by those streams of blood, Rama immediately rushed forward and saw them in the sky.

## Verse 14

तौ आपतन्तौ सहसा दृष्ट्वा राजीव लोचनः |
लक्ष्मणम् तौ अभिसंप्रेक्ष्य रामो वचनम् अब्रवीत् ||

Seeing those two rushing down suddenly, the lotus-eyed Rama looked toward Lakshmana and spoke.

## Verse 15

पश्य लक्ष्मण दुर्वृत्तान् राक्षसान् पिशित अशनान् |
मानवास्त्र समाधूतान् अनिलेन यथा घनान् ||

"Look, Lakshmana, at these wicked Rakshasas, eaters of flesh. I shall drive them away with the Manava weapon like the wind drives away clouds."

## Verse 16

करिष्यामि न संदेहो न उत्सहे हन्तुम् ईदृशान् |
इति उक्त्वा वचनम् रामः चापे संधाय वेगवान् ||

"I shall do this; there is no doubt. I do not wish to kill such beings." Having spoken thus, the swift Rama fitted the weapon to his bow.

## Verse 17

मानवम् परम उदारम् अस्त्रम् परम भास्वरम् |
चिक्षेप परम क्रुद्धो मारीच उरसि राघवः ||

The greatly enraged Raghava released the exceedingly powerful and radiant Manava weapon at Maricha's chest.

## Verse 18

स तेन परमास्त्रेण मानवेन समाहितः |
संपूर्णम् योजन शतम् क्षिप्तः सागर संप्लवे ||

Struck by that supreme Manava weapon, Maricha was hurled a full hundred yojanas away into the ocean.

## Verse 19

विचेतनम् विघूर्णन्तम् शीतेषु बल पीडितम् |
निरस्तम् दृश्य मारीचम् रामो लक्ष्मणम् अब्रवीत् ||

Seeing Maricha rendered unconscious, whirling about and overcome by the force of the weapon that had struck him away, Rama spoke to Lakshmana.

## Verse 20

पश्य लक्ष्मण शीतेषुम् मानवम् मनु संहितम् |
मोहयित्वा नयति एनम् न च प्राणैर् व्ययुज्यत ||

"See, Lakshmana, this Manava weapon taught by Manu has bewildered him and carried him away, yet it has not deprived him of his life."

## Verse 21

इमान् अपि वधिष्यामि निर्घृणान् दुष्ट चारिणः |
राक्षसान् पाप कर्मस्थान् यज्ञ घ्नान् रुधिर अशनान् ||

"But I shall kill these ruthless and wicked Rakshasas, perpetrators of sinful deeds, destroyers of sacrifices and drinkers of blood."

## Verse 22

इति उक्त्वा लक्ष्मणम् च अशु लाघवम् दर्शयन् इव |
संगृह्य सुमहत् च अस्त्रम् आग्नेयम् रघुनंदनः |
सुबाहु उरसि चिक्षेप स विद्धः प्रापतत् भुवि ||

Having said this to Lakshmana, the delight of the Raghu dynasty, as though displaying his swift skill, took up the exceedingly powerful Agneya weapon and hurled it at Subahu's chest. Struck by it, Subahu fell to the ground.

## Verse 23

शेषान् वायव्यम् आदाय निजघान महायशाः |
राघवः परमोदारो मुनीनाम् मुदम् आवहन् ||

The greatly illustrious and exceedingly generous Raghava took up the Vayavya weapon and destroyed the remaining Rakshasas, bringing delight to the sages.

## Verse 24

स हत्वा राक्षसान् सर्वान् यज्ञ घ्नान् रघुनंदनः |
ऋषिभिः पूजितः तत्र यथा इन्द्रो विजये पुरा ||

Having killed all the Rakshasas who were obstructing the sacrifice, Rama, the delight of the Raghu dynasty, was honored there by the sages just as Indra was honored in ancient times after his victory.

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदि काव्ये बाल काण्डे त्रिंशः सर्गः ॥

Thus ends the thirtieth Sarga of Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the first epic composed by Valmiki.
