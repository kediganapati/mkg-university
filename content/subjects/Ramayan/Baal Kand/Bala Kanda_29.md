## Verse 1

अथ तां रजनीमुष्य विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।
प्रहस्य राघवं वाक्यमुवाच मधुराक्षरम् ॥

After spending that night there, the great sage Vishvamitra smiled and spoke sweet words to Raghava.

## Verse 2

पश्य राम महाभागां रम्यां मलयपर्वताम् ।
एषा सा गिरिशार्दूल पुण्या मलयपर्वता ॥

"O Rama, greatly blessed one, behold this beautiful mountain. This is the sacred Malaya mountain, O tiger among mountains."

## Verse 3

ततः प्रभाते विमले कृताह्निकमुपागतम् ।
विश्वामित्रं महात्मानं राघवः प्रत्यभाषत ॥

Then, at the clear dawn, after completing his morning duties, Raghava spoke to the great-souled Vishvamitra.

## Verse 4

भगवन् किं नु देशोऽयं कस्य वा मुनिपुङ्गव ।
कथं चात्रागमिष्यामो निवासश्च कथं भवेत् ॥

"O Bhagavan, O foremost of sages, what is this region and to whom does it belong? How shall we proceed here, and how shall we stay here?"

## Verse 5

तमुवाच महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।
शृणु राम महाबाहो यथैतद् वनमाश्रितम् ॥

The greatly radiant great sage Vishvamitra said to him, "Listen, O mighty-armed Rama, to how this forest came to be inhabited."

## Verse 6

अयं देशः पुरा राम महर्षीणां तपस्विनाम् ।
आश्रमैः शोभितो नित्यं पुण्यैः पुण्यजनावृतः ॥

"O Rama, this region was formerly adorned with the hermitages of great sages performing austerities and was always inhabited by holy people."

## Verse 7

तत्र ते मुनयः सर्वे तपोबलसमन्विताः ।
अग्निहोत्रपराः सर्वे वेदाध्ययनतत्पराः ॥

"There all the sages were endowed with the power of austerity. They were devoted to Agnihotra and deeply engaged in the study of the Vedas."

## Verse 8

तेषां तपस्यतां तत्र देवदानवमानुषाः ।
आगच्छन्ति महाभागा दर्शनार्थं नरोत्तम ॥

"O best of men, gods, Danavas and human beings used to come there while those greatly blessed sages were performing austerities, wishing to see them."

## Verse 9

अथ काले महाबाहो तेषां धर्मे व्यवस्थिते ।
अभवद् भयं महद् राम राक्षसेभ्योऽतिदारुणम् ॥

"Then, O mighty-armed Rama, while those sages were firmly established in righteousness, a great and terrible fear arose among them because of the Rakshasas."

## Verse 10

ते राक्षसगणाः सर्वे मुनिभिर्विनिवारिताः ।
नाशं ययुर्महाभाग तपसा तेषु रक्षसाम् ॥

"Those hosts of Rakshasas were driven away by the sages, and many of the Rakshasas were destroyed through the power of the sages' austerities."

## Verse 11

ततो देवाः समागम्य ऋषयश्च तपोधनाः ।
विश्वामित्रमुपागम्य वचनं प्रत्यभाषिरे ॥

Then the gods and sages rich in austerity assembled and approached Vishvamitra and spoke to him.

## Verse 12

भगवन् राक्षसैर्घोरैस्ताप्यमानान् तपस्विनः ।
त्रातुमर्हसि काकुत्स्थं रामं सत्यपराक्रमम् ॥

"O Bhagavan, you should protect the ascetics who are being tormented by dreadful Rakshasas by bringing Rama, the truly valiant Kakutstha."

## Verse 13

स तानुवाच धर्मात्मा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।
रामं दशरथस्यैव याचिष्यामि न संशयः ॥

The righteous-souled great sage Vishvamitra said to them, "Without doubt, I shall ask Dasharatha for Rama."

## Verse 14

ततः स राजा काकुत्स्थं रामं सत्यपराक्रमम् ।
प्रेषयिष्यति धर्मात्मा मुनिकार्यार्थसिद्धये ॥

"Then the righteous king will send Rama, the truly valiant Kakutstha, for the accomplishment of the sages' purpose."

## Verse 15

ततो रामं समादाय लक्ष्मणं च महाबलम् ।
आगमिष्यामि धर्मिष्ठं मुनिकार्यार्थसिद्धये ॥

"Then I shall bring Rama and the mighty Lakshmana for the accomplishment of the sages' purpose."

## Verse 16

एवमुक्त्वा महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।
प्रययौ सहितो रामं लक्ष्मणेन च धीमता ॥

Having spoken thus, the greatly radiant great sage Vishvamitra proceeded together with Rama and the intelligent Lakshmana.

## Verse 17

तं दृष्ट्वा मुनयः सर्वे हृष्टाः परममानसाः ।
पूजयामासुरव्यग्राः प्रहृष्टेनान्तरात्मना ॥

Seeing him, all the sages became extremely delighted and, with joyful hearts, honored him without agitation.

## Verse 18

तत्र रामः स सौमित्रिर्मुनिभिः सह राघवः ।
उवास परमप्रीतो विश्वामित्रस्य शासनात् ॥

There Rama, together with Saumitra, stayed happily among the sages according to Vishvamitra's command.

## Verse 19

अथ तत्र महाभागाः सिद्धाश्रमनिवासिनः ।
रामं दृष्ट्वा महात्मानं लक्ष्मणं च महाबलम् ॥

Then the greatly blessed inhabitants of Siddhashrama, seeing the great-souled Rama and the mighty Lakshmana—

## Verse 20

हृष्टाः प्रमुदिताः सर्वे समीयुर्मुनिपुङ्गवाः ।
ऊचुश्च मधुरं वाक्यं कौशिकं मुनिसत्तमम् ॥

—became delighted and joyful. The foremost sages gathered and spoke sweet words to the excellent sage Kaushika.

## Verse 21

स्वागतं ते महाबाहो रामलक्ष्मणसंयुत ।
कृतार्थाः स्म महाभाग तवागमनकारणात् ॥

"Welcome, O mighty-armed one, together with Rama and Lakshmana. O greatly blessed one, because of your arrival we consider ourselves fulfilled."

## Verse 22

इत्युक्त्वा मुनयः सर्वे पूजयामासुरादरात् ।
रामं च लक्ष्मणं चैव कौशिकं च महामुनिम् ॥

Having spoken thus, all the sages respectfully honored Rama, Lakshmana and the great sage Kaushika.

## Verse 23

ततो रामः स सौमित्रिर्विश्वामित्रेण पालितः ।
तस्थौ तत्र महातेजाः सर्वलोकहिते रतः ॥

Then the greatly radiant Rama, together with Saumitra, remained there under the care of Vishvamitra, devoted to the welfare of all the worlds.

## Verse 24

अथ तत्र महायज्ञं यष्टुं समुपचक्रमे ।
विश्वामित्रो महातेजा ऋषिभिः सहितः प्रभुः ॥

Then the greatly radiant Vishvamitra, together with the sages, began preparations to perform a great sacrifice.

## Verse 25

तस्य यज्ञस्य विघ्नाय राक्षसाः समुपागमन् ।
मारीचश्च सुबाहुश्च ह्यन्ये च बलशालिनः ॥

The Rakshasas came to obstruct that sacrifice, including Maricha, Subahu and other powerful Rakshasas.

## Verse 26

तान् दृष्ट्वा मुनयः सर्वे त्रस्ताः परमदुर्बलाः ।
विश्वामित्रं महात्मानं ऊचुर्वचनमुत्तमम् ॥

Seeing them, all the sages became terrified and helpless and spoke these excellent words to the great-souled Vishvamitra.

## Verse 27

रक्षांसि घोररूपाणि विघ्नं कुर्वन्ति वै मुने ।
यज्ञस्यास्य विनाशाय तेषां नाशं विधीयताम् ॥

"O sage, dreadful Rakshasas are creating obstacles to this sacrifice. Their destruction should be accomplished so that this sacrifice may not be ruined."

## Verse 28

ततः स मुनिशार्दूलो विश्वामित्रो महातपाः ।
रामं च लक्ष्मणं चैव समादिशदनुत्तमौ ॥

Then the tiger among sages, the great ascetic Vishvamitra, gave his command to the excellent Rama and Lakshmana.

## Verse 29

इमौ कुमारौ राजेन्द्र दशरथस्य धीमतः ।
रक्षेतां मम यज्ञं वै राक्षसेभ्यो न संशयः ॥

"These two princes, the intelligent sons of King Dasharatha, shall protect my sacrifice from the Rakshasas. Of this there is no doubt."

## Verse 30

तयोस्तु वचनं श्रुत्वा मुनिवर्यस्य धीमतः ।
रामलक्ष्मणौ महात्मानौ तत्र तस्थतुरेव हि ॥

Hearing the words of the wise and excellent sage, the great-souled Rama and Lakshmana remained there ready to fulfill his command.

## Verse 31

विश्वामित्रवचः श्रुत्वा तु हृष्टौ दशरथात्मजौ ।
मुनिं प्रदक्षिणं कृत्वा यज्ञरक्षामुपागतौ ॥

Hearing Vishvamitra's words, the two sons of Dasharatha became delighted, circumambulated the sage, and took up the protection of the sacrifice.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे एकोनत्रिंशः सर्गः ॥

Thus ends the twenty-ninth Sarga of Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the first epic composed by Sage Valmiki.
