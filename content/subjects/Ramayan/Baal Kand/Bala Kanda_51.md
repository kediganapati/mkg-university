## Verse 1

जनकस्य वचः श्रुत्वा शतानन्दो महामुनिः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं विश्वामित्रं महामुनिम् ॥

Hearing the words of Janaka, the great sage Shatananda spoke sweetly to the great sage Vishvamitra.

## Verse 2

स्वागतं ते महामुने विश्वामित्र महायशः ।

कुशलं ते महाभाग कच्चित्ते कुशलं गृहे ॥

“O great sage Vishvamitra, greatly renowned one, welcome to you. O greatly fortunate one, I hope you are well and that everything is well in your hermitage.”

## Verse 3

महातेजा महाप्राज्ञः कौशिकः कुशलं वदन् ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं शतानन्दं महामुनिम् ॥

The greatly radiant and highly wise Kaushika spoke sweetly to the great sage Shatananda, enquiring about his welfare.

## Verse 4

कुशलं ते महाभाग शतानन्द तपोधन ।

कच्चिदाश्रमवासे ते नित्यं कुशलमस्ति हि ॥

“O greatly fortunate Shatananda, whose wealth is austerity, I hope you are well. I hope everything is always well in your life at the hermitage.”

## Verse 5

ततः शतानन्दो रामं दृष्ट्वा राजीवलोचनम् ।

पप्रच्छ कुशलं वीरं कौसल्यायाः प्रियं सुतम् ॥

Then Shatananda saw Rama, the lotus-eyed hero and beloved son of Kausalya, and enquired about his welfare.

## Verse 6

कच्चिद्दशरथस्य त्वं राम पुत्रो महात्मनः ।

कच्चिद्विश्वामित्रस्य त्वं शिष्यः कुशलवर्त्मनः ॥

“O Rama, are you indeed the son of the great-souled Dasharatha? Are you the disciple of Vishvamitra, who follows the righteous path?”

## Verse 7

कच्चित्ते कुशलं राम लक्ष्मणेन सहानघ ।

कच्चिदस्ति तपोवृद्धिः कौशिकस्य महात्मनः ॥

“O sinless Rama, are you well together with Lakshmana? Is the austerity and spiritual power of the great-souled Kaushika increasing?”

## Verse 8

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा शतानन्दस्य राघवः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं शतानन्दं महामुनिम् ॥

Hearing the words of Shatananda, Rama spoke sweetly to the great sage Shatananda.

## Verse 9

कुशलं मे महाभाग सर्वं च कुशलं गुरोः ।

तवापि कुशलं ब्रह्मन् पितुश्चैव महात्मनः ॥

“O greatly fortunate one, I am well, and everything is well with my teacher. O Brahman, I hope you too are well, as is my great-souled father.”

## Verse 10

ततः शतानन्दो रामं विश्वामित्रस्य धीमतः ।

कथयामास विस्तीर्णं चरितं परमाद्भुतम् ॥

Then Shatananda narrated to Rama the extraordinary and extensive story of the wise Vishvamitra.

## Verse 11

महर्षेस्तस्य काकुत्स्थ कौशिकस्य महात्मनः ।

यथावृत्तं पुरावृत्तं कथयामास सादरम् ॥

O Kakutstha, he respectfully narrated the ancient history of the great sage Kaushika exactly as it had happened.

## Verse 12

राजा किल महातेजाः प्रजापालनतत्परः ।

कुशस्य पुत्रो धर्मात्मा कुशनाभ इति स्मृतः ॥

There was once a greatly radiant king, devoted to protecting his subjects. He was the righteous son of Kusha and was known as Kushanabha.

## Verse 13

कुशनाभस्य धर्मात्मा पुत्रोऽभूद्गाधिनामकः ।

गाधेः पुत्रो महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ॥

The righteous Kushanabha had a son named Gadhi. The greatly radiant Vishvamitra, the great sage, was the son of Gadhi.

## Verse 14

राजा विश्वामित्रो नाम प्रजाः सम्यगुपालयन् ।

स कदाचिन्महातेजा वसिष्ठस्याश्रमं ययौ ॥

King Vishvamitra properly protected his subjects. Once, that greatly radiant king went to the hermitage of Vasistha.

## Verse 15

तत्र दृष्ट्वा मुनिश्रेष्ठं वसिष्ठं तपसि स्थितम् ।

पूजयामास धर्मात्मा विधिवत्सुसमाहितः ॥

There he saw the foremost of sages, Vasistha, engaged in austerities. The righteous king respectfully worshipped him according to the proper rites.

## Verse 16

तं दृष्ट्वा मुनिशार्दूलं विश्वामित्रं महामुनिः ।

वसिष्ठः परमप्रीतोऽभ्यागतं प्रत्यपूजयत् ॥

Seeing Vishvamitra, the tiger among sages, the great sage Vasistha became exceedingly pleased and properly honoured his guest.

## Verse 17

अर्घ्यं पाद्यं च गां चैव मधुपर्कं तथैव च ।

निवेदयामास विधिवद्राज्ञे परमधार्मिकः ॥

The highly righteous Vasistha duly offered the king arghya, water for washing his feet, a cow and madhuparka.

## Verse 18

प्रतिगृह्य तु तां पूजां विश्वामित्रो महाबलः ।

कृतार्थोऽभून्महातेजा वसिष्ठेन महात्मना ॥

Having accepted that honour, the mighty and greatly radiant Vishvamitra felt fulfilled by the hospitality of the great-souled Vasistha.

## Verse 19

ततः कुशलमव्यग्रं वसिष्ठो मुनिपुङ्गवः ।

पप्रच्छ मधुरं वाक्यं विश्वामित्रं महामुनिम् ॥

Then Vasistha, the foremost of sages, calmly enquired about the welfare of the great sage Vishvamitra in sweet words.

## Verse 20

कच्चित्ते कुशलं राजन् राज्ये च बले च ते ।

मित्रेषु च सुहृच्चैव कच्चित्सुखमवाप्स्यसि ॥

“O king, I hope you are well, as are your kingdom and army. I hope your friends and well-wishers are also well and that you are living happily.”

## Verse 21

कच्चित्ते कुशलं राम सर्वत्र च नरेश्वर ।

कच्चिद्भृत्याः प्रसन्नास्ते कच्चित्तेऽस्ति धनागमः ॥

“O Rama, I hope everything is well with you everywhere, O lord of men. I hope your servants are content and that wealth is coming to you.”

## Verse 22

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं वसिष्ठं मुनिसत्तमम् ॥

Hearing those words, the great sage Vishvamitra spoke sweetly to the excellent sage Vasistha.

## Verse 23

कुशलं मे महाभाग सर्वत्र कुशलं मम ।

राज्यं च कुशलं सर्वं बलं च कुशलं तथा ॥

“O greatly fortunate one, I am well, and everything is well with me. My kingdom is prosperous, and my army too is in good condition.”

## Verse 24

मित्राणि च सुहृच्चैव भृत्याश्च कुशलाः सदा ।

धनागमश्च मे ब्रह्मन् कुशलं च यथातथम् ॥

“My friends and well-wishers are well, and my servants are also always well. O Brahman, wealth is coming to me, and everything is as it should be.”

## Verse 25

एवमुक्त्वा महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

वसिष्ठं मुनिशार्दूलं पुनरेवाभ्यभाषत ॥

Having spoken thus, the greatly radiant great sage Vishvamitra again addressed Vasistha, the tiger among sages.

## Verse 26

इमौ कुमारौ भद्रं ते देवतुल्यपराक्रमौ ।

रामो दशरथस्यायं लक्ष्मणश्चापि वीर्यवान् ॥

“These two princes are of divine-like prowess. May you be blessed. This is Rama, the son of Dasharatha, and this is the mighty Lakshmana.”

## Verse 27

इमौ मुनिवरश्रेष्ठं मां शुश्रूषापरायणौ ।

आगतौ सहिता वीरौ यज्ञस्य परिरक्षणे ॥

“These two heroic princes have come with me, devoted to serving me and protecting my sacrifice.”

## Verse 28

अहं वेद्मि महात्मानौ रामलक्ष्मणयोर्बलम् ।

न वेत्ति हि भवान् सर्वं तयोर्वीर्यं महामुने ॥

“O great sage, I know the strength of these two great-souled princes, Rama and Lakshmana. You do not yet know all the extent of their valour.”

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे एकपञ्चाशः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the fifty-first Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
