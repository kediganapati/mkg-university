## Verse 1

तस्य अमात्या गुणैर् आसन् इक्ष्ह्वकोस्तु महात्मनः |
मंत्रज्ञाः च इङ्गितज्ञाः च नित्यम् प्रिय हिते रताः || १-७-१

The ministers of that great descendant of the Ikshvaku dynasty were endowed with noble qualities. They were skilled in counsel, understood intentions and gestures, and were always devoted to what was beneficial and pleasing to the king.

## Verse 2

अष्टौ बभूवुः वीरस्य तस्य अमात्या यशस्विनः |
शुचयः च अनुरक्ताः च राजकृत्येषु नित्यशः || १-७-२

The glorious and valiant king had eight ministers. They were pure in conduct, loyal, and constantly devoted to the affairs of the kingdom.

## Verse 3

धृष्टिर् जयन्तो विजयो सुराष्ट्रो राष्ट्र वर्धनः |
अकोपो धर्मपालः च सुमंत्रः च अष्टमो अर्थवित् || १-७-३

Dhrishti, Jayanta, Vijaya, Surashtra, Rashtravardhana, Akopa, Dharmapala, and Sumantra, the eighth and a knower of affairs, were his ministers.

## Verse 4

ऋत्विजौ द्वौ अभिमतौ तस्याः ताम् ऋषि सत्तमौ |
वशिष्ठो वामदेवः च मंत्रिणः च तथा अपरे || १-७-४

The two eminent sages, Vasishtha and Vamadeva, were his respected priests and counsellors, together with other ministers.

## Verses 5–6

सुयज्ञो अपि अथ जाबालिः काशय्पो अपि अथ गौतमः |
मार्कण्डेयः तु दीर्घायुः तथा कात्यायनो द्विजः ||
एतैः ब्रह्मर्षिभिर् नित्यम् ऋत्विजः तस्य पौर्वकाः |

Suyajna, Jabali, Kashyapa, Gautama, Markandeya, Dirghayu, and the Brahmana Katyayana were also present. Together with these Brahmarshis, they served as priests according to the traditions of his royal house.

## Verses 6–8

विद्या विनीता ह्रीमंतः कुशला नियतेन्द्रियाः ||
श्रीमन्तः च महात्मनः शास्त्रज्ञा धृढ विक्रमाः |
कीर्तिमन्तः प्रणिहिता यथा वचन कारिणः ||
तेजः क्षमा यशः प्राप्ताः स्मित पूर्व अभिभाषिणः |

They were educated and disciplined, modest, skilful, and self-controlled. They were prosperous and noble, knowledgeable in the sciences, firm in courage, renowned, composed, and true to their word. They possessed brilliance, patience, and fame, and spoke with a smile.

## Verses 8–9

क्रोधात् काम अर्थ हेतोर् वा न ब्रूयुर् अनृतम् वचः ||
तेषाम् अविदितम् किंचत् श्वेषु नास्ति परेषु वा |
क्रियमाणम् कृतम् वा अपि चारेण अपि चिकीर्षितम् || १-७-९

Whether from anger, desire, or the pursuit of wealth, they never spoke falsehood. Nothing occurring in their own land or in other lands—whether already done, being done, or intended to be done—remained unknown to them through their intelligence network.

## Verse 10

कुशला व्य्वहारेषु सौहृदेषु परीक्षिताः |
प्राप्त कालम् यथा दण्डम् धारयेयुः सुतेषु अपि || १-७-१०

They were skilled in administration and tested in friendship. When the proper occasion required it, they would administer punishment even to their own sons.

## Verse 11

कोश संग्रहणे युक्ता बलस्य च परिग्रहे |
अहितम् च अपि पुरुषम् न हिंस्युर् अविदूषकम् || १-७-११

They were diligent in managing the treasury and maintaining the armed forces. Even an unfriendly person was not harmed unless he had committed an offence.

## Verse 12

वीराः च नियतोत्साहा राज शास्त्रम् अनुष्ठिताः |
शुचीनाम् रक्षितारः च नित्यम् विषय वासिनाम् || १-७-१२

They were brave, consistently energetic, knowledgeable in the science of government, pure in conduct, and constant protectors of the people living in the kingdom.

## Verse 13

ब्रह्म क्षत्रम् अहिंसन्तः ते कोशम् समपूरयन् |
सुतीक्ष्ण दण्डाः संप्रेक्ष्य पुरुषस्य बलाबलम् || १-७-१३

They did not oppress the Brahmanas or Kshatriyas in order to fill the treasury. When punishment was necessary, they considered the strength and circumstances of the individual.

## Verse 14

शुचीनाम् एक बुद्धीनाम् सर्वेषाम् संप्रजानताम् |
न आसीत् पुरे वा राष्ट्रे वा मृषा वादी नरः क्वचित् || १-७-१४

Among those pure and united administrators, there was nowhere, either in the city or throughout the kingdom, a man who spoke falsely.

## Verse 15

कश्चिन् न दुष्टः तत्र आसीत् पर दार रतिर् नरः |
प्रशांतम् सर्वम् एव असीत् राष्ट्रम् पुरवरम् च तत् || १-७-१५

There was no wicked man who desired another person's wife. The entire kingdom and its excellent capital remained peaceful.

## Verse 16

सु वासस सु वेषाः च ते च सर्वे शुचिव्रताः |
हितार्थः च नरेन्द्रस्य जाग्रतो नय चक्षुषा || १-७-१६

All were well dressed and well adorned, observing pure conduct. With watchful eyes guided by proper policy, they remained devoted to the welfare of the king.

## Verse 17

गुरोर् गुण गृहीताः च प्रख्याताः च पराक्रमे |
विदेशेषु अपि विज्ञाता सर्वतो बुद्धि निश्चयाः || १-७-१७

They had acquired good qualities from their teachers and were renowned for their ability. Even in foreign lands, they were known for their sound judgment and intelligence.

## Verse 18

अभितो गुणवन्तः च न च आसन् गुण वर्जिताः |
सन्धि विग्रह तत्वज्ञाः प्रकृत्या संपदान्विताः || १-७-१८

They were endowed with virtues in every way, and none lacked good qualities. They understood the principles of peace and conflict and were naturally prosperous.

## Verse 19

मंत्र संवरणे शक्ताः शक्ताः सूक्ष्मासु बुद्धिषु |
नीति शास्त्र विशेषज्ञाः सततम् प्रिय वादिनः || १-७-१९

They were capable of keeping counsel confidential, skilled in subtle matters, experts in the science of governance, and always spoke pleasantly.

## Verse 20

ईदृशैः तैः अमात्यैः च राजा दशरथोऽनघः |
उपपन्नो गुणोपेतैः अन्वशासद् वसुंधराम् || १-७-२०

Surrounded by such virtuous and capable ministers, King Dasharatha ruled the earth.

## Verses 21–22

अवेक्षमाणः चारेण प्रजा धर्मेण रक्षयन् |
प्रजानाम् पालनम् कुर्वन् अधर्मम् परिवर्जयन् ||
विश्रुतः त्रिषु लोकेषु वदान्यः सत्य संगरः |
स तत्र पुरुषव्याघ्रः शशास पृथ्वीम् इमाम् || १-७-२२

Observing affairs through his agents, protecting the people according to Dharma, governing his subjects, and avoiding unrighteousness, Dasharatha became renowned in the three worlds. Generous and firmly devoted to truth, that foremost among men ruled the earth.

## Verse 23

न अध्यगच्छत् विशिष्टम् वा तुल्यम् वा शत्रुम् आत्मनः |
मित्रवान् नत सामन्तः प्रताप हत कण्टकः |
स शशास जगत् राजा दिवि देव पतिर् यथा || १-७-२३

He found no enemy superior or equal to himself. He had many allies, his subordinate rulers were obedient, and through his valour he had removed all threats. Thus the king ruled the world as Indra rules in heaven.

## Verse 24

तैः मंत्रिभिः मंत्र हितेः निविष्टैः
वृतोऽनुरक्तैः कुशलैः समर्थैः |
स पार्थिवो दीप्तिम् अवाप युक्तः
तेजोमयैः गोभिः इव उदितः अर्कः || १-७-२४

Surrounded by those loyal, skilful, capable ministers devoted to wise counsel and welfare, the king attained great splendour, like the rising sun accompanied by its radiant rays.
