---
title: "Kishkindha Kand"
---


All sarga of the Kishkindha Kand are available here.

Interpretations of some shlokas are still pending because I have not yet gained sufficient personal experience to interpret them. As my experience and understanding grow, I will continue adding interpretations until all sarga are covered.

Numbering of shlokas in each sarga is not my own — it comes from available sources. I do not agree with or support every shloka as absolute reality; some shlokas may have been included to hide the truth, and some may have been added to divert from it. So I am only giving the interpretation and meaning of the shlokas, not writing a new Bala Kand.
