*Deva Vandito Tuj*  
*Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya*

*Sang Kalki An Ganesha,*  
*Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya*

Vinamra Siddha Anima,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya  
Kaam Bhogi Kaamarupi,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya...1

Karuna Siddha Mahima,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya  
Pavitra Yogi Suryarupi,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya...2

Maryada Siddha Garima,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya  
Prem Rogi Chandrarupi,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya...3

Tatpara Siddha Laghima,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya  
Sukh Dhyani Agnirupi,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya...4

*Deva Vandito Tuj*  
*Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya*

*Sang Kalki An Ganesha,*  
*Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya*

Khoj Siddha Prapti,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya  
Shant Tyagi Vayurupi,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya...5

Ichchha Siddha Prakamya,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya  
Gyan Vani Varunarupi,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya...6

Kartavya Siddha Prabhutva,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya  
Anand Haavi Indrarupi,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya...7

Prabhav Siddha Vashitva,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya  
Mukt Dhaari Dhruvarupi,  
Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya...8

*Deva Vandito Tuj*  
*Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya*

*Sang Kalki An Ganesha,*  
*Tu Maitreya Buddhaya Maha Buddhaya*
