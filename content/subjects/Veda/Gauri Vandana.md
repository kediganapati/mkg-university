*Gauri Vandito Tuj, Hathdurge Navadurge*  
*Gauri Ganapati, Riddhi Siddhi Tu Mahadurge*

He Riddhi Tu Hathdurge, He Siddhi Tu Navadurge  
He Shakti Tu Hathdurge, He Bhakti Tu Navadurge...1

Dukh Haari Tu Hathdurge, Sukh Dhaari Tu Navadurge  
Hath Haari Tu Hathdurge, Nat Dhaari Tu Navadurge...2

Dasvidye Tu Hathdurge, Navarupe Tu Navadurge  
Atarangi Tu Hathdurge, Navarangi Tu Navadurge...3

Mahakali Tu Hathdurge, Shailyaputri Tu Navadurge  
Maa Tara Tu Hathdurge, Brahmacharini Tu Navadurge...4

*Gauri Vandito Tuj, Hathdurge Navadurge*  
*Gauri Ganapati, Riddhi Siddhi Tu Mahadurge*

Shodasi Tu Hathdurge, Chandraghanta Tu Navadurge  
Bhuvaneshwari Tu Hathdurge, Kushmanda Tu Navadurge...5

Chhinnamasta Tu Hathdurge, Skandamata Tu Navadurge  
Bhairavi Tu Hathdurge, Katyayani Tu Navadurge...6

Dhoomavati Tu Hathdurge, Kalaratri Tu Navadurge  
Bagalamukhi Tu Hathdurge, Mahagauri Tu Navadurge...7

Matangi Tu Hathdurge, Siddhidatri Tu Navadurge  
Aai Kamala Tu Hathdurge, Aai Amba Tu Navadurge...8

*Gauri Vandito Tuj, Hathdurge Navadurge*  
*Gauri Ganapati, Riddhi Siddhi Tu Mahadurge*

**Note:** This Gauri Vandana represents one bhajan from the *Deva Vandana* book, authored by Kedi Ganapati.
