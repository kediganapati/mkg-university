title: "Tripitaka"
---

All three Pitakas — Vinaya Pitaka, Sutta Pitaka, and Abhidhamma Pitaka — are available here, covering rules of monastic conduct, discourses attributed to Bhagwan Buddha, and later systematic analysis of his teachings.

The Sutta Pitaka's five Nikayas (Digha, Majjhima, Samyutta, Anguttara, and Khuddaka Nikaya) are being added progressively. The Khuddaka Nikaya, in particular, is not accepted by MKG as fully original — parts of it were added centuries after Buddha, only being finalised at the Third Buddhist Council. The Abhidhamma Pitaka too is not treated as Buddha's direct word, but as a later scholastic framework built on the suttas; interpretation is still provided based on the text as it exists today.

Interpretations of some suttas are still pending because I have not yet gained sufficient personal experience to interpret them. As my experience and understanding grow, I will continue adding interpretations until all Pitakas are covered.

Numbering and arrangement of suttas is not my own — it comes from available sources. I do not agree with or support every sutta as absolute reality; some portions may have been added, altered, or lost across centuries of oral and written transmission, and different traditions (Theravada, Sarvastivada, and others) preserve different versions of the canon. So I am only giving the interpretation and meaning of the texts as they exist today, not writing a new Tripitaka.
