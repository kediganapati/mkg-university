*Deva Vandito Tuj, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana*  
*Sang Lakshmi, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana*

Chakra Dhaari Chakradhar, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Shankha Dhaari Shankhadhar, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Urja Dhaari Urjanath, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Path Dhaari Pathnath, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana.1

Vaikuntha Dhaari Vaikunthadhar, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Hiranya Dhaari Hiranyadhar, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Grah Dhaari Grahanath, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Pataal Dhaari Pataalnath, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana.2

Bhagya Dhaari Bhagyanath, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Punya Dhaari Punyanath, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Vasu Dhaari Vasudev, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Avataar Dhaari Avataari, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana.3

Matsya Dhaari Jal Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Kachchha Dhaari Ubhay Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Varaha Dhaari Thal Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Narad Dhaari Maanav Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana.4

Narasimha Dhaari Shankar Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Vaman Dhaari Datt Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Parashu Dhaari Parashuram Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Ram Dhaari Ram Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana.5

Krishna Dhaari Krishna Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Buddha Dhaari Buddha Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Kalki Dhaari Kalki Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana  
Ganesh Dhaari Ganesh Man, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana.6

*Deva Vandito Tuj, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana*  
*Sang Lakshmi, Tu Vishnu Tu Narayana*

**Note:** This Vishnu Vandana represents one bhajan from the *Deva Vandana* book, authored by Kedi Ganapati.
