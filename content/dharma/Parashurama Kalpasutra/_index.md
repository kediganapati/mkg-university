title: "Parashurama Kalpasutra"
---

The Parashurama Kalpasutra is presented here with all available sutras, along with the original Sanskrit text, English translation, and Kedi Ganapati's interpretation.

Interpretations of some sutras may still be pending because I have not yet gained sufficient personal experience to interpret them. As my experience and understanding grow, I will continue adding interpretations until the complete text is covered.

Numbering and arrangement of the sutras are not my own — they come from available sources. I am only presenting the original text, its translation, and my interpretation, not writing a new Parashurama Kalpasutra.
