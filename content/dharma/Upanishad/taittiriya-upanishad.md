<a id="shloka1"></a>

**Shloka 1:1**

*शं नो मित्रः शं वरुणः ।*
*शं नो भवत्वर्यमा ।*
*शं न इन्द्रो बृहस्पतिः ।*
*शं नो विष्णुरुरुक्रमः ।*
*नमो ब्रह्मणे ।*
*नमस्ते वायो ।*
*त्वमेव प्रत्यक्षं ब्रह्मासि ।*
*त्वामेव प्रत्यक्षं ब्रह्म वदिष्यामि ।*
*ऋतं वदिष्यामि ।*
*सत्यं वदिष्यामि ।*
*तन्मामवतु ।*
*तद्वक्तारमवतु ।*
*अवतु माम् ।*
*अवतु वक्तारम् ॥*
*शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥*

*Sham no Mitrah sham Varunah*
*Sham no bhavatv Aryama*
*Sham na Indro Brihaspatih*
*Sham no Vishnur Urukramah*
*Namo Brahmane*
*Namaste Vayo*
*Tvam eva pratyaksham Brahmasi*
*Tvam eva pratyaksham Brahma vadishyami*
*Ritam vadishyami*
*Satyam vadishyami*
*Tan mam avatu*
*Tad vaktaram avatu*
*Avatu mam*
*Avatu vaktaram*
*Shantih Shantih Shantih*

**Translation**

May Mitra, Varuna, Aryaman, Indra, Brihaspati, and Vishnu bless us with peace. Salutations to Brahman. Salutations to Vayu, for You are the directly perceptible Brahman. I shall proclaim the Cosmic Truth and speak only the truth. May Brahman protect both me and my teacher. Peace, Peace, Peace.

<a id="shloka2"></a>

**Shloka 1:2**

*शिक्षां व्याख्यास्यामः ।*
*वर्णः स्वरः ।*
*मात्रा बलम् ।*
*साम सन्तानः ।*
*इत्युक्तः शिक्षाध्यायः ॥*

*Shiksham vyakhyasyamah*
*Varnah svarah*
*Matra balam*
*Sama santanah*
*Ity uktah shikshadhyayah*

**Translation**

We shall now explain the science of phonetics. It includes letters, accent, duration, strength, harmony of recitation, and continuity. Thus begins the lesson on proper pronunciation.

<a id="shloka4"></a>

**Shloka 1:4**

*अथातः संहितायाः उपनिषदं व्याख्यास्यामः ।*
*पञ्चसु अधिकरणेषु ।*
*अधिलोकम् ।*
*अधिज्योतिषम् ।*
*अधिविद्यम् ।*
*अधिप्रजम् ।*
*अध्यात्मम् ।*
*ता महासंहिताः इत्याचक्षते ॥*

*Athatah samhitaayah upanishadam vyakhyasyamah*
*Panchasu adhikaraneshu*
*Adhilokam*
*Adhijyotisham*
*Adhividyam*
*Adhiprajam*
*Adhyatmam*
*Ta mahasamhitah ity achakshate*

**Translation**

Now we shall explain the secret teaching of the sacred combinations (Samhita). It is understood through five fields: the worlds, light, knowledge, progeny, and the Self. These are known as the Great Samhitas.

<a id="shloka5"></a>

**Shloka 1:5**

*अथाधिलोकम् ।*
*पृथिवी पूर्वरूपम् ।*
*द्यौरुत्तररूपम् ।*
*आकाशः सन्धिः ।*
*वायुः सन्धानम् ।*
*इत्यधिलोकम् ॥*

*Athadhilokam*
*Prithivi purvarupam*
*Dyauh uttararupam*
*Akashah sandhih*
*Vayuh sandhanam*
*Ity adhilokam*

**Translation**

Now, with respect to the worlds: the Earth is the first form, Heaven is the second form, Space is their meeting point, and Air is the force that unites them. This is the contemplation concerning the worlds.

**Conversation**

**Shloka 1:6**

*अथाधिज्योतिषम् ।*

*आदित्य उत्तररूपम् ।*
*आपः सन्धिः ।*
*वैद्युतः सन्धानम् ।*
*इत्यधिज्योतिषम् ॥*

*Athadhijyotisham*
*Agnih purvarupam*
*Aditya uttararupam*
*Apah sandhih*
*Vaidyutah sandhanam*
*Ity adhijyotisham*

**Translation**

Now, concerning the lights: Fire is the first form, the Sun is the second form, Water is their meeting point, and Lightning is the connecting force. This is the contemplation concerning the luminous powers.

<a id="shloka7"></a>

**Shloka 1:7**

*अथाधिविद्यम् ।*
*आचार्यः पूर्वरूपम् ।*
*अन्तेवास्युत्तररूपम् ।*
*विद्या सन्धिः ।*
*प्रवचनं सन्धानम् ।*
*इत्यधिविद्यम् ॥*

*Athadhividyam*
*Acharyah purvarupam*
*Antevasy uttararupam*
*Vidya sandhih*
*Pravachanam sandhanam*
*Ity adhividyam*

**Translation**

Now, concerning knowledge: The teacher is the first form, the student is the second form, knowledge is their meeting point, and instruction is the bond that unites them. This is the contemplation concerning learning.

<a id="shloka8"></a>

**Shloka 1:8**

*अथाधिप्रजम् ।*
*माता पूर्वरूपम् ।*
*पितोत्तररूपम् ।*
*प्रजा सन्धिः ।*
*प्रजननं सन्धानम् ।*
*इत्यधिप्रजम् ॥*

*Athadhiprajam*
*Mata purvarupam*
*Pita uttararupam*
*Praja sandhih*
*Prajananam sandhanam*
*Ity adhiprajam*

**Translation**

Now, concerning progeny: The mother is the first form, the father is the second form, the child is their meeting point, and procreation is the bond that unites them. This is the contemplation concerning progeny.

<a id="shloka9"></a>

**Shloka 1:9**

*अथाध्यात्मम् ।*
*अधरा हनुः पूर्वरूपम् ।*
*उत्तरा हनुरुत्तररूपम् ।*
*वाक् सन्धिः ।*
*जिह्वा सन्धानम् ।*
*इत्यध्यात्मम् ॥*

*Athadhyatmam*
*Adhara hanuh purvarupam*
*Uttara hanur uttararupam*
*Vak sandhih*
*Jihva sandhanam*
*Ity adhyatmam*

**Translation**

Now, concerning the individual self: The lower jaw is the first form, the upper jaw is the second form, speech is their meeting point, and the tongue is the connecting instrument. This is the contemplation concerning the Self.

**Shloka 1:10**

*इतीमा महासंहिताः ।*
*य एवं एता महासंहिताः व्याख्याताः वेद ।*
*सन्धीयते प्रजया पशुभिः ।*
*ब्रह्मवर्चसेन ।*
*अन्नाद्येन सुवर्ग्येण लोकेन ॥*

*Iti ima mahasamhitah*
*Ya evam eta mahasamhitah vyakhyata veda*
*Sandhiyate prajaya pashubhih*
*Brahmavarchasena*
*Annadyena suvargyena lokena*

**Translation**

These are the Great Contemplations. Whoever understands and realizes them becomes endowed with worthy offspring, prosperity, spiritual brilliance, abundance of food, and an exalted state of life.

**Shloka 1:11**

*स यश्चायं पुरुषे ।*
*यश्चासावादित्ये ।*
*स एकः ।*
*स य एवं वेद ॥*

*Sa yash chayam purushe*
*Yash chasav aditye*
*Sa ekah*
*Sa ya evam veda*

**Translation**

The Being who dwells within the human person and the Being who shines in the Sun are one and the same. Whoever realizes this truth understands the essential unity of all existence.

**Shloka 1:12**

*सह नाववतु ।*
*सह नौ भुनक्तु ।*
*सह वीर्यं करवावहै ।*
*तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु ।*
*मा विद्विषावहै ॥*
*शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥*

*Saha nav avatu*
*Saha nau bhunaktu*
*Saha viryam karavavahai*
*Tejasvinav adhitam astu*
*Ma vidvishavahai*
*Shantih Shantih Shantih*

**Translation**

May the Supreme protect us both. May He nourish us both. May we work together with great energy. May our study be enlightening and fruitful. May we never have hatred toward one another. Peace, Peace, Peace.

<a id="shloka13"></a>

**Shloka 1:13**

*ब्रह्मविदाप्नोति परम् ।*
*तदेषाऽभ्युक्ता ।*
*सत्यं ज्ञानमनन्तं ब्रह्म ।*
*यो वेद निहितं गुहायां परमे व्योमन् ।*
*सोऽश्नुते सर्वान् कामान्त्सह ब्रह्मणा विपश्चितेति ॥ १३ ॥*

*brahmavidapnoti param*
*tadesha abhyukta*
*satyam gyanam anantam brahma*
*yo veda nihitam guhayam parame vyoman*
*so ashnute sarvan kaman saha brahmana vipashchiteti*

**Translation**

He who knows Brahman attains the Supreme. Regarding this it is said: Brahman is Truth, Knowledge, and Infinite. He who knows It as existing in the cave of the heart, in the highest akasha, enjoys all desires simultaneously along with the all-knowing Brahman.

**Shloka 1:14**

*अन्नाद्वै प्रजाः प्रजायन्ते ।*
*याः काश्च पृथिवीं श्रिताः ।*
*अथो अन्नेनैव जीवन्ति ।*
*अथैनदपि यन्त्यन्ततः ॥ १४ ॥*

*annadvai prajah prajayante*
*yah kashcha prithivim shritah*
*atho annenaiva jivanti*
*athainadapi yantyantatah*

**Translation**

From food indeed are creatures born, whatever creatures dwell on the earth. Moreover, by food alone do they live, and into it, in the end, they pass.

*prano hi bhutanam ayuh*
*tasmat sarvayushamuchyate*

**Translation**

The gods breathe following the vital breath, as do men and animals. For breath is the life of beings, therefore it is called the life of all.

<a id="shloka18"></a>

**Shloka 1:18**

*असन्नेव स भवति ।*
*असद्ब्रह्मेति वेद चेत् ।*
*अस्ति ब्रह्मेति चेद्वेद ।*
*सन्तमेनं ततो विदुरिति ॥ १८ ॥*

*asanneva sa bhavati*
*asad brahmeti veda chet*
*asti brahmeti chedveda*
*santam enam tato viduriti*

**Translation**

He himself becomes non-existent, if he knows Brahman as non-existent. If he knows that Brahman exists, then the wise know him as existent.

<a id="shloka19"></a>

**Shloka 1:19**

*असद्वा इदमग्र आसीत् ।*
*ततो वै सदजायत ।*
*तदात्मानं स्वयमकुरुत ।*
*तस्मात्तत्सुकृतमुच्यत इति ॥ १९ ॥*

*asadva idamagra asit*
*tato vai sadajayata*
*tadatmanam swayamakuruta*
*tasmat tat sukritam uchyata iti*

**Translation**

In the beginning, this was non-existent (unmanifest). From that, existence (the manifest) was born. That made itself by itself; therefore it is called self-made (well-made).

From fear of Him, the wind blows; from fear of Him, the sun rises; from fear of Him, Agni and Indra function, and Death, the fifth, runs its course.

**Shloka 1:21**

*एतं ह वाव न तपति ।*
*किमहं साधु नाकरवम् ।*
*किमहं पापमकरवमिति ।*
*स य एवं विद्वानेते आत्मानं स्पृणुते ॥ २१ ॥*

*etam ha vava na tapati*
*kimaham sadhu na akaravam*
*kimaham papam akaravam iti*
*sa ya evam vidvanete atmanam sprinute*

**Translation**

This does not trouble him: "Why did I not do good?" or "Why did I do evil?" He who knows this rises above both (good and evil) — this indeed is the essence of this Upanishad.

<a id="shloka22"></a>

**Shloka 1:22**

*भृगुर्वै वारुणिः । वरुणं पितरमुपससार । अधीहि भगवो ब्रह्मेति । तस्मा एतत्प्रोवाच । अन्नं प्राणं चक्षुःश्रोत्रं मनो वाचमिति । तं होवाच । यतो वा इमानि भूतानि जायन्ते । येन जातानि जीवन्ति । यत्प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्ति । तद्विजिज्ञासस्व । तद्ब्रह्मेति । स तपोऽतप्यत । स तपस्तप्त्वा ॥ २२ ॥*

*bhriguvai varunih, varunam pitaramupasasara, adhihi bhagavo brahmeti, tasma etatpravocha, annam pranam chakshuh shrotram mano vachamiti, tam hovacha, yato va imani bhutani jayante, yena jatani jivanti, yatprayantyabhisamvishanti, tadvigyasasva, tadbrahmeti, sa tapoatapyata, sa tapastaptva*

**Translation**

Bhrigu, son of Varuna, approached his father and said, "Lord, teach me Brahman." Varuna told him: food, breath, eye, ear, mind, and speech (are the means to know it). He further said: "That from which these beings are born, that by which, once born, they live, and that into which they depart and merge — seek to know that. That is Brahman." Bhrigu performed austerity.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka23"></a>

**Shloka 1:23**

*अन्नं ब्रह्मेति व्यजानात् । अन्नाद्ध्येव खल्विमानि भूतानि जायन्ते । अन्नेन जातानि जीवन्ति । अन्नं प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति । तद्विज्ञाय । पुनरेव वरुणं पितरमुपससार । अधीहि भगवो ब्रह्मेति । तं होवाच । तपसा ब्रह्म विजिज्ञासस्व । तपो ब्रह्मेति । स तपोऽतप्यत । स तपस्तप्त्वा ॥ २३ ॥*

*annam brahmeti vyajanat, annaddhyeva khalvimani bhutani jayante, annena jatani jivanti, annam prayantyabhisamvishantiti, tadvigyaya, punareva varunam pitaramupasasara, adhihi bhagavo brahmeti, tam hovacha, tapasa brahma vijigyasasva, tapo brahmeti, sa tapoatapyata, sa tapastaptva*

**Translation**

He realized that food is Brahman — for from food indeed all beings are born, by food, once born, they live, and into food they depart and merge. Having understood this, he again approached his father Varuna: "Lord, teach me further about Brahman." Varuna said: "Seek to know Brahman through austerity; austerity is Brahman." Bhrigu performed austerity.

**Conversation**

<a id="shloka24"></a>

**Shloka 1:24**

*प्राणो ब्रह्मेति व्यजानात् । प्राणाद्ध्येव खल्विमानि भूतानि जायन्ते । प्राणेन जातानि जीवन्ति । प्राणं प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति । तद्विज्ञाय । पुनरेव वरुणं पितरमुपससार । अधीहि भगवो ब्रह्मेति । तं होवाच । तपसा ब्रह्म विजिज्ञासस्व । तपो ब्रह्मेति । स तपोऽतप्यत । स तपस्तप्त्वा ॥ २४ ॥*

*prano brahmeti vyajanat, pranaddhyeva khalvimani bhutani jayante, pranena jatani jivanti, pranam prayantyabhisamvishantiti, tadvigyaya, punareva varunam pitaramupasasara, adhihi bhagavo brahmeti, tam hovacha, tapasa brahma vijigyasasva, tapo brahmeti, sa tapoatapyata, sa tapastaptva*

**Translation**

He realized that breath (prana) is Brahman — for from breath all beings are born, by breath they live, and into breath they depart and merge. Having understood this, he again approached Varuna for further teaching. Varuna again said: "Seek to know Brahman through austerity." Bhrigu performed austerity.

<a id="shloka25"></a>

**Shloka 1:25**

*मनो ब्रह्मेति व्यजानात् । मनसो ह्येव खल्विमानि भूतानि जायन्ते । मनसा जातानि जीवन्ति । मनः प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति । तद्विज्ञाय । पुनरेव वरुणं पितरमुपससार । अधीहि भगवो ब्रह्मेति । तं होवाच । तपसा ब्रह्म विजिज्ञासस्व । तपो ब्रह्मेति । स तपोऽतप्यत । स तपस्तप्त्वा ॥ २५ ॥*

*mano brahmeti vyajanat, manaso hyeva khalvimani bhutani jayante, manasa jatani jivanti, manah prayantyabhisamvishantiti, tadvigyaya, punareva varunam pitaramupasasara, adhihi bhagavo brahmeti, tam hovacha, tapasa brahma vijigyasasva, tapo brahmeti, sa tapoatapyata, sa tapastaptva*

**Translation**

He realized that mind is Brahman — for from mind all beings are born, by mind they live, and into mind they depart and merge. Having understood this, he again approached Varuna. Varuna again directed him: "Seek to know Brahman through austerity." Bhrigu performed austerity.

<a id="shloka26"></a>

**Shloka 1:26**

*विज्ञानं ब्रह्मेति व्यजानात् । विज्ञानाद्ध्येव खल्विमानि भूतानि जायन्ते । विज्ञानेन जातानि जीवन्ति । विज्ञानं प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति । तद्विज्ञाय । पुनरेव वरुणं पितरमुपससार । अधीहि भगवो ब्रह्मेति । तं होवाच । तपसा ब्रह्म विजिज्ञासस्व । तपो ब्रह्मेति । स तपोऽतप्यत । स तपस्तप्त्वा ॥ २६ ॥*

*vigyanam brahmeti vyajanat, vigyanaddhyeva khalvimani bhutani jayante, vigyanena jatani jivanti, vigyanam prayantyabhisamvishantiti, tadvigyaya, punareva varunam pitaramupasasara, adhihi bhagavo brahmeti, tam hovacha, tapasa brahma vijigyasasva, tapo brahmeti, sa tapoatapyata, sa tapastaptva*

**Translation**

He realized that intellect/knowledge (vijnana) is Brahman — for from intellect all beings are born, by intellect they live, and into intellect they depart and merge. Having understood this, he again approached Varuna. Varuna once more said: "Seek to know Brahman through austerity." Bhrigu performed austerity.

<a id="shloka27"></a>

**Shloka 1:27**

*आनन्दो ब्रह्मेति व्यजानात् । आनन्दाद्ध्येव खल्विमानि भूतानि जायन्ते । आनन्देन जातानि जीवन्ति । आनन्दं प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति । सैषा भार्गवी वारुणी विद्या । परमे व्योमन्प्रतिष्ठिता । स य एवं वेद प्रतितिष्ठति । अन्नवानन्नादो भवति । महान्भवति प्रजया पशुभिर्ब्रह्मवर्चसेन । महान्कीर्त्या ॥ २७ ॥*

*anando brahmeti vyajanat, anandaddhyeva khalvimani bhutani jayante, anandena jatani jivanti, anandam prayantyabhisamvishantiti, saisha bhargavi varuni vidya, parame vyomanpratishthita, sa ya evam veda pratitishthati, annavanannado bhavati, mahanbhavati prajaya pashubhirbrahmavarchasena, mahankirtya*

**Translation**

Finally he realized that bliss (ananda) is Brahman — for from bliss all beings are born, by bliss they live, and into bliss they depart and merge. This is the knowledge that Bhrigu received from his father Varuna, established in the highest heaven. He who knows this becomes established; he becomes possessed of food and an eater of food; he becomes great in offspring, cattle, and the radiance of holiness, and great in fame.

<a id="shloka28"></a>

**Shloka 1:28**

*अन्नं न निन्द्यात् । तद्व्रतम् । प्राणो वा अन्नम् । शरीरमन्नादम् । प्राणे शरीरं प्रतिष्ठितम् । शरीरे प्राणः प्रतिष्ठितः । तदेतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितम् । स य एतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितं वेद प्रतितिष्ठति । अन्नवानन्नादो भवति । महान्भवति प्रजया पशुभिर्ब्रह्मवर्चसेन । महान्कीर्त्या ॥ २८ ॥*

*annam na nindyat, tadvratam, prano va annam, shariramannadam, prane shariram pratishthitam, sharire pranah pratishthitah, tadetadannamanne pratishthitam, sa ya etadannamanne pratishthitam veda pratitishthati, annavanannado bhavati, mahanbhavati prajaya pashubhirbrahmavarchasena, mahankirtya*

**Translation**

One should not disrespect food — this is the vow. Breath indeed is food, and the body is the eater of food. The body rests on breath, and breath rests on the body; thus food rests upon food. He who knows this — that food rests upon food — becomes established, becomes possessed of food, great in offspring, cattle, and holiness, and great in fame.

<a id="shloka29"></a>

**Shloka 1:29**

*अन्नं न परिचक्षीत । तद्व्रतम् । आपो वा अन्नम् । ज्योतिरन्नादम् । अप्सु ज्योतिः प्रतिष्ठितम् । ज्योतिष्यापः प्रतिष्ठिताः । तदेतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितम् । स य एतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितं वेद प्रतितिष्ठति । अन्नवानन्नादो भवति । महान्भवति प्रजया पशुभिर्ब्रह्मवर्चसेन । महान्कीर्त्या ॥ २९ ॥*

*annam na parichakshita, tadvratam, apo va annam, jyotirannadam, apsu jyotih pratishthitam, jyotishyapah pratishthitah, tadetadannamanne pratishthitam, sa ya etadannamanne pratishthitam veda pratitishthati, annavanannado bhavati, mahanbhavati prajaya pashubhirbrahmavarchasena, mahankirtya*

**Translation**

One should not turn away food — this is the vow. Water indeed is food, and light (fire) is the eater of food. Light rests on water, and water rests on light; thus food rests upon food. He who knows this becomes established, becomes possessed of food, great in offspring, cattle, and holiness, and great in fame.

<a id="shloka30"></a>

**Shloka 1:30**

*अन्नं बहु कूर्वीत । तद्व्रतम् । पृथिवी वा अन्नम् । आकाशोऽन्नादः । पृथिव्यामाकाशः प्रतिष्ठितः । आकाशे पृथिवी प्रतिष्ठिता । तदेतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितम् । स य एतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितं वेद प्रतितिष्ठति । अन्नवानन्नादो भवति । महान्भवति प्रजया पशुभिर्ब्रह्मवर्चसेन । महान्कीर्त्या ॥ ३० ॥*

*annam bahu kurvita, tadvratam, prithivi va annam, akashoannadah, prithivyam akashah pratishthitah, akashe prithivi pratishthita, tadetadannamanne pratishthitam, sa ya etadannamanne pratishthitam veda pratitishthati, annavanannado bhavati, mahanbhavati prajaya pashubhirbrahmavarchasena, mahankirtya*

**Translation**

One should produce food in abundance — this is the vow. Earth indeed is food, and space (akasha) is the eater of food. Space rests on earth, and earth rests in space; thus food rests upon food. He who knows this becomes established, becomes possessed of food, great in offspring, cattle, and holiness, and great in fame.

<a id="shloka31"></a>

**Shloka 1:31**

*न कञ्चन वसतौ प्रत्याचक्षीत । तद्व्रतम् । तस्माद्यया कया च विधया बह्वन्नं प्राप्नुयात् । अराध्यस्मा अन्नमित्याचक्षते । एतद्वै मुखतोऽन्नं राद्धम् । ...य एवं विद्वानेते आत्मानं स्पृणुते । उभे ह्येवैष एते आत्मानं स्पृणुते । य एवं वेद । इत्युपनिषत् ॥ ३१ ॥*

*na kanchana vasatau pratyachakshita, tadvratam, tasmadyaya kaya cha vidhaya bahvannam prapnuyat, aradhyasma annamityachakshate... aham annam aham annam aham annam, aham annado aham annado aham annadah, aham shlokakrid aham shlokakrid aham shlokakrit, ahamasmi prathamaja ritasya, purvam devebhyo amritasya nabhayi, yo ma dadati sa ideva ma avah, ahamannam annamadantamadmi, aham vishvam bhuvanamabhyabhavam, suvarna jyotih, ya evam veda, ityupanishat*

**Translation**

One should not turn away any guest seeking shelter — this is the vow. Therefore, by whatever means, one should obtain abundant food; food, they say, is (thus) well-earned for him. This becomes the famous final declaration: "I am food, I am food, I am food. I am the eater of food, the eater of food, the eater of food. I am the maker of verses, the maker of verses, the maker of verses. I am the firstborn of the cosmic order, prior to the gods, at the centre of immortality. Whoever gives me away, he alone preserves me thus. I, who am food, eat the eater of food. I have overcome the whole universe, I am radiant like the sun." He who knows this (attains liberation). This indeed is the Upanishad.
