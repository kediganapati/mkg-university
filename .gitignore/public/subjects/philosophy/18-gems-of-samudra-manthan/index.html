5400 KM Ride…Zero Knowledge

Kedi: “I did a 5400-kilometre bike journey to visit 5 big temples, but I didn’t get any knowledge about religion. Have I gained any knowledge that I am ignoring?”

Ganesh: “Which knowledge did you want to gain?”

Kedi: “Any knowledge that is related to Sanatan Dharma?”

Ganesh: “Then you are not even worthy of gaining any knowledge.”

Kedi: “Why am I not worthy of gaining knowledge?”

Ganesh: “Until we do not know which knowledge we need, we cannot search for any knowledge. If you had thought that you needed knowledge of one particular subject, then the elements related to that subject would start appearing to you. But when you do not even know which subject’s knowledge you need, even if thousands of elements related to that subject are placed in front of you, you cannot acquire any knowledge.”

Friend Mode ON! Teacher Mode OFF

Kedi: “Your words sound like the sermons of a great elder. I feel that you and I are very far apart. Can you not explain like a friend? I am not able to understand your words easily.”

Ganesh: “You have no friend; that is why you are asking to speak like a friend. Friends are very nasty; if you fall, they will first grab both your legs and spin you 10 times, then pick you up and ask, ‘Did you enjoy it?’ Do you want me also to pull your leg, tease you, and when you give up, then console you?”

Kedi: “Yes, it will work; you explain just like a friend. When you give sermons like a guru, it becomes difficult to understand.”

Ocean in Front… Samudra Manthan in Mind

Ganesh: “Okay, now first do one thing: choose any one topic related to Sanatan Dharma about which you want to know, or because of which topic you have doubt about Sanatan Dharma, and yes, until you think about this, do not disturb me. I want to taste a little of the salty air of this ocean.”

Hearing Ganesh speak in this manner, I was astonished and also enjoying it. I started thinking: there are many topics in Sanatan Dharma; about which topic do I have doubt? Which topic have I left incomplete?

I was sitting on the seashore of Jagannath Puri; only the ocean was coming to my mind. Then suddenly I remembered that I was writing the chapter of Samudra Manthan, in which the 14 ratnas were mentioned, which seemed strange to me. I got the topic — Samudra Manthan.

Kedi: “I have got the topic; I need the knowledge of Samudra Manthan.”

Ganesh: “You are not only stingy, you are lazy too; seeing the ocean in front of you, you found the topic of Samudra Manthan.”

Kedi: “Which are the 18 jewels?”

Cosmic Collision, Memory Erased

Kedi: “What is the story of Samudra Manthan?”

Ganesh: “A dangerous explosive event happened in the entire universe; after that event, all the devtas and ganas that existed in the universe—everyone’s mind-light—went out. Everyone had forgotten their memory; no one knew their role. Whose crores of wealth were lying where? Everyone had forgotten all that. What they had to do, what they should not do — they had forgotten that too. The master was saluting the servant, and the servant was eating someone else’s wealth for free without working. One rakshasa, being a rakshasa, was afraid of another rakshasa, and one devata, being a devata, was suspecting another devata. No one could figure out who they really were.

In the universe there were 8 types of devtas, 8 types of davow, 12 types of daityas, 12 types of rakshasas, 8 types of chandals, 8 types of asuras, 8 types of suras, 8 types of nagas, 8 types of gandharvas, 8 types of sages, 4 types of apsaras, 4 types of kinnars, 4 types of devasena, 4 types of yakshas, 4 types of dwarapalas, 4 types of dhanvantaris, 2 types of danavdoot, and 2 types of devdoot. In total, 8 devtas and 108 types of ganas existed — but everyone had forgotten who was which type of gana and who was which type of devta. A very big mess had happened.”

Kedi: “why stop? Tell the story, tell the whole story at once; I want to hear it now.”

Ganesh: “I am not your grandma to tell you the whole story at once; still, listen. Parmeshwar Ganapati descended knowledge into 2 danavdoots—Shumbha and Nishumbha—and 2 devdoots—the Ashwini Kumar—and sent them to Lord Vishnu to find a solution to this problem.

Danavdoots themselves do not do any work; they get their work done by asuri ganas. Because the danavdoots received knowledge from Parmeshwar Ganapati, they came to know which of the 108 ganas were asuri ganas. Taking all those Asuri Ganas, they went to Lord Vishnu.

Devdoots also cannot do any work themselves; they get their work done by daiviya ganas. Because the devdoots received knowledge from Parmeshwar Ganapati, they came to know which of the 108 ganas were daiviya ganas and devtas. Taking all those daiviya ganas and devtas, they went to Lord Vishnu.”

Ganesh: “Did you understand this much, or is your attention still counting the ocean waves?”

Do the Work vs Get the Work Done

Kedi: “Why can’t Devdoot and Danavdoot do work themselves?”

Ganesh: “Devdoot and danavdoot do not have the quality to enter a body. All ganas can enter a suitable body according to their qualities, but devdoots and danavdoots cannot enter any body. To do any work, having a body is necessary. Now understood?”

Kedi: “The devdoots and danavdoots who cannot do work themselves—why did Parmeshwar Ganapati give them wisdom?”

Ganesh: “Bro, everyone’s wisdom is different; your wisdom is different, my wisdom is different, and those people in front who are enjoying it right now—everyone’s wisdom is different.”

Kedi: “I know that everyone’s wisdom is different; someone’s wisdom keeps developing, and someone’s wisdom becomes weak.”

Ganesh: “There are two types of wisdom—to do work and to get work done. Parmeshwar gave those devdoots and danavdoots the wisdom to get work done by others and gave them the realization to send all ganas to Lord Vishnu. After receiving that realisation, the devdoots and danavdoots took all the ganas to Lord Vishnu.”

Gods Confused, Demons Confused — Total Chaos!

Kedi: “Then what happened?”

Ganesh: “Lord Vishnu explained to all the Ganas that a strange, destructive but eternally ongoing event has occurred, and this event will keep happening in the future too. Whenever all the celestial bodies present in this Hiranyagarbha come in a straight line, a destructive energy will manifest and will forever erase the memory of all Devas and Ganas descending on the celestial bodies. This very event has destroyed everyone’s memory, and now you are not able to understand what your character is, what your home and destination are, what the purpose of your life is, who your friend and enemy are, and most importantly, who you are going to become; you have forgotten everything.”

Kedi: “Does this event destroy even God’s memory? Why didn’t the memory of Supreme Lord Ganapati and Lord Vishnu get destroyed?”

Ganesh: “Tell the truth, are you listening to me or staring at the girls walking on the beach?”

Kedi: “Bro, why are you saying this? I am only listening to you and trying to understand.”

Ganesh: “I had explained to you last year itself that Gods and Chiranjivis remain in the same character from birth to death in a human body; their character never gets destroyed. Only the character of Devas and Ganas keeps changing, because Devas and Ganas can enter a human body anytime and can leave that body anytime. Have you forgotten all this?”

Kedi: I remember now.

Kedi: “Then what did Lord Vishnu say?”

Ganesh: “Lord Vishnu told the solution to all those Devtas and Ganas to get rid of their problem. Lord Vishnu told them all, ‘Your memory can come back, for which all of you have to drink two drops of Amrit. As soon as you drink drops of Amrit, you will get self-realisation of who you are, what your character is, and what your rights, responsibilities and proof of existence are.

That Amrit is buried in this Kshirsagar; by churning this Kshirsagar, that Amrit will come out, and by obtaining it, you will get your memory, your existence and your character. Do you want to obtain that Amrit by doing Samudra Manthan?’ All Devas and Ganas said, ‘Yes, we want to obtain that Amrit by doing Samudra Manthan.’

Lord Vishnu told them all, ‘To churn this Kshirsagar, we will need a base pillar, churning rod, rope and divine-demonic two opposing energies. You arrange all these and then return to this very place; we will do the process of Samudra Manthan here.’”

Ganesh: “The Devas and Ganas said to Lord Vishnu, ‘Our memory is destroyed, so we don’t know what base pillar, churning rod, rope and divine-demonic two opposing energies are; we don’t remember, and even if we see them, we won’t recognise them. You yourself guide us.’”

Kedi: “Then what happened?”

Ganesh: “All ganas and devtas started pleading to Lord Vishnu to tell them the way to regain their memory power. By losing memory, everyone had lost their existence. Neither the Devtas knew they were Devtas nor the Danav knew they were Danav. Because of this dilemma, they were not able to do their work and were not able to enjoy the results of their work.

Lord Vishnu told all of them that their memory power would return when they drank two drops of the amrit obtained from Samudra Manthan.”

Kedi: “So did all of them drink the drops of amrit?”

Ganesh: “Was the drop of amrit in their pocket so that they could put their hand inside and drink it? To drink the drop of amrit, first they had to do the process of Samudra Manthan. After that—”

Kshirsagar: Cosmic Ocean, Not Salty Ocean

Kedi: “Then what did Lord Vishnu do?”

Ganesh: “Lord Vishnu told all those ganas the process of doing Samudra Manthan and took everyone to Kshirsagar.”

Kedi: “The ocean that is in front right now, is this not Kshirsagar?”

Ganesh: “Yes, this is Kshirsagar, you are Vishnu, I am Ganapati, and all these people in front enjoying themselves are devtas and daVow. Brother, think a little before speaking. Samudra Manthan is happening; one wrong question from you and your own manthan will start.”

Kedi: “Then what is this Kshirsagar?”

Ganesh: “Just like this universe, there are a total of 7 universes. All 7 universes are circling one Vaikuntha, and 7 universes and 1 Vaikuntha—all this is inside 1 Hiranyagarbha. Hiranyagarbha itself is the womb of Parmeshwar Ganapati.

The empty space between all the universes and Vaikuntha present inside Hiranyagarbha—that entire space is Kshirsagar. The entire Hiranyagarbha is filled with Kshirsagar, and inside that Kshirsagar are the universes and Vaikuntha.

Out of those 7 universes, in one universe there are trillions of Suryamandals; out of them, in one Suryamandal, out of 8 planets, there is one planet — Earth. Out of 7 Mahasagars, there is one Sagar in front of which you are sitting, and you are thinking this small Sagar is Kshirsagar. Now tell me, what should I say to you?”

Kedi: “The entire universe is surrounded by Kshirsagar; the whole universe is in Kshirsagar, so at which place in Kshirsagar did the Samudra Manthan happen?”

Ganesh: “In the region of Kshirsagar, exactly above Vaikunth, inside Kshirsagar, Lord Vishnu gathered all the ganas.”

Kedi: “Why exactly in the Kshirsagar above Vaikunth? Why not somewhere else?”

Ganesh: “You weren’t born then; otherwise we would have done the churning in your navel. Simple thing — churning always happens at the centre. The centre of the entire Kshirsagar is Vaikunth, so the Samudra Manthan could happen in the region above Vaikunth. Understood now?”

A Spiritual Process, Not a Physical Event

Kedi: “Then what happened?”

Ganesh: “When all the ganas and devas were standing in attendance at the centre of Kshirsagar above Vaikunth and in front of Lord Vishnu, then Lord Vishnu explained the process of Samudra Manthan and told them about the 18 jewels that would emerge from Kshirsagar.”

Kedi: “Before Samudra Manthan itself, Goddess Lakshmi, four types of Dhanvantari, and the moon all already exist—so how can they emerge again from Samudra Manthan?”

Ganesh: “Seeing your antics, laughter can come out from within me every day, so can’t someone emerge again from Samudra Manthan?”

Kedi: “What is the connection between your laughter and the jewels of Samudra Manthan?”

Ganesh: “Just as laughter cannot be seen but the one laughing can be seen—similarly, the 18 jewels emerging from Samudra Manthan are invisible and spiritual elements. They cannot be seen, but to understand them, their symbols can be shown. All 18 jewels are symbols of 18 spiritual attainments; they are not physical objects.”

Kedi: “So is Samudra Manthan also a spiritual process, not a physical process?”

Ganesh: “My effect is working—you are thinking like me. Correct. Yes, Samudra Manthan is the symbolic form of the principles of karma created by Supreme Lord Ganapati and planned by Lord Vishnu.”

Kedi: “How?”

5 Forces Churn Kshirsagar

Ganesh: “First listen to the complete process of Samudra Manthan; then I will explain.

To churn any substance, 5 forces are needed:

A vessel to hold the substance A base pillar to stabilize the vessel A churning rod A rope to rotate the churning rod The opposing force that creates friction by rotating the churning rod back and forth.”

Kedi: “Which substance? Where is that substance?”

Ganesh: “The entire Kshirsagar itself is the substance, and that substance is everywhere inside Hiranyagarbha. So, the vessel in which that substance is—that vessel itself is Hiranyagarbha.”

Kedi: “Then the base pillar for the stability of Hiranyagarbha should be outside Hiranyagarbha, but you had said that there is nothing outside Hiranyagarbha.”

Ganesh: “There are two types of base pillars; one is outside the substance, and one is inside the substance. The base pillar of Kshirsagar and the root base pillar of all substances present in Kshirsagar are inside that substance. The root base pillar of you and all humans is also within oneself.”

Kedi: “Which are those 5 forces needed for Samudra Manthan?”

Ganesh: “This very question was asked by an Asuri Gana to Lord Vishnu: ‘Are you that Asuri Gana who accidentally dropped on Earth?’”

Kedi: “Please, tell me which are those 5 forces?”

Ganesh: “Kshirsagar had to be churned; a huge churning rod would be needed, so Lord Vishnu took all the Ganas near Mount Meru and told all the Ganas, ‘This Mount Meru itself is the churning rod; take it and establish it in the centre above Vaikunth.’

All divine and demonic Ganas lifted Mount Meru and established it in the centre above Vaikunth. To rotate that huge Mount Meru, a big rope was needed. Lord Vishnu took all the Ganas to the Vasuki snake and told all the Ganas, ‘This Vasuki snake itself is the rope; wrap it around Mount Meru.’”

Ganesh: “A poisonous smell was coming from the Vasuki snake’s mouth, due to which whoever stood near the Vasuki snake’s mouth experienced a different kind of intoxication, and a sweet music tune was playing from the Vasuki snake’s tail, so whoever stood near the Vasuki snake’s tail experienced being lost in music.

The demonic Ganas liked the experience of intoxication, so the demonic Ganas caught hold of the Vasuki snake’s mouth part. The divine Ganas liked the music tune, so the divine Ganas caught hold of the Vasuki snake’s tail. All Ganas caught Vasuki and wrapped it around Mount Meru.”

Ganesh: “Mount Meru could not stay in one place without a base pillar, so Lord Vishnu took all the Ganas to Muladhar tortoise and told all the Ganas, ‘This Muladhar tortoise itself is the base pillar; place it under Mount Meru.’

All Ganas lifted the Muladhar tortoise and placed it under Mount Meru.

The time had come to churn Kshirsagar by rotating Mount Meru. On Lord Vishnu’s order, the Devas and demonic Ganas holding the Vasuki snake wrapped around Mount Meru started doing Samudra Manthan.”

Ganesh: “Understood what? Anything to ask?”

Without a body, no god or gana can drink that nectar. No god or gana has their own body; they have to enter a human body to drink the nectar.”

Ganesh: “Maybe you are hungry; on an empty stomach your brain doesn’t work. Eat something first, and then I’ll explain.”

Kedi: “After Apsara Rambha, what emerges?”

Goddess Lakshmi — Symbol of Contentment

Ganesh: “Goddess Lakshmi emerges. Goddess Lakshmi is a spiritual element. Goddess Lakshmi is the symbol of contentment. When a person gets praise for his work, then he feels contentment; it is called obtaining Goddess Lakshmi.”

Kedi: “After Goddess Lakshmi, what emerges?”

Rajhans — Symbol of Selection

Ganesh: “Rajhans emerges. Rajhans is a spiritual element. Rajhans is the symbol of selection. When a person gets contentment from his work, then to maintain that contentment, the person selects righteous and favourable work so that he gets contentment from that work too. When a person starts selecting work according to his wish and does not do any work against his desire, then he obtains the power and confidence to select; it is called obtaining Rajhans.”

Kedi: “After Rajhans, what emerges?”

Varuni — Symbol of Ego

Ganesh: “Varuni liquor emerges. Varuni liquor is a spiritual element. Varuni liquor is the symbol of ego. When a person has excessive confidence in his work, his self-confidence exceeds his consciousness, and then he starts getting ego; it is called obtaining Varuni liquor.”

Kedi: “After Varuni liquor, what emerges?”

Ardhachandra — Symbol of Adultery

Ganesh: “Ardhchandra (half-moon) emerges. Ardhachandra is a spiritual element. Ardhachandra is the symbol of adultery. The full moon is the symbol of love. When a person gets an ego, then to display his ego, he feels a desire to commit adultery. When he starts committing adultery to fulfil his ego, he obtains Ardhchandra.”

Kedi: “After Ardhchandra, what emerges?”

Indradhanush — Symbol of Flattery

Ganesh: “Indradhanush (rainbow) emerges. Indradhanush is a spiritual element. Indradhanush is the symbol of flattery. When a person becomes successful in fulfilling his ego and adultery, then he needs people who flatter him. To get his flattery done, a person keeps 7–8 specimens with him; it is called obtaining Indradhanush.”

Kedi: “After Indradhanush, what emerges?”

Parijat — Symbol of Show Off

Ganesh: “The Parijat tree emerges. The Parijat tree is a spiritual element. The Parijat tree is the symbol of show-off. When a person learns from his sycophants that his ego and adultery are visible to everyone, then to hide his ego and adultery, he pretends to be egoless and truthful. To pretend to be egoless and truthful, a person adopts a fake character; it is called obtaining the Parijat tree”.

Kedi: “After the Parijat tree, what emerges?”

Panchajanya — Symbol of Dilemma

Ganesh: “The Panchajanya conch emerges. The Panchajanya conch is a spiritual element. It is the symbol of dilemma. When a person fails to balance between his real character and fake character and fails to hide his fake character, then he has to face many dilemmas; it is called obtaining the Panchajanya conch.”

Kedi: “After the Panchajanya conch, what emerges?”

Indrajaal — Symbol of Downfall

Ganesh: “Whirlwind (Indrajaal) emerges. Whirlwind is a spiritual element. It is the symbol of downfall. When a person takes right decisions according to his real character and wrong decisions in trying to show a fake character as real, then the success he had got because of his real character—the downfall of that success—starts. That downfall is called obtaining Whirlwind (Indrajaal).”

Kedi: “After Whirlwind (Indrajaal), what emerges?”

Dhanvantari — Symbol of Remedy

Ganesh: “Dhanvantari emerges. Dhanvantari is a spiritual element. Dhanvantari is the symbol of remedy. When a person realises that the cause of his downfall was his fake show-off, then he finds a remedy to rise again; it is called obtaining Dhanvantari.”

Kedi: “After Dhanvantari, what emerges?”

Amrit — Symbol of Self Realisation

Ganesh: “Amrit emerges. Amrit is a spiritual element. Amrit is the symbol of self-realisation. When a person obtains various spiritual elements, then finally he gets self-realisation about who he really is—what the purpose of his life is, what he has to achieve, and what he has to return. When a person gets self-realisation, it is called obtaining Amrit.”

Kedi: “After Amrit, what emerges?”

Mohini — Symbol of Prestige

Ganesh: “Daitya Mohini emerges. Daitya Mohini is a spiritual element. It is the symbol of prestige. When a person gets self-realisation, he does influential work for society; because of that influential work, he gets prestige. It is called obtaining Daitya Mohini.”

Ganesh: “Now I have told you about all 18 jewels. Any doubt?”

Kedi: “What is the difference between fame and prestige?”

Ganesh: “When thousands of people know a person but do not respect him, it is called fame. When thousands of people know a person and also respect him, it is called prestige.”

No Fixed Order — Karma Decides

Kedi: “Do all 18 jewels emerge in the same order?”

Ganesh: “No. The order changes; every person’s karma is different, and their decision-making ability is different; therefore, not all people obtain the jewels in the same order. Not all people are able to obtain all 18 jewels; some people can obtain only 4–5 jewels, but some people can obtain all 18 jewels again and again.”

Kedi: “Does this Samudra Manthan happen with all people?”

Ganesh: “In the body of every living human, devas and ganas are rotating Meru mountain with Vasuki snake, producing 18 different jewels in his life, entering his body and enjoying those jewels. Samudra Manthan is happening inside all humans in whose body Meru mountain and Vasuki snake are active; various jewels are continuously being produced in his life.”

Kedi: “Is Samudra Manthan happening inside me right now too?”

Ganesh: “As long as we both are talking, no gana or deva can even touch your Meru mountain, but when we are not talking, every gana shakes your Meru mountain and makes you dance here and there, and you feel that you are dancing, meaning you are having fun.”

When I left home for the religious bike journey of 5 grand temples, I thought I would gain some new religious knowledge. At the last temple, I received the knowledge of Samudra Manthan, which was already inside him; I just had to open my mind and read it. Ganesh made the journey successful by giving the knowledge of Samudra Manthan.

Note: This content represents a chapter of Kedi Purana, a 64-chapter work authored by Kedi Ganapati. Kedi Purana is a modern Purana of the present and final Kaliyuga.
