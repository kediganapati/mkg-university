Ramayan — Bala Kanda — Sarga 11

Ramayan Bala Kanda

Verse 1

सुमन्त्रश्चोदितो राज्ञा प्रोवाचेदं वचस्तदा | यथा ऋष्यशृङ्गस्त्वानीतो येनोपायेन मन्त्रिभिः || १-११-१

Sumantra, prompted by the king, then spoke these words, telling how Rishyasringa had been brought by the ministers and by what means.

Verse 2

तत् शृणुष्व महीपाल यथावृत्तं पुरातनम् | सनत्कुमारो भगवान् पूर्वं कथितवान् कथाम् || १-११-२

“O lord of the earth, listen to this ancient account as it occurred. The divine Sanatkumara formerly narrated this story.”

Verse 3

ऋषीणां सन्निधौ राजन् तव पुत्रागमं प्रति | काश्यपस्य तु पुत्रोऽस्ति विभाण्डक इति श्रुतः || १-११-३

“O King, in the presence of the sages, concerning the coming of your sons, it was said that Kashyapa had a son known as Vibhandaka.”

Verse 4

ऋष्यशृङ्ग इति ख्यातस्तस्य पुत्रो भविष्यति | स वने नित्यसंवृद्धो मुनिर्वनचरः सदा || १-११-४

“His son will become known as Rishyasringa. He will grow up continually in the forest and will always live as a forest-dwelling sage.”

Verse 5

नान्यं जानाति विप्रेन्द्रो नित्यं पित्रनुवर्तनात् | द्वैविध्यं ब्रह्मचर्यस्य भविष्यति महात्मनः || १-११-५

“That foremost Brahmana will know no one else, always following his father. The great-souled sage will remain devoted to Brahmacharya.”

Verse 6

लोकेषु प्रथितं राजन् विप्रैः कथितमेव च | तस्यैवं वर्तमानस्य कालः समभिवर्तत || १-११-६

“O King, his way of life will become renowned throughout the worlds and will be spoken of by Brahmanas. While he lived in this manner, time passed.”

Verse 7

स कदाचिद् वनं गच्छन् पितरं चानुवर्तते | तत्रापश्यत् स्त्रियः सर्वा रूपेणाप्रतिमाः शुभाः || १-११-७

Once, while going through the forest and following his father, he saw some women of incomparable and beautiful appearance.

Verse 8

तासां दृष्ट्वा तु रूपाणि मनसा समचिन्तयत् | किमेतदिति तं देशं पप्रच्छ पितरं पुनः || १-११-८

Seeing their forms, he wondered in his mind what they were and again asked his father about that place and those beings.

Verse 9

तस्य पित्रा तु तद् दृष्ट्वा पुत्रस्याकुलितं मनः | उवाच वचनं तत्र सर्वं धर्मार्थसंहितम् || १-११-९

Seeing his son’s mind disturbed, his father spoke to him words concerning Dharma and its proper conduct.

Verse 10

स्त्रियो नाम महाभाग यास्त्वया दृष्टपूर्वकाः | एताः सर्वा न गन्तव्याः समीपं ते कथञ्चन || १-११-१०

“My son, those who are called women, whom you have seen, must never be approached by you under any circumstances.”

Verse 11

इत्युक्त्वा स पिता तस्य पुनरेव वनं ययौ | ऋष्यशृङ्गस्तु तद्वाक्यं पितुर्वाक्यममन्यत || १-११-११

Having said this, his father again went into the forest. Rishyasringa accepted his father’s words.

Verse 12

ततः कालेन महता रोमपादस्य मन्त्रिभिः | आनीतः स ऋषिश्रेष्ठो वर्षयामास तं देशम् || १-११-१२

After a long period of time, the ministers of Romapada brought that foremost sage there, and he caused rain to fall upon that land.

Verse 13

स तु राजा दशरथः श्रुत्वा वृत्तान्तमुत्तमम् | ऋष्यशृङ्गस्य चरितं हृष्टः परमविस्मितः || १-११-१३

King Dasharatha, hearing this wonderful account of the life and deeds of Rishyasringa, became delighted and greatly astonished.

Verse 14

सुमन्त्रं पुनरेवेदं वचनं प्रत्यभाषत | ऋष्यशृङ्गस्य वृत्तान्तं विस्तरेण ब्रवीहि मे || १-११-१४

He then said again to Sumantra:

“Tell me in detail the entire account concerning Rishyasringa.”

Verse 15

सुमन्त्रस्तु ततो राजा यथावृत्तं न्यवेदयत् | रोमपादस्य राजर्षेः कन्यां दत्त्वा च शान्तया || १-११-१५

Sumantra then related to the king the events as they had occurred, including the giving of Shanta, the daughter of the royal sage Romapada, in marriage.

Verse 16

ऋष्यशृङ्गाय राजेन्द्र तस्य पुत्रत्वमागतम् | ततो वर्षं समुत्पन्नं देशे तस्मिन् यथाक्रमम् || १-११-१६

“O great king, Rishyasringa became his son-in-law, and thereafter rain came to that land in due course.”

Verse 17

तं दृष्ट्वा च महीपालो हृष्टः परमधार्मिकः | उवाच वचनं राजा प्रीत्या परमया युतः || १-११-१७

Seeing this, the highly righteous king became delighted and, filled with great affection, spoke these words.

Verse 18

स्वागतं ते महाभाग ऋष्यशृङ्ग महातपाः | अद्य मे सफलं जन्म सफलं च तपो मम || १-११-१८

“Welcome to you, O greatly fortunate Rishyasringa, possessed of great austerity. Today my birth has become fruitful, and my austerities have become fruitful.”

Verse 19

त्वया हि वर्षिते देशे प्रजाः सर्वाः प्रमोदिताः | तस्मात् त्वं मे प्रियतमो नान्यः कश्चित् द्विजोत्तम || १-११-१९

“Because you have caused rain to fall upon this land, all the people have become joyful. Therefore you are especially dear to me, O foremost Brahmana.”

Verse 20

इत्युक्त्वा तं महाभागं पूजयामास पार्थिवः | अर्घ्यपाद्यादिभिर्द्रव्यैः कामैश्च विविधैस्तथा || १-११-२०

Having spoken thus, the king honoured that greatly fortunate sage with arghya, water for the feet, and various other offerings and desired objects.

Verse 21

ततः शान्तां च कन्यां तु ददौ तस्मै महीपतिः | ऋष्यशृङ्गाय विप्राय विधिना धर्मसंहिताम् || १-११-२१

Then the king gave his daughter Shanta to the Brahmana Rishyasringa in marriage according to the prescribed sacred rites.

Verse 22

स तां भार्यां समासाद्य शान्तां धर्मेण धर्मवित् | तया सह समागम्य न्यवसद् राजमन्दिरे || १-११-२२

The knower of Dharma, Rishyasringa, having taken Shanta as his wife according to Dharma, lived with her in the royal residence.

Verse 23

ततः कालेन महता स राजा दशरथस्तदा | अपुत्रः पुत्रकामस्तु यज्ञार्थं समुपागमत् || १-११-२३

After a long period, King Dasharatha, who was without sons and desired a son, came to the decision to undertake a sacrifice.

Verse 24

स तु राजा दशरथो हृष्टो यज्ञमुपागमत् | ऋष्यशृङ्गं पुरस्कृत्य वसिष्ठं च पुरोहितम् || १-११-२४

King Dasharatha, delighted, proceeded with the sacrifice, placing Rishyasringa and his royal priest Vasishtha at the forefront.

Verse 25

ततो यज्ञसमारम्भे सर्वकार्याणि चाकरोत् | ऋत्विजश्च समाहूय यथाशास्त्रं यथाक्रमम् || १-११-२५

Then, at the commencement of the sacrifice, he arranged all the necessary matters, inviting the priests and carrying out everything according to the scriptures and in proper order.

Verse 26

अश्वमेधेन यक्ष्येऽहमिति राजा व्यवस्थितः | ऋष्यशृङ्गं पुरस्कृत्य वसिष्ठं च महामुनिम् || १-११-२६

The king resolved: “I shall perform the Ashvamedha sacrifice,” placing the great sage Vasishtha and Rishyasringa at its forefront.

Verse 27

सर्वकामफलप्राप्तिं पुत्रलाभं च चिन्तयन् | यज्ञकर्म समारब्धं नृपस्यासीत् पुरोहितैः || १-११-२७

Thinking of obtaining the fulfilment of his desires and, especially, the birth of sons, the priests began the sacrificial rites for the king.

Verse 28

ततो यज्ञसमाप्त्यर्थं यथाशास्त्रं द्विजोत्तमाः | सर्वे समागतास्तत्र राज्ञो हितचिकीर्षवः || १-११-२८

Then, for the completion of the sacrifice, all the foremost Brahmanas assembled there according to the prescribed procedure, desiring the welfare of the king.

Verse 29

ऋष्यशृङ्गस्तु तत्रासीद् वसिष्ठश्च महामुनिः | अन्ये च ऋत्विजः सर्वे यज्ञकर्मसु निष्ठिताः || १-११-२९

Rishyasringa was there, as was the great sage Vasishtha, together with all the other priests who were accomplished in the sacrificial rites.

Verse 30

ततो दशरथो राजा तान् सर्वान् ब्राह्मणर्षभान् | पूजयामास विधिवत् सर्वकामैः समन्वितान् || १-११-३०

Then King Dasharatha properly honoured all those foremost Brahmanas with various offerings and desired objects.

Verse 31

एवं स यज्ञः सम्पूर्णो राज्ञो दशरथस्य वै | पुत्रकामस्य राजर्षेर्विधिवत् सम्प्रवर्तितः || १-११-३१

Thus the sacrifice of King Dasharatha, the royal sage who desired sons, proceeded properly toward completion.
