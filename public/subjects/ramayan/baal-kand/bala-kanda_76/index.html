Ramayan — Bala Kanda — Sarga 76

Ramayan Bala Kanda

Verse 1

अथ राजा दशरथो हृष्टः पुत्रमुपागतम् ।

दृष्ट्वा रामं महात्मानं प्रत्यनन्दत सादरम् ॥

Then King Dasharatha, delighted to see his great-souled son Rama who had returned, received him with great affection.

Verse 2

ततः स राजा काकुत्स्थः पुत्रैः सर्वैः समन्वितः ।

प्रविश्य नगरीं रम्यामयोध्यां मुदितोऽभवत् ॥

Then the Kakutstha king, accompanied by all his sons, entered the beautiful city of Ayodhya and became joyful.

Verse 3

ततो रामस्य चरितं श्रुत्वा सर्वे नराः पुरे ।

प्रहृष्टमनसः सर्वे बभूवुर्मुदितेक्षणाः ॥

Hearing of Rama’s deeds, all the people in the city became delighted, their minds filled with joy and their eyes cheerful.

Verse 4

ततो रामं महात्मानं सर्वे ते रघुनन्दनाः ।

उपेत्य सहिताः सर्वे प्रहृष्टाः पर्युपासते ॥

Then all the descendants of Raghu approached the great-souled Rama together and joyfully attended upon him.

Verse 5

ततः काले बहुगुणे ऋतूनां परिवर्तनात् ।

पिता दशरथः श्रीमान् रामं राज्येऽभ्यषेचयत् ॥

After some time had passed and the seasons had changed, the illustrious King Dasharatha decided to consecrate Rama as king.

6

ततो रामाभिषेकाय राजा दशरथो नृपः ।

मन्त्रिभिः सहितः श्रीमान् मन्त्रं चक्रे सुदुर्मतिः ॥

Then King Dasharatha, together with his ministers, deliberated upon the consecration of Rama.

7

ततः स राजा काकुत्स्थः सर्वान् मन्त्रिगणान् नृपः ।

समाहूय महातेजाः प्रोवाच मधुरं वचः ॥

Then the greatly radiant Kakutstha king summoned all his ministers and spoke sweet words to them.

8

अद्य रामं महात्मानं युवराजं करिष्यति ।

इत्युक्त्वा मन्त्रिणः सर्वान् प्रहृष्टो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥

“Today I shall appoint the great-souled Rama as the crown prince.” Having said this, the delighted king addressed all the ministers.

9

ततः सर्वे महात्मानो मन्त्रिणः प्रत्यपूजयन् ।

राजानं हृष्टमनसः साधु साध्विति चाब्रुवन् ॥

Then all the great-souled ministers honoured the king and, with delighted minds, said, “Excellent! Excellent!”

10

ततः राजा दशरथः कौसल्यां च यशस्विनीम् ।

कैकेयीं च सुमित्रां च आहूयेदं वचोऽब्रवीत् ॥

Then King Dasharatha summoned the illustrious Kausalya, Kaikeyi and Sumitra and spoke these words.

11

रामस्य युवराजत्वे ममाभीष्टं हि निश्चितम् ।

तदर्थं भवतीभिश्च यतितव्यं यथाविधि ॥

“My decision to make Rama the crown prince is firm. Therefore, you should all make the necessary preparations according to the proper rites.”

12

ततः सर्वाः स्त्रियः श्रुत्वा राज्ञो वचनमुत्तमम् ।

प्रहृष्टमनसः सर्वा रामस्य प्रियकाम्यया ॥

Hearing the king’s excellent words, all the women, wishing for Rama’s welfare, became delighted.

13

ततः पुरोहितः श्रीमान् वसिष्ठो भगवानृषिः ।

राममामन्त्रयामास युवराज्याय धीमतः ॥

Then the illustrious and venerable sage Vasistha, the royal priest, summoned the wise Rama for his consecration as crown prince.

14

ततो रामः प्रसन्नात्मा वसिष्ठं प्रत्यपूजयत् ।

प्रणम्य शिरसा तस्य वचनं प्रत्यपद्यत ॥

Then Rama, with a serene mind, honoured Vasistha, bowed his head before him and accepted his words.

15

ततो रामो महातेजा लक्ष्मणेन सहानघः ।

उपवासं तदा चक्रे यौवराज्यार्थमात्मवान् ॥

Then the greatly radiant and sinless Rama, together with Lakshmana, observed a fast in preparation for his installation as crown prince.

16

ततः सीता महाभागा रामस्य प्रियकाम्यया ।

उपवासं चकाराथ सह रामेण भामिनी ॥

Then the greatly fortunate Sita, desiring Rama’s welfare, also observed the fast together with Rama.

17

ततः प्रभाते विमले कृतकर्मा नराधिपः ।

वसिष्ठं प्रेषयामास रामस्याभ्युदयाय च ॥

At the clear dawn, after completing his duties, the king sent Vasistha to make preparations for Rama’s prosperity and consecration.

18

ततो वसिष्ठो भगवान् रामं दशरथात्मजम् ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं सर्वकार्येषु तत्त्ववित् ॥

Then the venerable Vasistha, knower of the proper conduct of all affairs, spoke sweet words to Rama, the son of Dasharatha.

19

अद्य राम महाबाहो युवराज्याभिषेचनम् ।

सम्प्राप्तं तव धर्मज्ञ राज्ञश्चाभीप्सितं महत् ॥

“O mighty-armed Rama, O knower of righteousness, today your consecration as crown prince has been arranged, and this is the king’s great desire.”

20

ततः प्रहृष्टो रामस्तु पितुर्वचनमब्रवीत् ।

यथाज्ञापयसे राजन् तथा करिष्यते मया ॥

Then delighted Rama replied to his father: “O king, I shall do exactly as you command.”

21

ततो दशरथः श्रीमान् कैकेयीमन्तरं गृहम् ।

प्रविश्य दुःखसंतप्तो रामं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥

Then the illustrious Dasharatha entered Kaikeyi’s chamber, overwhelmed with sorrow, and spoke to Rama.

22

राम राम महाबाहो न शक्नोमि वचः प्रभो ।

वक्तुं दुःखाभिसंतप्तो मनसा च विचेतसा ॥

“Rama, O mighty-armed one, I am unable to speak to you, O lord, being afflicted with sorrow and distressed in mind.”

23

ततः कैकेयी वचनं रामाय मधुरं ददौ ।

पितुर्वचनमाज्ञाय वनं गन्तुमर्हसि ॥

Then Kaikeyi spoke to Rama: “Knowing your father’s command, you must go to the forest.”

24

रामोऽपि तद्वचः श्रुत्वा कैकेय्या दुःखितस्य च ।

पितुश्च वचनं श्रुत्वा वनवासाय निश्चितः ॥

Hearing Kaikeyi’s words and seeing his father afflicted with sorrow, Rama accepted his father’s command and resolved to go into exile.

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे षट्सप्ततितमः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the seventy-sixth Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
