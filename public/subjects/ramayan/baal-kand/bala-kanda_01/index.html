Ramayan — Bala Kanda — Sarga 01

Ramayan Bala Kanda

Verse 1

तपः स्वाध्याय निरताम् तपस्वी वाग्विदाम् वरम् | नारदम् परिपप्रच्छ वाल्मीकिः मुनि पुंगवम् || १-१-१

Valmiki, devoted to austerity and self-study, asked Narada, the foremost among sages and the best among those skilled in speech.

Verse 2

कः नु अस्मिन् सांप्रतम् लोके गुणवान् कः च वीर्यवान् | धर्मज्ञः च कृतज्ञः च सत्य वाक्यो धृढ व्रतः || १-१-२

Who in this world at the present time is endowed with virtues and strength? Who knows righteousness, who is grateful, who speaks the truth, and who is firm in his vows?

Verse 3

चारित्रेण च को युक्तः सर्व भूतेषु को हितः | विद्वान् कः कः समर्थः च कः च एक प्रिय दर्शनः || १-१-३

Who is endowed with excellent character? Who works for the welfare of all beings? Who is learned, who is capable, and who is pleasing in appearance?

Verse 4

आत्मवान् को जित क्रोधो द्युतिमान् कः अनसूयकः | कस्य बिभ्यति देवाः च जात रोषस्य संयुगे || १-१-४

Who is self-controlled? Who has conquered anger? Who is radiant and free from jealousy? And when such a person becomes angry in battle, whom do even the gods fear?

Verse 5

एतत् इच्छामि अहम् श्रोतुम् परम् कौतूहलम् हि मे | महर्षे त्वम् समर्थोऽसि ज्ञातुम् एवम् विधम् नरम् || १-१-५

I wish to hear about such a person, for I have great curiosity. O great sage, you are capable of knowing such a man.

Verse 6

श्रुत्वा च एतत् त्रिलोकज्ञो वाल्मीकेः नारदो वचः | श्रूयताम् इति च आमंत्र्य प्रहृष्टो वाक्यम् अब्रवीत् || १-१-६

Hearing these words of Valmiki, Narada, the knower of the three worlds, gladly addressed him and said, “Listen.”

Verse 7

बहवो दुर्लभाः च एव ये त्वया कीर्तिता गुणाः | मुने वक्ष्ष्यामि अहम् बुद्ध्वा तैः उक्तः श्रूयताम् नरः || १-१-७

O sage, the qualities you have described are many and difficult to find. I shall tell you of a man who possesses them. Listen to me.

Verse 8

इक्ष्वाकु वंश प्रभवो रामो नाम जनैः श्रुतः | नियत आत्मा महावीर्यो द्युतिमान् धृतिमान् वशी || १-१-८

He is Rama, known among the people, born in the Ikshvaku dynasty. He is self-controlled, greatly powerful, radiant, steadfast, and master of himself.

Verse 9

बुद्धिमान् नीतिमान् वाङ्ग्मी श्रीमान् शत्रु निबर्हणः | विपुलांसो महाबाहुः कंबु ग्रीवो महाहनुः || १-१-९

He is intelligent, wise in conduct, eloquent, glorious, and destroyer of enemies. His shoulders are broad, his arms are mighty, his neck is shaped like a conch, and his cheeks are prominent.

Verse 10

महोरस्को महेष्वासो गूढ जत्रुः अरिन्दमः | आजानु बाहुः सुशिराः सुललाटः सुविक्रमः || १-१-१०

He is broad-chested, mighty with the bow, with well-covered collarbones and a subduer of enemies. His arms reach to his knees, his head is well formed, his forehead is broad, and his stride is majestic.

Verse 11

समः सम विभक्त अंगः स्निग्ध वर्णः प्रतापवान् | पीन वक्षा विशालाक्षो लक्ष्मीवान् शुभ लक्षणः || १-१-११

His body is proportionately formed, with evenly developed limbs. His complexion is smooth and radiant. He is powerful, broad-chested, wide-eyed, prosperous, and marked with auspicious signs.

Verse 12

धर्मज्ञः सत्य सन्धः च प्रजानाम् च हिते रतः | यशस्वी ज्ञान संपन्नः शुचिः वश्यः समाधिमान् || १-१-१२

He knows righteousness, is true to his word, and is devoted to the welfare of his subjects. He is renowned, endowed with knowledge, pure in conduct, self-controlled, and composed.

Verse 13

प्रजापति समः श्रीमान् धता रिपु निषूदनः | रक्षिता जीवलोकस्य धर्मस्य परि रक्षिता || १-१-१३

He is like Prajapati, glorious and the sustainer of the world. He destroys enemies and protects the world of living beings. He is a protector of righteousness.

Verse 14

रक्षिता स्वस्य धर्मस्य स्व जनस्य च रक्षिता | वेद वेदाङ्ग तत्त्वज्ञो धनुर् वेदे च निष्ठितः || १-१-१४

He protects his own righteousness and protects his own people. He knows the essence of the Vedas and their auxiliary disciplines, and he is accomplished in the science of archery.

Verse 15

सर्व शास्त्र अर्थ तत्त्वज्ञो स्मृतिमान् प्रतिभानवान् | सर्वलोक प्रियः साधुः अदीनाअत्मा विचक्षणः || १-१-१५

He knows the essence and meaning of all the scriptures. He possesses a strong memory and great intelligence. He is beloved by all people, gentle, undismayed, and discerning.

Verse 16

सर्वदा अभिगतः सद्भिः समुद्र इव सिन्धुभिः | अर्यः सर्वसमः च एव सदैव प्रिय दर्शनः || १-१-१६

As rivers always approach the ocean, so the good always approach him. He is noble, treats all equally, and is always pleasing to behold.

Verse 17

स च सर्व गुणोपेतः कौसल्य आनंद वर्धनः | समुद्र इव गाम्भीर्ये धैर्येण हिमवान् इव || १-१-१७

He is endowed with every virtue and increases the joy of Kausalya. In depth he is like the ocean, and in steadfastness he is like the Himalaya.

Verse 18

विष्णुना सदृशो वीर्ये सोमवत् प्रिय दर्शनः | काल अग्नि सदृशः क्रोधे क्षमया पृथ्वी समः || १-१-१८

In strength he is comparable to Vishnu. In appearance he is pleasing like the moon. In anger he is like the fire at the end of time, and in forgiveness he is like the earth.

Verse 19

धनदेन समः त्यागे सत्ये धर्म इव अपरः | तम् एवम् गुण संपन्नम् रामम् सत्य पराक्रमम् || १-१-१९

In generosity he is equal to Kubera, and in truthfulness he is like Dharma himself. Such is Rama, endowed with these qualities and possessing true valor.

Verse 20

ज्येष्टम् श्रेष्ट गुणैः युक्तम् प्रियम् दशरथः सुतम् | प्रकृतीनाम् हितैः युक्तम् प्रकृति प्रिय कांयया || १-१-२०

Dasharatha regarded Rama, his eldest son, endowed with the highest qualities and dear to him, as devoted to the welfare of the people and beloved by the subjects.

Verse 21

यौव राज्येन संयोक्तुम् ऐच्छत् प्रीत्या महीपतिः | तस्य अभिषेक संभारान् दृष्ट्वा भार्या अथ कैकयी || १-१-२१

The king desired, with affection, to install Rama as heir to the kingdom. When his wife Kaikeyi saw the preparations for Rama’s consecration,

Verse 22

पूर्वम् दत्त वरा देवी वरम् एनम् अयाचत | विवासनम् च रामस्य भरतस्य अभिषेचनम् || १-१-२२

the queen, who had previously been granted two boons, asked for those boons: the exile of Rama and the consecration of Bharata as king.

Verse 23

स सत्य वचनात् राजा धर्म पाशेन संयतः | विवासयामास सुतम् रामम् दशरथः प्रियम् || १-१-२३

Bound by his word and bound by righteousness, King Dasharatha exiled his beloved son Rama to the forest.

Verse 24

स जगाम वनम् वीरः प्रतिज्ञाम् अनुपालयन् | पितुर् वचन निर्देशात् कैकेय्याः प्रिय कारणात् || १-१-२४

That heroic Rama went to the forest, fulfilling the promise, obeying his father’s command, and acting for the sake of fulfilling Kaikeyi’s request.

Verse 25

तम् व्रजंतम् प्रियो भ्राता लक्ष्मणः अनुजगाम ह | स्नेहात् विनय संपन्नः सुमित्र आनंद वर्धनः || १-१-२५

Lakshmana, his dear brother, followed Rama as he departed. Endowed with humility and affection, he followed his brother out of love, bringing joy to his mother Sumitra.

Verse 26

रामस्य दयिता भार्या नित्यं प्राणसमा हिता | जनकस्य कुले जाता देवमायेव निर्मिता | सर्वलक्षण संपन्ना नारीणाम् उत्तमा वधूः || १-१-२६, २७

Sita, the beloved wife of Rama, was always dear to him as his own life. Born in the family of Janaka, she appeared as though created by divine power. Endowed with every auspicious quality, she was the foremost among women.

Verse 28

सीताप्यनुगता रामं शशिनं रोहिणी यथा | पौरैरनुगतो दूरं पित्रा दशरथेन च || १-१-२८

Sita too followed Rama, just as Rohini follows the Moon. The citizens followed him for a long distance, and his father Dasharatha also followed him.

Verse 29

शृङ्गिबेरपुरे सूतं गङ्गाकूले व्यसर्जयत् | गुहमासाद्य धर्मात्मा निषादाधिपतिं प्रियम् || १-१-२९

At Sringaverapura, on the bank of the Ganga, Rama sent back the charioteer. He approached Guha, the beloved king of the Nishadas.

Verse 30

गुहेन सहितो रामो लक्ष्मणेन च सीतया | ते वनेन वनं गत्वा नदीस्तीर्त्वा बहूदकाः || १-१-३०

Rama, together with Guha, Lakshmana, and Sita, proceeded onward. They travelled from forest to forest and crossed rivers filled with abundant waters.

Verse 31

चित्रकूटमनुप्राप्य भरद्वाजस्य शासनात् | रम्यमावसथं कृत्वा रममाणा वने त्रयः | देवगन्धर्वसङ्काशास्तत्र ते न्यवसन् सुखम् || १-१-३१

Having reached Chitrakuta according to the direction of Bharadwaja, the three made a beautiful dwelling there. Resembling the gods and Gandharvas, they lived happily in the forest.

Verse 32

चित्रकूटं गते रामे पुत्रशोकातुरस्तथा | राजा दशरथः स्वर्गं जगाम विलपन् सुतम् || १-१-३२

When Rama had gone to Chitrakuta, King Dasharatha, overcome with grief for his son, departed to heaven while lamenting for him.

Verse 33

मृते तु तस्मिन् भरतो वसिष्ठप्रमुखैर्द्विजैः | नियुज्यमानो राज्याय नैच्छद्राज्यं महाबलः || १-१-३३

After Dasharatha had died, the mighty Bharata was urged by the Brahmins led by Vasistha to take the kingdom, but he did not desire the kingdom.

Verse 34

स जगाम वनं वीरो रामपादप्रसादकः || १-१-३४

The heroic Bharata went to the forest to seek the grace of Rama’s feet.

Verse 35

गत्वा तु सुमहात्मानं रामं सत्यपराक्रमम् | अयाचद्भ्रातरं राममार्यभावपुरस्कृतः | त्वमेव राजा धर्मज्ञ इति रामं वचोऽब्रवीत् || १-१-३५

Having reached the great-souled and truly valorous Rama, Bharata, with reverence, entreated his brother Rama. He said to Rama, “You alone are the king, O knower of righteousness.”

Verse 36

रामोऽपि परमोदारः सुमुखः सुमहायशाः | न चैच्छत्पितुरादेशाद्राज्यं रामो महाबलः || १-१-३६

Rama, though exceedingly generous, gracious, greatly renowned, and mighty, did not desire the kingdom because of his father’s command.

Verse 37

पादुके चास्य राज्याय न्यासं दत्त्वा पुनः पुनः | निवर्तयामास ततो भरतं भरताग्रजः || १-१-३७

The elder brother of Bharata gave his sandals to him as a trust for the kingdom and repeatedly persuaded Bharata to return.

Verse 38

स काममनवाप्यैव रामपादावुपस्पृशन् | नन्दिग्रामेऽकरोद्राज्यं रामागमनकाङ्क्षया || १-१-३८

Having failed to obtain his desire, Bharata touched Rama’s feet and, longing for Rama’s return, ruled the kingdom from Nandigrama.

Verse 39

गते तु भरते श्रीमान् सत्यसन्धो जितेन्द्रियः | रामस्तु पुनरालक्ष्य नागरस्य जनस्य च || १-१-३९

After Bharata had departed, the glorious Rama, firm in truth and master of his senses, perceived that the citizens might again come to him.

Verse 40

तत्रागमनमेकाग्रो दण्डकान्प्रविवेश ह || १-१-४०

With his mind firmly resolved, he entered the Dandaka forest.

Verse 41

प्रविश्य तु महारण्यं रामो राजीवलोचनः | विराधं राक्षसं हत्वा शरभङ्गं ददर्श ह | सुतीक्ष्णं चाप्यगस्त्यं च अगस्त्यभ्रातरं तथा || १-१-४१, ४२

Having entered the great forest, lotus-eyed Rama killed the Rakshasa Viradha and saw the sages Sarabhanga, Sutikshna, Agastya, and the brother of Agastya.

Verse 42

अगस्त्यवचनाच्चैव जग्राहैन्द्रं शरासनम् | खड्गं च परमप्रीतस्तूणी चाक्षयसायकौ || १-१-४२

At the instruction of Agastya, Rama received the bow of Indra. With great delight he also received a sword and two quivers containing inexhaustible arrows.

Verse 43

वसतस्तस्य रामस्य वने वनचरैः सह | ऋषयोऽभ्यागमन् सर्वे वधायासुररक्षसाम् || १-१-४३

While Rama was dwelling in the forest with the forest-dwellers, all the sages approached him for the destruction of the Asuras and Rakshasas.

Verse 44

स तेषां प्रतिशुश्राव राक्षसानां तथा वने | प्रतिज्ञातश्च रामेण वधः संयति रक्षसाम् || १-१-४४

Rama listened to their request concerning the Rakshasas in the forest and promised to destroy the Rakshasas in battle.

Verse 45

ऋषीणामग्निकल्पानां दण्डकारण्यवासिनाम् || १-१-४५

He made this promise to the sages dwelling in Dandakaranya, whose radiance was like that of fire.

Verse 46

तेन तत्रैव वसता जनस्थाननिवासिनी | विरूपिता शूर्पणखा राक्षसी कामरूपिणी || १-१-४६

While Rama was dwelling there, the Rakshasi Shurpanakha, a resident of Janasthana and capable of assuming different forms at will, was disfigured.

Verse 47

ततः शूर्पणखावाक्यादुद्युक्तान् सर्वराक्षसान् | खरं त्रिशिरसं चैव दूषणं चैव राक्षसम् | निजघान वने रामस्तेषां चैव पदानुगान् || १-१-४७

Then, provoked by the words of Shurpanakha, Rama killed in the forest all the Rakshasas who came prepared for battle, including Khara, Trishiras, and Dushana, together with their followers.

Verse 48

वने तस्मिन्निवसता जनस्थाननिवासिनाम् | रक्षसां निहतान्यासन् सहस्राणि चतुर्दश || १-१-४८

While living in that forest, Rama killed fourteen thousand Rakshasas who were inhabitants of Janasthana.

Verse 49

ततो ज्ञातिवधं श्रुत्वा रावणः क्रोधमूर्छितः | सहायं वरयामास मारीचं नाम राक्षसम् || १-१-४९

Hearing of the destruction of his kinsmen, Ravana became overwhelmed with anger and sought the assistance of a Rakshasa named Maricha.

Verse 50

वार्यमाणः सुबहुशो मारीचेन स रावणः | न विरोधो बलवता क्षमो रावण तेन ते || १-१-५०

Maricha repeatedly tried to dissuade Ravana, saying:

“O Ravana, hostility with that mighty one is not appropriate for you.”

Verse 51

अनादृत्य तु तद् वाक्यं रावणः काल चोदितः | जगाम सह मारीचः तस्य आश्रम पदं तदा ||

Disregarding those words, Ravana, driven onward by fate, then went together with Maricha to Rama’s hermitage.

Verse 52

तेन मायाविना दूरम् अपवाह्य नृप आत्मजौ | जहार भार्याम् रामस्य गृध्रम् हत्वा जटायुषम् ||

By means of the deceitful Maricha, the two princes were drawn far away, and Ravana abducted Rama’s wife after killing the vulture Jatayu.

Verse 53

गृध्रम् च निहतम् दृष्ट्वा हृताम् श्रुत्वा च मैथिलीम् | राघवः शोक संतप्तो विललाप आकुल इन्द्रियः ||

Seeing the vulture struck down and hearing that Maithili had been carried away, Raghava, overwhelmed with grief, lamented with his senses disturbed.

Verse 54

ततः तेन एव शोकेन गृध्रम् दग्ध्वा जटायुषम् | मार्गमाणो वने सीताम् राक्षसम् संददर्श ह ||

Then, consumed by that grief, Rama cremated Jatayu and, while searching for Sita in the forest, saw a Rakshasa named Kabandha.

Verse 55

कबंधम् नाम रूपेण विकृतम् घोर दर्शनम् |

He saw Kabandha, whose form was deformed and whose appearance was terrifying.

Verse 56

तम् निहत्य महाबाहुः ददाह स्वर्गतः च सः | स च अस्य कथयामास शबरीम् धर्म चारिणीम् ||

The mighty-armed Rama killed him and performed his cremation. As Kabandha proceeded to heaven, he told Rama about Shabari, the righteous ascetic woman.

Verse 57

श्रमणाम् धर्म निपुणाम् अभिगच्छ इति राघव | सः अभ्य गच्छन् महातेजाः शबरीम् शत्रु सूदनः ||

“Go to the ascetic Shabari, who is devoted to righteousness and accomplished in Dharma.”

The greatly radiant Rama, destroyer of enemies, then approached Shabari.

Verse 58

शबर्या पूजितः संयक् रामो दशरथ आत्मजः | पंपा तीरे हनुमता संगतो वानरेण ह ||

Rama, the son of Dasharatha, was properly received and honoured by Shabari. On the bank of Lake Pampa he met Hanuman, the Vanara.

Verse 59

हनुमत् वचनात् च एव सुग्रीवेण समागतः | सुग्रीवाय च तत् सर्वम् शंसद् रामो महाबलः ||

At Hanuman’s word, Rama met Sugriva. The mighty Rama told Sugriva everything that had happened.

Verse 60

आदितः तत् यथा वृत्तम् सीतायाः च विशेषतः | सुग्रीवः च अपि तत् सर्वम् श्रुत्वा रामस्य वानरः ||

He narrated the events from the beginning, especially what had happened to Sita. Sugriva, the Vanara, heard all of this from Rama.

Verse 61

चकार सख्यम् रामेण प्रीतः च एव अग्नि साक्षिकम् | ततः वानर राजेन वैर अनुकथनम् प्रति ||

Sugriva, pleased, made friendship with Rama, with the sacred fire as witness. Then the Vanara king narrated the story of his enmity.

Verse 62

रामाय आवेदितम् सर्वम् प्रणयात् दुःखितेन च | प्रतिज्ञातम् च रामेण तदा वालि वधम् प्रति ||

The sorrowful Sugriva, in friendship, told Rama everything. Rama then promised to kill Vali.

Verse 63

वालिनः च बलम् तत्र कथयामास वानरः | सुग्रीवः शंकितः च आसीत् नित्यम् वीर्येण राघवे ||

Sugriva described Vali’s strength. He remained constantly doubtful about the power of Raghava.

Verse 64

राघवः प्रत्ययार्थम् तु दुंदुभेः कायम् उत्तमम् | दर्शयामास सुग्रीवः महापर्वत संनिभम् ||

To give Sugriva confidence, Sugriva showed Rama the enormous body of Dundubhi, which resembled a great mountain.

Verse 65

उत्स्मयित्वा महाबाहुः प्रेक्ष्य च अस्ति महाबलः | पाद अंगुष्ठेन चिक्षेप संपूर्णम् दश योजनम् ||

The mighty-armed Rama looked at the remains and smiled. Then the powerful Rama kicked them with the tip of his toe and sent them a distance of ten yojanas.

Verse 66

बिभेद च पुनः सालान् सप्त एकेन महा इषुणा | गिरिम् रसातलम् चैव जनयन् प्रत्ययम् तथा ||

Again, with a single great arrow, he pierced seven Sala trees, a mountain, and the depths of the earth, thereby giving Sugriva confidence.

Verse 67

ततः प्रीत मनाः तेन विश्वस्तः स महाकपिः | किष्किंधाम् राम सहितो जगाम च गुहाम् तदा ||

Then the great Vanara Sugriva became pleased and gained confidence in Rama. Together with Rama, he went toward the cave-like city of Kishkindha.

Verse 68

ततः अगर्जत् हरिवरः सुग्रीवो हेम पिंगलः | तेन नादेन महता निर्जगाम हरीश्वरः ||

Then the foremost of the Vanaras, Sugriva, whose complexion was golden-brown, roared loudly. Hearing that great roar, the lord of the Vanaras, Vali, came out.

Verse 69

अनुमान्य तदा ताराम् सुग्रीवेण समागतः | निजघान च तत्र एनम् शरेण एकेन राघवः ||

After taking leave of Tara, Vali came forth and confronted Sugriva. There, Raghava killed him with a single arrow.

Verse 70

ततः सुग्रीव वचनात् हत्वा वालिनम् आहवे | सुग्रीवम् एव तत् राज्ये राघवः प्रत्यपादयत् ||

Having killed Vali in battle at Sugriva’s request, Raghava established Sugriva in that kingdom.

Verse 71

स च सर्वान् समानीय वानरान् वानरर्षभः | दिशः प्रस्थापयामास दिदृक्षुः जनक आत्मजाम् ||

Then Sugriva, the foremost of the Vanaras, summoned all the Vanaras and sent them in all directions in search of Janaka’s daughter.

Verse 72

ततः गृध्रस्य वचनात् संपातेः हनुमान् बली | शत योजन विस्तीर्णम् पुप्लुवे लवण अर्णवम् ||

Then, following the words of the vulture Sampati, the mighty Hanuman leapt across the salt ocean, which extended for one hundred yojanas.

Verse 73

तत्र लंकाम् समासाद्य पुरीम् रावण पालिताम् | ददर्श सीताम् ध्यायन्तीम् अशोक वनिकाम् गताम् ||

Having reached Lanka, the city ruled by Ravana, he saw Sita in the Ashoka grove, absorbed in thought.

Verse 74

निवेदयित्वा अभिज्ञानम् प्रवृत्तिम् च निवेद्य च | समाश्वास्य च वैदेहीम् मर्दयामास तोरणम् ||

After presenting the identifying token and informing Vaidehi about Rama’s condition, and after giving her reassurance, Hanuman began to destroy the gateway of the Ashoka grove.

Verse 75

पंच सेन अग्रगान् हत्वा सप्त मंत्रि सुतान् अपि | शूरम् अक्षम् च निष्पिष्य ग्रहणम् समुपागमत् ||

Having killed five commanders of the army and also seven sons of ministers, and having crushed the heroic Aksha, Hanuman allowed himself to be captured.

Verse 76

अस्त्रेण उन्मुक्तम् आत्मानम् ज्ञात्वा पैतामहात् वरात् | मर्षयन् राक्षसान् वीरो यन्त्रिणः तान् यदृच्छया ||

Knowing through the boon granted by Brahma that he could free himself from the weapon, the heroic Hanuman deliberately tolerated the Rakshasas who bound him.

Verse 77

ततो दग्ध्वा पुरीम् लंकाम् ऋते सीताम् च मैथिलीम् | रामाय प्रियम् आख्यातुम् पुनः आयात् महाकपिः ||

Then, having burned the city of Lanka, except for the place where Maithili Sita was staying, the great Vanara returned to tell Rama the joyful news.

Verse 78

सः अभिगंय महात्मानम् कृत्वा रामम् प्रदक्षिणम् | न्यवेदयत् अमेयात्मा दृष्टा सीता इति तत्त्वतः ||

The immeasurable-minded Hanuman approached the great-souled Rama, circumambulated him, and truthfully reported, “Sita has been seen.”

Verse 79

ततः सुग्रीव सहितो गत्वा तीरम् महा उदधेः | समुद्रम् क्षोभयामास शरैः आदित्य सन्निभैः ||

Then, together with Sugriva, Rama went to the shore of the great ocean and agitated the ocean with arrows resembling the rays of the sun.

Verse 80

दर्शयामास च आत्मानम् समुद्रः सरिताम् पतिः | समुद्र वचनात् च एव नलम् सेतुम् अकारयत् ||

The lord of the rivers, the Ocean, revealed himself. At the Ocean’s instruction, Rama caused Nala to construct a bridge across the sea.

Verse 81

तेन गत्वा पुरीम् लंकाम् हत्वा रावणम् आहवे | रामः सीताम् अनुप्राप्य पराम् व्रीडाम् उपागमत् ||

By that bridge Rama went to the city of Lanka and killed Ravana in battle. Having recovered Sita, he subsequently experienced great distress.

Verse 82

ताम् उवाच ततः रामः परुषम् जन संसदि | अमृष्यमाणा सा सीता विवेश ज्वलनम् सती ||

Then Rama spoke harsh words to Sita in the assembly. Unable to bear those words, the virtuous Sita entered the blazing fire.

Verse 83–84

ततः अग्नि वचनात् सीताम् ज्ञात्वा विगत कल्मषाम् | कर्मणा तेन महता त्रैलोक्यम् स चराचरम् | स देवर्षि गणम् तुष्टम् राघवस्य महात्मनः || बभौ रामः संप्रहृष्टः पूजितः सर्व देवतैः ||

Then, through the words of Agni, Rama came to know that Sita was free from all impurity. By that great deed of the great-souled Raghava, the three worlds, together with all their moving and unmoving beings and the hosts of divine sages, became pleased.

Rama himself became greatly joyful and shone forth, honoured by all the gods.

Verse 85

अभ्यषिच्य च लंकायाम् राक्षस इन्द्रम् विभीषणम् | कृतकृत्यः तदा रामो विज्वरः प्रमुमोद ह ||

Having consecrated Vibhishana as the lord of the Rakshasas in Lanka, Rama, considering his task accomplished, became free from anxiety and rejoiced greatly.

Verse 86

देवताभ्यो वराम् प्राप्य समुत्थाप्य च वानरान् | अयोध्याम् प्रस्थितः रामः पुष्पकेण सुहृत् वृतः ||

Having obtained a boon from the gods and restored the fallen Vanaras to life, Rama departed for Ayodhya in the Pushpaka vehicle, surrounded by his friends.

Verse 87

भरद्वाज आश्रमम् गत्वा रामः सत्यपराक्रमः | भरतस्य अंतिकम् रामो हनूमंतम् व्यसर्जयत् ||

Rama, whose valour was founded upon truth, went to the hermitage of Bharadwaja and sent Hanuman ahead to Bharata.

Verse 88

पुनः आख्यायिकाम् जल्पन् सुग्रीव सहितः तदा | पुष्पकम् तत् समारूह्य नंदिग्रामम् ययौ तदा ||

Then, together with Sugriva, Rama again boarded the Pushpaka and went to Nandigrama, speaking with his companions about the events that had taken place.

Verse 89

नंदिग्रामे जटाम् हित्वा भ्रातृभिः सहितो अनघः | रामः सीताम् अनुप्राप्य राज्यम् पुनः अवाप्तवान् ||

At Nandigrama, the sinless Rama, together with his brothers, removed his matted locks. Having regained Sita, Rama again obtained his kingdom.

Verse 90

प्रहृष्टो मुदितो लोकः तुष्टः पुष्टः सुधार्मिकः | निरामयो हि अरोगः च दुर्भिक्ष भय वर्जितः ||

The people became delighted and joyful, contented and prosperous, and devoted to righteousness. They were free from troubles and disease and were without fear of famine.

Verse 91

न पुत्र मरणम् केचित् द्रक्ष्यन्ति पुरुषाः क्वचित् | नार्यः च अविधवा नित्यम् भविष्यन्ति पति व्रताः ||

No man anywhere would witness the death of his son. The women would remain devoted to their husbands and would not become widows.

Verse 92–93a

न च अग्निजम् भयम् किन्चित् न अप्सु मज्जन्ति जन्तवः | न वातजम् भयम् किन्चित् न अपि ज्वर कृतम् तथा || न च अपि क्षुत् भयम् तत्र न तस्कर भयम् तथा ||

There would be no fear arising from fire. Living beings would not perish in the waters. There would be no fear caused by violent winds and no fear caused by disease.

There would be no fear of hunger and no fear of thieves.

Verse 93b–94a

नगराणि च राष्ट्राणि धन धान्य युतानि च | नित्यम् प्रमुदिताः सर्वे यथा कृत युगे तथा ||

The cities and kingdoms would be filled with wealth and grain. All the people would always remain joyful, just as they had been during the Krita Yuga.

Verse 94b–95a

अश्वमेध शतैः इष्ट्वा तथा बहु सुवर्णकैः | गवाम् कोट्ययुतम् दत्त्वा विद्वभ्यो विधि पूर्वकम् | असंख्येयम् धनम् दत्त्वा ब्राह्मणेभो महायशाः ||

The greatly renowned Rama would perform hundreds of Ashvamedha sacrifices and other sacrifices involving abundant gold.

He would give millions upon millions of cows to learned people according to the prescribed rites and would give immeasurable wealth to the Brahmanas.

Verse 96

राज वंशान् शत गुणान् स्थाप इष्यति राघवः | चातुर् वर्ण्यम् च लोके अस्मिन् स्वे स्वे धर्मे नियोक्ष्यति ||

Raghava would establish hundreds of royal lineages and would establish the four social orders in this world in their respective duties.

Verse 97

दश वर्ष सहस्राणि दश वर्ष शतानि च | रामो राज्यम् उपासित्वा ब्रह्म लोकम् प्रयास्यति ||

Having presided over the kingdom for ten thousand years and another thousand years, Rama would depart to the world of Brahma.

Verse 98

इदम् पवित्रम् पापघ्नम् पुण्यम् वेदैः च संमितम् | यः पठेत् राम चरितम् सर्व पापैः प्रमुच्यते ||

This account of Rama is sacred, destroys sin, is meritorious, and accords with the Vedas. Whoever reads the story of Rama is freed from all sins.

Verse 99

एतत् आख्यानम् आयुष्यम् पठन् रामायणम् नरः | स पुत्र पौत्रः स गणः प्रेत्य स्वर्गे महीयते ||

A person who reads this life-giving narrative, the Ramayana, will live with his sons, grandsons, and companions, and after death will be honoured in heaven.

Verse 100

पठन् द्विजो वाक् ऋषभत्वम् ईयात् | स्यात् क्षत्रियो भूमि पतित्वम् ईयात् || वणिक् जनः पण्य फलत्वम् ईयात् | जनः च शूद्रो अपि महत्त्वम् ईयात् ||

A Brahmana who reads it will attain excellence in speech.

A Kshatriya will attain lordship over the earth.

A Vaishya will attain the fruits of his commerce.

And a Shudra too will attain greatness.
