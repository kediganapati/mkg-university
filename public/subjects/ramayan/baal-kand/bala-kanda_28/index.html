Ramayan — Bala Kanda — Sarga 28

Ramayan Bala Kanda

Verse 1

प्रतिगृह्य ततोऽस्त्राणि प्रहृष्टवदनः शुचिः । गच्छन्नेव च काकुत्स्थो विश्वामित्रमथाब्रवीत् ॥

Having received the weapons, Rama, with a delighted face and purified, proceeded onward and spoke to Vishvamitra.

Verse 2

भगवन् के नु मन्त्राश्च बलामतिबलां तथा । याभ्यां न श्रमते रामो न ज्वरिष्यति वा क्वचित् ॥

“O Bhagavan, what are the mantras of Bala and Atibala by which Rama will never become fatigued or afflicted by fever?”

Verse 3

न चास्य सदृशो वीर्ये त्रिषु लोकेषु कश्चन । न चास्य सदृशो ज्ञाने तपसा वा पराक्रमे ॥

“No one in the three worlds will be equal to him in strength, nor equal to him in knowledge, austerity, or valor.”

Verse 4

कथं चैतत् समाख्यातं भगवन् ब्रह्मवादिभिः । एतदिच्छामि विज्ञातुं तत्त्वेन मुनिपुङ्गव ॥

“How have these teachings been described by the knowers of Brahman? O foremost of sages, I wish to know this in truth.”

Verse 5

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः । प्रहस्य राघवं वाक्यमुवाच मधुरं वचः ॥

Hearing his words, the great sage Vishvamitra smiled and spoke sweetly to Raghava.

Verse 6

श्रूयतां राम तत्त्वेन बलामतिबलां तथा । याभ्यां न श्रमते रामो न ज्वरिष्यति वा क्वचित् ॥

“Listen, Rama, to the truth concerning Bala and Atibala, by which a person will never become fatigued or afflicted by fever.”

Verse 7

न चास्य सदृशो वीर्ये त्रिषु लोकेषु कश्चन । न चास्य सदृशो ज्ञाने तपसा वा पराक्रमे ॥

“No one in the three worlds will equal him in strength, nor in knowledge, austerity, or valor.”

Verse 8

न सौभाग्ये न दाक्षिण्ये न ज्ञाने बुद्धिनिश्चये । नोत्तरे प्रतिवक्तव्ये समो लोके तवानघ ॥

“O sinless one, no one in the world will equal you in beauty, skill, knowledge, firmness of understanding, or ability to answer.”

Verse 9

एतद्विद्याद्वये लब्धे न भवेत्सदृशस्तव । बलामतिबलां चैव सर्वज्ञानस्य मातरौ ॥

“Having obtained these two teachings, no one will be your equal. Bala and Atibala are the mothers of all knowledge.”

Verse 10

क्षुत्पिपासे न ते राम भविष्येते नरोत्तम । बलामतिबलां चैव पठतः तात राघव ॥

“O Rama, best of men, hunger and thirst will not trouble you while you possess Bala and Atibala, my dear Raghava.”

Verse 11

गृहाण सर्वलोकस्य गुप्तये रघुनन्दन । विद्याद्वयमधीयाने यशश्चाथ भवेद्भुवि ॥

“Receive these two teachings for the protection of all the worlds, O delight of the Raghus. By learning these two teachings, you will attain fame upon the earth.”

Verse 12

पितामहसुते ह्येते विद्ये तेजःसमन्विते । प्रदातुं तव काकुत्स्थ सदृशस्त्वं हि पार्थिव ॥

“These two radiant teachings are the daughters of the Grandsire. O Kakutstha, O prince, you are worthy of receiving them.”

Verse 13

कामं बहुगुणाः सर्वे त्वय्येते नात्र संशयः । तपसा सम्भृते चैते बहुरूपे भविष्यतः ॥

“All these many qualities are already present in you; there is no doubt about this. When cultivated through austerity, these teachings will produce many forms of excellence.”

Verse 14

ततो रामो जलं स्पृष्ट्वा प्रहृष्टवदनः शुचिः । प्रतिजग्राह ते विद्ये महर्षेर्भावितात्मनः ॥

Then Rama touched the water, purified himself, and with a delighted face received those two teachings from the great sage whose mind was devoted to austerity.

Verse 15

विद्यासमुदितो रामः शुशुभे भीमविक्रमः । सहस्ररश्मिर्भगवान् शरदीव दिवाकरः ॥

Rama, endowed with terrible valor and radiant with the knowledge he had received, shone like the thousand-rayed Sun in autumn.

Verse 16

गुरुकार्याणि सर्वाणि नियुज्य कुशिकात्मजे । ऊषुस्तां रजनीं तत्र सरय्वां सुसुखं त्रयः ॥

Having performed all the duties owed to their teacher, the three of them spent that night happily on the bank of the Sarayu.

Verse 17

प्रभाते तु महातेजा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः । रामं च लक्ष्मणं चैव प्राबोधयत् शुभेक्षणौ ॥

At dawn, the greatly radiant great sage Vishvamitra awakened Rama and Lakshmana, the two of auspicious appearance.

Verse 18

कौसल्या सुप्रजा राम पूर्वा सन्ध्या प्रवर्तते । उत्तिष्ठ नरशार्दूल कर्तव्यं दैवमाह्निकम् ॥

“O Rama, Kausalya is blessed to have you as her son. The eastern dawn is appearing. Arise, O tiger among men; the morning duties to the gods are to be performed.”

Verse 19

उत्तिष्ठतौ नरव्याघ्रौ कर्तव्यं दैवमाह्निकम् । स्नात्वा तौ विधिवद् वीरौ जपेतां परमं जपम् ॥

“Arise, O tigers among men. The morning duties to the gods must be performed.” The two heroes bathed according to the prescribed rule and recited the sacred prayer.

Verse 20

कृतोदकौ तु तौ वीरौ कृतसन्ध्यौ महात्मनौ । उपेत्य मुनिशार्दूलं तस्थतुश्चारुदर्शनौ ॥

Having offered water and completed their morning twilight prayers, the two great-souled and handsome heroes approached the tiger among sages and stood before him.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे अष्टाविंशः सर्गः ॥

Thus ends the twenty-eighth Sarga of Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the first epic composed by Sage Valmiki.
