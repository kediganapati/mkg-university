Ramayan — Bala Kanda — Sarga 54

Ramayan Bala Kanda

Verse 1

कामधेनुं वसिष्ठोऽपि यदा न त्यजते मुनिः ।

तदास्य शबलां राम विश्वामित्रोऽन्वकर्षत ॥

When the sage Vasistha did not give up Kamadhenu, O Rama, Vishvamitra began to drag Shabala away.

Verse 2

नीयमाना तु शबला राम राज्ञा महात्मना ।

दुःखिता चिन्तयामास रुदती शोककर्शिता ॥

While being led away by the great king Vishvamitra, Shabala, distressed and afflicted with sorrow, began to lament and think.

Verse 3

परित्यक्ता वसिष्ठेन किमहं सुमहात्मना ।

या अहं राजभृतैर्दीना ह्रियेयं भृशदुःखिता ॥

“Have I been abandoned by the great-souled Vasistha, that I, helpless and greatly distressed, am being carried away by the king’s servants?”

Verse 4

किं मयापहृतं तस्य महर्षेर्भावितात्मनः ।

यन्मामनागसं भक्तामिष्टां त्यजति धार्मिकः ॥

“What wrong have I done to that great sage whose mind is purified by austerity, that the righteous one is abandoning me, who am innocent, devoted and dear to him?”

Verse 5

इति सा चिन्तयित्वा तु वसिष्ठं परमं गुरुम् ।

निश्चक्रामाश्रमात्तूर्णं रामं दृष्ट्वेव मैथिली ॥

Having thought thus of Vasistha, her supreme master, she quickly left the hermitage, as Maithili would later go forth upon seeing Rama.

Verse 6

साश्रुनेत्रा तु शबला वसिष्ठमुपसर्पती ।

निवारयन्ती राजर्षेर्बलमाह महात्मनः ॥

With tears in her eyes, Shabala approached Vasistha and spoke to the great sage about restraining the army of the royal sage.

Verse 7

किं मया ह्यपकृतं ते राज्ञा वा मुनिसत्तम ।

यन्मां नयति राजर्षिर्बलाद्बलसमन्वितः ॥

“O best of sages, what wrong have I done to you or to the king, that the royal sage, accompanied by his army, is forcibly taking me away?”

Verse 8

न त्वं त्यजसि मां ब्रह्मन् न चाहं त्वां जहाम्यहम् ।

त्वया सह मया नित्यं वत्स्यामि मुनिसत्तम ॥

“O Brahman, you are not abandoning me, nor will I abandon you. O best of sages, I shall always remain with you.”

Verse 9

एवं विलपमानां तु शबलां मुनिसत्तमः ।

वसिष्ठः सान्त्वयामास पुत्रीमिव सुमध्यमाम् ॥

As Shabala lamented thus, the excellent sage Vasistha consoled her as though she were his own daughter.

Verse 10

मा शुचः शबले भद्रे न त्वां त्यक्ष्यामि कर्हिचित् ।

बलाद्धि नयते राजा त्वां बलवान् ममाश्रमात् ॥

“Do not grieve, O auspicious Shabala. I shall never abandon you. The powerful king is forcibly taking you away from my hermitage.”

Verse 11

मया त्वं परिरक्ष्या वै न शक्या राजसत्तमात् ।

न हि शक्तोऽस्मि राजेन्द्र बलाद्रक्षितुमात्मनः ॥

“You must be protected by me, but I am unable to protect you from this excellent king. O king, I am not able to protect myself by force against him.”

Verse 12

सैन्येन महता राजन् त्वया मां बाधते बलात् ।

तद्बलं मे न शक्नोमि निवारयितुमद्य वै ॥

“O king, with your great army you are forcibly oppressing me. Today I am unable to restrain that army.”

Verse 13

ततः क्रुद्धा तु शबला वसिष्ठं प्रत्यभाषत ।

सृजस्व सैन्यं विपुलं त्वदर्थं मुनिसत्तम ॥

Then Shabala, becoming angry, replied to Vasistha, “O best of sages, create a vast army for your sake.”

Verse 14

तस्यास्तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा वसिष्ठो मुनिपुङ्गवः ।

सृजस्वेति तदा प्राह शबलां कामदुहां वराम् ॥

Hearing her words, Vasistha, the foremost of sages, said to Shabala, the excellent wish-fulfilling cow, “Create it.”

Verse 15

ततोऽसृजत तदा सैन्यं शबला कामदोहिनी ।

तस्याः स्तनात्समुद्भूताः पयोधरसमप्रभाः ॥

Then Shabala, the fulfiller of desires, created an army. From her udder emerged beings radiant like clouds.

Verse 16

पहलवाः शतशो राम कंबोजाश्च सहस्रशः ।

यवनाश्च शतशोऽथ शकाः परमशोभनाः ॥

O Rama, hundreds of Pahlavas, thousands of Kambojas, hundreds of Yavanas and splendid Shakas were produced.

Verse 17

ततोऽपराणि सैन्यानि नानादेशसमुद्भवानि ।

शबलायाः प्रसूतानि तद्राज्ञो बलमर्दयन् ॥

Then other armies originating from various regions were produced by Shabala, and they destroyed the king’s army.

Verse 18

तद्बलं बलवद्भीमं राज्ञो विश्वामित्रस्य ह ।

विनाशयद्भृशं तत्र शबलासृजितं महत् ॥

The mighty and formidable army of King Vishvamitra was then greatly destroyed by the vast forces created by Shabala.

Verse 19

ततः क्रुद्धो महातेजा विश्वामित्रोऽभ्यभाषत ।

वसिष्ठं मुनिशार्दूलं हन्यतामिति तद्बलम् ॥

Then the greatly radiant Vishvamitra became angry and said to Vasistha, the tiger among sages, “Let this army be destroyed.”

Verse 20

ततो राजर्षिसैन्येन शबलायाः सुदारुणम् ।

युद्धमासीत्सुदुर्धर्षं देवासुररणोपमम् ॥

Then a terrible and extremely fierce battle took place between the royal sage’s army and Shabala’s forces, resembling the battle between the gods and Asuras.

Verse 21

तैः आसीत् संवृता भूमिः शकैर्यवनमिश्रितैः ।

प्रभावद्भिर्महावीर्यैर्हेमकिंजल्कसन्निभैः ॥

The earth became covered with Shakas mixed with Yavanas, powerful and immensely valiant, whose appearance was like the golden filaments of flowers.

Verse 22

दीर्घासिपट्टिशधरैर्हेमवर्णाम्बरावृतैः ।

निर्दग्धं तत् बलं सर्वं प्रदीप्तैरिव पावकैः ॥

Wielding long swords and broad lances and clad in golden-coloured garments, they completely destroyed that entire army, like blazing fires.

Verse 23

ततोऽस्त्राणि महातेजा विश्वामित्रो मुमोच ह ।

तैस्तैर्यवनकाम्बोजा बर्बराश्चाकुलीकृताः ॥

Then the greatly radiant Vishvamitra released his weapons, by which the Yavanas, Kambojas and Barbaras were thrown into confusion.

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे चतुःपञ्चाशः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the fifty-fourth Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
