Ramayan — Bala Kanda — Sarga 22

Ramayan Bala Kanda

Verse 1

तथा वसिष्ठे ब्रुवति राजा दशरथः स्वयम् । प्रहृष्टवदनो राममाजुहाव सलक्ष्मणम् ॥

When Vashishtha spoke thus, King Dasharatha himself, with a delighted face, called Rama together with Lakshmana.

Verse 2

कृतः स्वस्त्ययनं मात्रा पित्रा दशरथेन च । पुरोधसा वसिष्ठेन मङ्गलैरभिमन्त्रितम् ॥

Rama was blessed for his auspicious journey by his mother and by his father, King Dasharatha, and was consecrated with auspicious rites by the royal priest Vashishtha.

Verse 3

स पुत्रं मूर्ध्न्युपाघ्राय राजा दशरथस्तदा । ददौ कुशिकपुत्राय सुप्रीतेनान्तरात्मना ॥

Then King Dasharatha kissed his son on the head and, with a joyful heart, entrusted him to the son of Kushika, Vishvamitra.

Verse 4

ततो वायुः सुखस्पर्शो नीरजस्को ववौ तदा । विश्वामित्रगतं रामं दृष्ट्वा राजीवलोचनम् ॥

Then, seeing the lotus-eyed Rama proceeding with Vishvamitra, a pleasant and dustless breeze began to blow.

Verse 5

पुष्पवृष्टिर्महत्यासीद्देवदुन्दुभिनिःस्वनैः । शङ्खदुन्दुभिनिर्घोषः प्रयाते तु महात्मनि ॥

As the great-souled Rama departed, there was a great shower of flowers, accompanied by the sounds of divine drums and the loud sounds of conches and drums.

Verse 6

विश्वामित्रो ययावग्रे ततो रामो महायशाः । काकपक्षधरो धन्वी तं च सौमित्रिरन्वगात् ॥

Vishvamitra went ahead. Then the greatly renowned Rama, with his bow and youthful locks, followed him, and Lakshmana followed Rama.

Verse 7

कलापिनौ धनुष्पाणी शोभयानौ दिशो दश । विश्वामित्रं महात्मानं त्रिशीर्षाविव पन्नगौ । अनुजग्मतुरक्षुद्रौ पितामहमिवाश्विनौ ॥

The two brothers, each carrying a bow and quiver, illuminated the ten directions. They followed the great-souled Vishvamitra like a three-headed serpent following its leader, and like the Ashvin twins following Brahma.

Verse 8

तदा कुशिकपुत्रं तु धनुष्पाणी स्वलङ्कृतौ । बद्धगोधाङ्गुलित्राणौ खड्गवन्तौ महाद्युती ॥

Then the two greatly radiant princes, beautifully adorned, carrying bows, wearing leather finger-protectors and bearing swords—

Verse 9

कुमारौ चारुवपुषौ भ्रातरौ रामलक्ष्मणौ । अनुयातौ श्रिया दीप्तौ शोभयेतामनिन्दितौ ॥

—the brothers Rama and Lakshmana, youthful and beautiful in form, radiant with their splendor, followed Vishvamitra and enhanced his appearance.

Verse 10

स्थाणुं देवमिवाचिन्त्यं कुमाराविव पावकी । अध्यर्धयोजनं गत्वा सरय्वा दक्षिणे तटे ॥

They appeared like the two sons of the Fire-god following the inconceivable Lord Shiva. After travelling one and a half yojanas along the southern bank of the Sarayu—

Verse 11

रामेति मधुरां वाणीं विश्वामित्रोऽभ्यभाषत । गृहाण वत्स सलिलं मा भूत्कालस्य पर्ययः ॥

Vishvamitra spoke in a sweet voice: “Rama, my child, take some water. Let there be no loss of time.”

Verse 12

मन्त्रग्रामं गृहाण त्वं बलामतिबलां तथा । न श्रमो न ज्वरो वा ते न रूपस्य विपर्ययः ॥

“Receive the group of mantras called Bala and Atibala. Then neither fatigue nor fever nor any change in your appearance will affect you.”

Verse 13

न च सुप्तं प्रमत्तं वा धर्षयिष्यन्ति नैरृताः । न बाह्वोः सदृशो वीर्ये पृथिव्यामस्ति कश्चन ॥

“The Rakshasas will not attack you whether you are asleep or careless. There will be no one on earth equal to you in the strength of your arms.”

Verse 14

त्रिषु लोकेषु वा राम न भवेत्सदृशस्तव । बलामतिबलां चैव पठतः तात राघव ॥

“O Rama, there will be none equal to you in the three worlds. My dear Raghava, by learning Bala and Atibala—”

Verse 15

न सौभाग्ये न दाक्षिण्ये न ज्ञाने बुद्धिनिश्चये । नोत्तरे प्रतिवक्तव्ये समो लोके तवानघ ॥

“—no one in the world will equal you in beauty, ability, knowledge, discernment, or in answering and responding, O sinless one.”

Verse 16

एतद्विद्याद्वये लब्धे न भवेत्सदृशस्तव । बलाचातिबला चैव सर्वज्ञानस्य मातरौ ॥

“Having obtained these two teachings, no one will be your equal. Bala and Atibala are the mothers of all knowledge.”

Verse 17

क्षुत्पिपासे न ते राम भविष्येते नरोत्तम । बलामतिबलां चैव पठतः तात राघव ॥

“O Rama, best of men, hunger and thirst will not trouble you while you learn Bala and Atibala, my dear Raghava.”

Verse 18

गृहाण सर्वलोकस्य गुप्तये रघुनन्दन । विद्याद्वयमधीयाने यशश्चाथ भवेद्भुवि ॥

“Receive these two teachings for the protection of all the worlds, O delight of the Raghus. By studying these two teachings, you shall attain great fame on earth.”

Verse 19

पितामहसुते ह्येते विद्ये तेजःसमन्विते । प्रदातुं तव काकुत्स्थ सदृशस्त्वं हि पार्थिव ॥

“These two radiant teachings are the daughters of the Grandsire Brahma. O Kakutstha, you alone are worthy of receiving them.”

Verse 20

कामं बहुगुणाः सर्वे त्वय्येते नात्र संशयः । तपसा सम्भृते चैते बहुरूपे भविष्यतः ॥

“All these many qualities are already abundantly present in you, without doubt. Yet, when cultivated through austerity, these teachings will produce many forms of excellence.”

Verse 21

ततो रामो जलं स्पृष्ट्वा प्रहृष्टवदनः शुचिः । प्रतिजग्राह ते विद्ये महर्षेर्भावितात्मनः ॥

Then Rama touched the water, purified himself, and with a delighted face received those two teachings from the great sage Vishvamitra, whose mind was devoted to austerity.

Verse 22

विद्यासमुदितो रामः शुशुभे भीमविक्रमः । सहस्ररश्मिर्भगवान् शरदीव दिवाकरः । गुरुकार्याणि सर्वाणि नियुज्य कुशिकात्मजे । ऊषुस्तां रजनीं तत्र सरय्वां सुसुखं त्रयः ॥

Rama, possessed of terrible valor and having obtained the teachings, shone like the thousand-rayed Sun in autumn. Having performed all the duties owed to his teacher, the three of them spent that night very happily on the bank of the Sarayu.

Verse 23

दशरथनृपसूनुसत्तमाभ्यां तृणशयनेऽनुचिते सहोषिताभ्याम् । कुशिकसुतवचोऽनुलालिताभ्यां सुखमिव सा विबभौ विभावरी च ॥

The night appeared pleasant to the two excellent sons of King Dasharatha, Rama and Lakshmana, although they slept together on an unaccustomed bed of grass, comforted by the gentle words of the son of Kushika.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे द्वाविंशः सर्गः ॥

Thus ends the twenty-second Sarga of Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the first epic composed by Sage Valmiki.
