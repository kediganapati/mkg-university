Ramayan — Bala Kanda — Sarga 38

Ramayan Bala Kanda

Verse 1

विश्वामित्रवचः श्रुत्वा राघवः सह लक्ष्मणः ।

उवाच परमप्रीतो विश्वामित्रं महामुनिम् ॥

Hearing the words of Vishvamitra, Rama, together with Lakshmana, became exceedingly pleased and spoke to the great sage.

Verse 2

कथयस्व महाभाग गङ्गावतरणं शुभम् ।

कथं त्रिभुवनं सर्वं पावयामास जाह्नवी ॥

“O greatly fortunate one, please narrate the auspicious descent of Ganga. How did Jahnavi purify all the three worlds?”

Verse 3

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं राघवं रघुनन्दनम् ॥

Hearing those words, the great sage Vishvamitra spoke sweetly to Rama, the delight of the Raghu dynasty.

Verse 4

शृणु राम महाभाग गङ्गावतरणं शुभम् ।

कथयिष्यामि ते सर्वं यथावद् रघुनन्दन ॥

“O greatly fortunate Rama, listen to the auspicious account of the descent of Ganga. O delight of the Raghu dynasty, I shall tell you everything exactly as it happened.”

Verse 5

राजा सगरो नाम सगरस्यैव चात्मजः ।

असमान्ज इति ख्यातः पुत्रस्तस्य महात्मनः ॥

There was a king named Sagara. His great-souled son was known as Asamanja.

Verse 6

केशिन्या चापि सञ्जाता पुत्रस्तस्य महात्मनः ।

सगरस्य नृपश्रेष्ठ तस्य नाम महाबलः ॥

O best of kings, that mighty son of the great King Sagara was born to his wife Keshini.

Verse 7

सगरस्य तु पुत्राणां षष्टिः पुत्रसहस्रकम् ।

और्वस्य वरदानाच्च जातं भरतसत्तम ॥

O best among the descendants of Bharata, through the boon granted by the sage Aurva, Sagara obtained sixty thousand sons.

Verse 8

ते सर्वे बलसम्पन्ना महाबलपराक्रमाः ।

पितुर्वचनमादाय दिग्विजयाय निर्ययुः ॥

All those sons were powerful and possessed great strength and valour. Obeying their father’s command, they set out to conquer the directions.

Verse 9

तेषां तु बलिनां वीर्यं न शक्नोति महीधरः ।

धारयितुं महाभाग तदा देवाः समागताः ॥

O greatly fortunate one, the earth itself could not bear the force and power of those mighty sons. Then the gods assembled.

Verse 10

ते सर्वे देवताः राम सगरस्य महात्मनः ।

पुत्राणां बलमासाद्य भीताः सर्वे समागताः ॥

O Rama, all those gods, having witnessed the power of the sons of the great-souled Sagara, became frightened and assembled together.

Verse 11

अथ देवगणाः सर्वे ब्रह्माणमुपचक्रमुः ।

प्रणिपत्य महात्मानं ब्रह्माणमिदमब्रुवन् ॥

Then all the hosts of gods approached Brahma. Bowing before the great-souled Brahma, they spoke these words.

Verse 12

भगवन् सगरस्यैते पुत्राः परमदारुणाः ।

भूमिं सर्वां खनिष्यन्ति यज्ञार्थं हयमिच्छवः ॥

“O Bhagavan, these sons of Sagara are exceedingly fierce. Desiring to recover the horse for the sacrifice, they will dig up the entire earth.”

Verse 13

तेषां तु कर्मणा देव पृथिवी क्षोभिता भवेत् ।

लोकाः सर्वे च संतप्ता भविष्यन्ति न संशयः ॥

“O Lord, because of their actions the earth will be disturbed, and all the worlds will suffer. There is no doubt about this.”

Verse 14

एवमुक्तस्तु देवैस्तु ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ।

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं देवानां हितकाम्यया ॥

Thus addressed by the gods, Brahma, the grandsire of the worlds, spoke sweet words for the welfare of the gods.

Verse 15

मया तेषां महाभागा वरः पूर्वं प्रदत्तवान् ।

न तेषां नाशमिच्छामि सत्यं सत्यं वदामि वः ॥

“I had previously granted them a boon. I do not desire their destruction. I tell you the truth, indeed the truth.”

Verse 16

तेषां पुत्रसहस्राणां नाशो भवति नान्यथा ।

कालस्य नियमेनेह सर्वेषां नात्र संशयः ॥

“The destruction of those thousands of sons will certainly take place according to the ordinance of Time. Of this there is no doubt.”

Verse 17

एवमुक्त्वा तु भगवान् ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ।

जगाम स्वं महद् धाम देवानां च यथागतम् ॥

Having spoken thus, Bhagavan Brahma, the grandsire of the worlds, returned to his own great abode, and the gods departed to their respective places.

Verse 18

ततः सगरपुत्रास्तु हयमन्वेषणाय वै ।

दिशो दश महाभागा विचेरुर्वसुधातले ॥

Then the sons of Sagara, O greatly fortunate one, searched for the sacrificial horse and wandered through the earth in all ten directions.

Verse 19

तेषां तु खनतां भूमिं समुद्रः समपद्यत ।

तेनासीत् सागरः ख्यातो लोकेऽस्मिन् सागरात्मजैः ॥

As they dug through the earth, the great ocean was formed. Because of the sons of Sagara, it became known in this world as Sagara.

Verse 20

ततः सगरपुत्रास्तु खनन्तो वसुधातलम् ।

ददृशुः कपिलं तत्र विष्णुमव्यक्तरूपिणम् ॥

Then, while digging through the surface of the earth, the sons of Sagara saw Kapila, Vishnu in his unmanifest form.

Verse 21

ते तमूचुर्महात्मानं कपिलं पुरुषोत्तमम् ।

अश्वं त्वं हरसे देव यज्ञियं नृपतेः प्रभो ॥

They addressed that great-souled being, Kapila, the supreme person: “O Lord, you have taken away the sacrificial horse belonging to our king.”

Verse 22

एवमुक्तस्तु कपिलः क्रोधसंरक्तलोचनः ।

हुङ्कारं तान् सुतान् कृत्वा भस्मराशीकृतान् प्रभुः ॥

Thus addressed, Kapila, his eyes reddened with anger, uttered a roar. By that roar, the sons were reduced to a heap of ashes.

Verse 23

ततः सगरपुत्राणां नाशं श्रुत्वा महात्मनाम् ।

सगरो दुःखसंतप्तो नृपश्रेष्ठोऽभवत् तदा ॥

Hearing of the destruction of his great-souled sons, Sagara, the best of kings, was then overcome with grief.

Verse 24

स पुत्रशोकसंतप्तः कपिलं पुरुषोत्तमम् ।

गत्वा तुष्टाव धर्मात्मा पुत्राणां मुक्तिहेतवे ॥

Afflicted with grief for his sons, the righteous Sagara approached Kapila, the supreme person, and praised him for the sake of securing liberation for his sons.

इति वाल्मीकि रामायणे आदि काव्ये बाल काण्डे अष्टात्रिंशः सर्गः ।

Thus ends the thirty-eighth Sarga of the Bala Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana, the original epic.
