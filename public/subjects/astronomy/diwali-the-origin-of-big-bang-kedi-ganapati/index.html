Before Creation: The State Beyond Identity

Parmeshwar was first alone, unborn, and omnipresent; no one existed except Parmeshwar; even Parmeshwar was not Parmeshwar himself, because to be Parmeshwar, Ishwar, Bhagwan, or Devta had to exist.

The Parmeshwar was not even the Ganapati himself, because to be the Ganapati, the Gana (spirit) had to exist. The Parmeshwar was not even Aadi himself, because to be Aadi, the existence of some other one later had to exist. The Parmeshwar was not even the male himself, because to be the male, the existence of the female was necessary.

The Nine Dimensions Within Parmeshwar

Each particle of Parmeshwar was composed of nine-dimensional elements; each particle contained nine dimensions, such as peace, time, space, energy, power, cycle, light, vibration, and matrix. Because the peace dimension existed in Parmeshwar’s particle, Parmeshwar was in a completely peaceful and stable state.

The Parmeshwar was constantly experiencing supreme peace and bliss in the same stable state. The Parmeshwar wanted to experience different states and emotions in different forms of Himself. Because the peace dimension existed in each particle of Parmeshwar, there could not be the slightest change or movement in Parmeshwar.

Creation Required the Destruction of Peace

In order for Parmeshwar to become changeable and dynamic, Parmeshwar had to destroy the peace dimension from their 9-dimensional particles. But Parmeshwar did not want to destroy the peace dimension from all their particles because Parmeshwar wanted to remain in their original state and also wanted to exist in different states; therefore, Parmeshwar destroyed the peace dimension from some area within their center.

Diwali: The Illumination of the Divine Center

As soon as Parmeshwar destroyed the peace dimension from their central part, all the dimensions—such as time, space, energy, power, cycle, light, vibration, matrix, etc.—that were present in each particle of Parmeshwar separated from each other, became dynamic, and illuminated. Light was already present at the center of Parmeshwar, and along with the light, all other dimensions were present, but due to the peace dimension, the light was in a stable state and could not reflect. Due to the destruction of the peace dimension, the light dimension collided with all the other dimensions, and suddenly the center of Parmeshwar became illuminated.

Formation of Hiranyagarbha Boundary

Parmeshwar Aadi Ganapati first created a boundary at the center of Himself. After that, He destroyed the peace dimension inside that boundary, and the Diwali cosmic event occurred inside that boundary. Outside the boundary, the peace dimension still existed, so no illumination happened outside it.

That boundary is the cover of Hiranyagarbha. To keep the 8-dimensional illuminated dynamic space separate from the 9-dimensional stable space, Parmeshwar Aadi Ganapati created the cover of that boundary and converted the entire 8-dimensional illuminated dynamic space into Hiranyagarbha. Our universe is inside Hiranyagarbha, and it has fixed space; the universe is not expanding.

Spiritual Vision: Seeing the Cosmic Events

Whenever Bhagwan incarnates on Earth to reestablish Sanatan Dharma, he explains the creation of God and the universe. That’s why all Indians celebrate this cosmic event as the Diwali festival. Indian sages have often observed this cosmic event with their 9-dimensional spiritual vision, just as I have seen this cosmic event.

Every human being is born with this information within them, but they have to achieve 9-dimensional spiritual vision to look within themselves, and then they can see this cosmic event happening before their eyes.

I was weak, so it took me 18 years to attain the vision of 9 dimensions. I made the wish in 2001, then forgot about it along the way, and finally achieved it in 2024. If you are not as forgetful as I was, you may attain the 9-dimensional vision in about 10–12 years of dedicated spiritual practice.

In this practice, you must understand the origin of everything. The 9-dimensional vision becomes active only when we repeatedly try to discover the source of things. Simply practicing meditation will bring peace, but not knowledge. For true knowledge, self-reflection is essential. When you deeply reflect to understand the origin of everything, you will attain the 9-dimensional vision.

Big Bang vs Diwali: Law of Creation

Western scientists call this Diwali cosmic event the Big Bang. But they cannot see how and why this Big Bang happened. They even think that before the Big Bang there was nothing, but in reality, nothing is created from nothing—that is the first law of creation. They also think that the universe is expanding, but in reality, nothing is created without boundaries—that is the second law of creation.

The most important law of creation is the destruction of peace before creation. The entire universe is created by the law of creation, and even the Diwali cosmic Big Bang event happened because the law of creation was followed and the peace dimension was destroyed.

Don’t Expand the Universe, Expand Mind!

Kedi: “Is the universe really expanding?”

Ganesh: “If the universe were truly expanding, then the distances between planets and stars would also be expanding. But all planets and stars move along fixed celestial paths. Their trajectories are constant; that is why, from Satya Yuga to Kali Yuga, calculations of planetary positions have been possible. Next time someone says the universe is expanding, expand that thought instead.”

Kedi: “Some scientists say that different galaxies are expanding because the measured energy of light appears different. Is that also false?”

Ganesh: “Just as a planet sometimes appears to move forward in transit and sometimes appears retrograde, and just as the distance from the Sun sometimes appears to increase and sometimes decrease, the distance of every celestial body appears to change at times. The heat of light also changes according to distance—when the distance increases, less heat is received; when the distance decreases, more heat is received.

The light dimension is connected to the vibration dimension. Because of the vibration dimension, light is able to reach a place. When distance and position change, the vibration, heat, and even the reflected color of light also change. This is common sense for a scientist. Even after knowing this, if a scientist still says that the universe is expanding, then he is not a scientist but a seeker of science.”

Conclusion

Diwali is the first moment of creation and illumination. It is a matter of pride for all Hindus who celebrate this festival. For those who do not celebrate Diwali—even if you are not Hindu—your dharma is still Sanatan Dharma; therefore, you too should celebrate Diwali as the first moment of creation, illumination, or the Big Bang.
