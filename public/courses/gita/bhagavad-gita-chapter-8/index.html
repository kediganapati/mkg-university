Bhagavad Gita — Chapter 8

Bhagvad Gita

Shloka 8:1

अर्जुन उवाच ।
किं तद्ब्रह्म किमध्यात्मं किं कर्म पुरुषोत्तम ।
अधिभूतं च किं प्रोक्तमधिदैवं किमुच्यते ॥ ८:१ ॥

arjuna uvacha
kim tad brahma kim adhyatmam kim karma purushottama
adhibhutam cha kim proktam adhidaivam kim uchyate

Translation

Arjuna said: O Supreme Person, what is Brahman? What is the Self (Adhyatma)? What is Karma? What is declared to be Adhibhuta, and what is called Adhidaiva?

Conversation

Shloka 8:2

अधियज्ञः कथं कोऽत्र देहेऽस्मिन्मधुसूदन ।
प्रयाणकाले च कथं ज्ञेयोऽसि नियतात्मभिः ॥ ८:२ ॥

adhiyajnah katham ko ’tra dehe ‘smin madhusudana
prayana-kale cha katham jneyo ‘si niyatatmabhih

Translation

O Madhusudana, who is Adhiyajna in this body, and how does He dwell here? And how are You to be known by the self-controlled at the time of death?

Conversation

Shloka 8:3

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।
अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमं स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते ।
भूतभावोद्भवकरो विसर्गः कर्मसंज्ञितः ॥ ८:३ ॥

shri bhagavan uvacha
aksharam brahma paramam svabhavo ‘dhyatmam uchyate
bhuta-bhavodbhava-karo visargah karma-samjnitah

Translation

The Blessed Lord said: The Imperishable Supreme is Brahman. One’s own essential nature is called Adhyatma, and the offering that causes the creation of beings is known as Karma.

Conversation

Shloka 8:4

अधिभूतं क्षरो भावः पुरुषश्चाधिदैवतम् ।
अधियज्ञोऽहमेवात्र देहे देहभृतां वर ॥ ८:४ ॥

adhibhutam ksharo bhavah purushash chadhidaivatam
adhiyajno ‘ham evatra dehe deha-bhritam vara

Translation

Adhibhuta is the perishable world of beings; Adhidaiva is the Cosmic Person; and I alone am Adhiyajna here in this body, O best of embodied beings.

Conversation

Shloka 8:5

अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेवरम् ।
यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः ॥ ८:५ ॥

anta-kale cha mam eva smaran muktva kalevaram
yah prayati sa mad-bhavam yati nasty atra samshayah

Translation

Whoever departs from the body at the time of death remembering Me alone attains My divine nature. Of this there is no doubt.

Conversation

Shloka 8:6

यं यं वापि स्मरन्भावं त्यजत्यन्ते कलेवरम् ।
तं तमेवैति कौन्तेय सदा तद्भावभावितः ॥ ८:६ ॥

yam yam vapi smaran bhavam tyajaty ante kalevaram
tam tam evaiti kaunteya sada tad-bhava-bhavitah

Translation

Whatever state of being one remembers at the time of death while leaving the body, O Kaunteya, that state alone one attains, having always been absorbed in it.

Conversation

Shloka 8:7

तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयम् ॥ ८:७ ॥

tasmat sarveshu kaleshu mam anusmara yudhya cha
mayy arpita-mano-buddhir mam evaishyasy asamshayam

Translation

Therefore, remember Me at all times and also perform your duty of fighting. With your mind and intellect dedicated to Me, you shall surely attain Me.

Conversation

Shloka 8:8

अभ्यासयोगयुक्तेन चेतसा नान्यगामिना ।
परमं पुरुषं दिव्यं याति पार्थानुचिन्तयन् ॥ ८:८ ॥

abhyasa-yoga-yuktena chetasa nanya-gamina
paramam purusham divyam yati parthanuchintayan

Translation

With the mind disciplined through constant practice of yoga and not wandering elsewhere, one who continuously meditates upon the Supreme Divine Person attains Him, O Partha.

Conversation

Shloka 8:9

कविं पुराणमनुशासितारमणोरणीयांसमनुस्मरेद्यः ।
सर्वस्य धातारमचिन्त्यरूपमादित्यवर्णं तमसः परस्तात् ॥ ८:९ ॥

kavim puranam anushasitaram anor aniyamsam anusmared yah
sarvasya dhataram achintya-rupam aditya-varnam tamasah parastat

Translation

One should meditate upon the Supreme Person, who is the omniscient, the ancient, the ruler of all, subtler than the subtlest, the sustainer of everything, inconceivable in form, self-effulgent like the sun, and beyond all darkness.

Conversation

Shloka 8:10

प्रयाणकाले मनसाचलेन भक्त्या युक्तो योगबलेन चैव ।
भ्रुवोर्मध्ये प्राणमावेश्य सम्यक् स तं परं पुरुषमुपैति दिव्यम् ॥ ८:१० ॥

prayana-kale manasachalena bhaktya yukto yoga-balena chaiva
bhruvor madhye pranam avesya samyak sa tam param purusham upaiti divyam

Translation

At the time of death, one who, with an unwavering mind, devotion, and the power of yoga, fixes the life-breath between the eyebrows and remembers the Supreme Divine Person attains Him.

Conversation

Shloka 8:11

यदक्षरं वेदविदो वदन्ति विशन्ति यद्यतयो वीतरागाः ।
यदिच्छन्तो ब्रह्मचर्यं चरन्ति तत्ते पदं सङ्ग्रहेण प्रवक्ष्ये ॥ ८:११ ॥

yad aksharam veda-vido vadanti vishanti yad yatayo vita-ragah
yad icchanto brahmacharyam charanti tat te padam sangrahena pravakshye

Translation

I shall now briefly explain to you that imperishable goal which the knowers of the Vedas declare, into which the self-controlled and detached sages enter, and desiring which people practice celibacy.

Conversation

Shloka 8:12

सर्वद्वाराणि संयम्य मनो हृदि निरुध्य च ।
मूर्ध्न्याधायात्मनः प्राणमास्थितो योगधारणाम् ॥ ८:१२ ॥

sarva-dvarani samyamya mano hridi nirudhya cha
murdhny adhayatmanah pranam asthito yoga-dharanam

Translation

Controlling all the senses, fixing the mind in the heart, and establishing the life-breath at the crown of the head, one should remain steadfast in the practice of yoga.

Conversation

Shloka 8:13

ओमित्येकाक्षरं ब्रह्म व्याहरन्मामनुस्मरन् ।
यः प्रयाति त्यजन्देहं स याति परमां गतिम् ॥ ८:१३ ॥

om ity ekaksharam brahma vyaharan mam anusmaran
yah prayati tyajan deham sa yati paramam gatim

Translation

Whoever departs from the body while uttering the one-syllable Brahman, Om, and remembering Me, attains the supreme destination.

Conversation

Shloka 8:14

अनन्यचेताः सततं यो मां स्मरति नित्यशः ।
तस्याहं सुलभः पार्थ नित्ययुक्तस्य योगिनः ॥ ८:१४ ॥

ananya-chetah satatam yo mam smarati nityashah
tasyaham sulabhah partha nitya-yuktasya yoginah

Translation

O Partha, I am easily attainable by that yogi who always remembers Me with an undivided mind and remains constantly united with Me.

Conversation

Shloka 8:15

मामुपेत्य पुनर्जन्म दुःखालयमशाश्वतम् ।
नाप्नुवन्ति महात्मानः संसिद्धिं परमां गताः ॥ ८:१५ ॥

mam upetya punar janma duhkhalayam ashashvatam
napnuvanti mahatmanah samsiddhim paramam gatah

Translation

Having attained Me, the great souls do not take birth again in this temporary world of suffering, for they have reached the highest perfection.

Conversation

Shloka 8:16

आब्रह्मभुवनाल्लोकाः पुनरावर्तिनोऽर्जुन ।
मामुपेत्य तु कौन्तेय पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते ॥ ८:१६ ॥

a-brahma-bhuvanal lokah punar avartino ‘rjuna
mam upetya tu kaunteya punar janma na vidyate

Translation

O Arjuna, all worlds up to the realm of Brahma are subject to repeated birth and death. But having attained Me, O Kaunteya, there is no rebirth.

Conversation

Shloka 8:17

सहस्रयुगपर्यन्तमहर्यद्ब्रह्मणो विदुः ।
रात्रिं युगसहस्रान्तां तेऽहोरात्रविदो जनाः ॥ ८:१७ ॥

sahasra-yuga-paryantam ahar yad brahmano viduh
ratrim yuga-sahasrantam te ‘ho-ratra-vido janah

Translation

Those who understand the nature of day and night know that a day of Brahma lasts for a thousand yugas, and his night also extends for a thousand yugas.

Conversation

Shloka 8:18

अव्यक्ताद्व्यक्तयः सर्वाः प्रभवन्त्यहरागमे ।
रात्र्यागमे प्रलीयन्ते तत्रैवाव्यक्तसंज्ञके ॥ ८:१८ ॥

avyaktad vyaktayah sarvah prabhavanty ahar-agame
ratry-agame praliyante tatraivavyakta-samjnake

Translation

At the beginning of Brahma’s day, all manifested beings arise from the unmanifest, and at the arrival of his night, they dissolve back into the unmanifest.

Conversation

Shloka 8:19

भूतग्रामः स एवायं भूत्वा भूत्वा प्रलीयते ।
रात्र्यागमेऽवशः पार्थ प्रभवत्यहरागमे ॥ ८:१९ ॥

bhuta-gramah sa evayam bhutva bhutva praliyate
ratry-agame ‘vashah partha prabhavaty ahar-agame

Translation

O Partha, this multitude of beings repeatedly comes into existence and is dissolved again at the arrival of night, only to reappear when the day begins.

Conversation

Shloka 8:20

परस्तस्मात्तु भावोऽन्योऽव्यक्तोऽव्यक्तात्सनातनः ।
यः स सर्वेषु भूतेषु नश्यत्सु न विनश्यति ॥ ८:२० ॥

paras tasmat tu bhavo ’nyo ‘vyakto ‘vyaktat sanatanah
yah sa sarveshu bhuteshu nashyatsu na vinashyati

Translation

Yet beyond this unmanifest is another eternal, transcendental unmanifest reality, which does not perish even when all beings perish.

Conversation

Shloka 8:21

अव्यक्तोऽक्षर इत्युक्तस्तमाहुः परमां गतिम् ।
यं प्राप्य न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥ ८:२१ ॥

avyakto ‘kshara ity uktas tam ahuh paramam gatim
yam prapya na nivartante tad dhama paramam mama

Translation

That unmanifest and imperishable reality is called the supreme destination. Having attained it, one never returns; that is My supreme abode.

Conversation

Shloka 8:22

पुरुषः स परः पार्थ भक्त्या लभ्यस्त्वनन्यया ।
यस्यान्तःस्थानि भूतानि yeन सर्वमिदं ततम् ॥ ८:२२ ॥

purushah sa parah partha bhaktya labhyas tv ananyaya
yasyantah-sthani bhutani yena sarvam idam tatam

Translation

O Partha, that Supreme Person, within whom all beings dwell and by whom this entire universe is pervaded, is attained only through exclusive devotion.

Conversation

Shloka 8:23

यत्र काले त्वनावृत्तिमावृत्तिं चैव योगिनः ।
प्रयाता यान्ति तं कालं वक्ष्यामि भरतर्षभ ॥ ८:२३ ॥

yatra kale tv anavrittim avrittim chaiva yoginah
prayata yanti tam kalam vakshyami bharatarshabha

Translation

O best of the Bharatas, I shall now explain the times at which departing yogis either do not return or return again to this world.

Conversation

Shloka 8:24

अग्निर्ज्योतिरहः शुक्लः षण्मासा उत्तरायणम् ।
तत्र प्रयाता गच्छन्ति ब्रह्म ब्रह्मविदो जनाः ॥ ८:२४ ॥

agnir jyotir ahah shuklah shan-masa uttarayanam
tatra prayata gachchhanti brahma brahma-vido janah

Translation

Those who know Brahman and depart during the path of fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, and the six months of the sun’s northern course attain Brahman.

Conversation

Shloka 8:25

धूमो रात्रिस्तथा कृष्णः षण्मासा दक्षिणायनम् ।
तत्र चान्द्रमसं ज्योतिर्योगी प्राप्य निवर्तते ॥ ८:२५ ॥

dhumo ratris tatha krishnah shan-masa dakshinayanam
tatra chandramasam jyotir yogi prapya nivartate

Translation

The yogi who departs during the path of smoke, night, the dark fortnight, and the six months of the sun’s southern course reaches the lunar light and returns again.

Conversation

Shloka 8:26

शुक्लकृष्णे गती ह्येते जगतः शाश्वते मते ।
एकया यात्यनावृत्तिमन्ययावर्तते पुनः ॥ ८:२६ ॥

shukla-krishne gati hy ete jagatah shashvate mate
ekaya yaty anavrittim anyayavartate punah

Translation

These two paths, the bright and the dark, are considered the eternal ways of the world. By the one, a person does not return; by the other, one returns again.

Conversation

Shloka 8:27

नैते सृती पार्थ जानन्योगी मुह्यति कश्चन ।
तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु योगयुक्तो भवार्जुन ॥ ८:२७ ॥

naite sriti partha janan yogi muhyati kashchana
tasmat sarveshu kaleshu yoga-yukto bhavarjuna

Translation

O Partha, knowing these two paths, no yogi is ever deluded. Therefore, O Arjuna, remain steadfast in yoga at all times.

Conversation

Shloka 8:28

वेदेषु यज्ञेषु तपःसु चैव दानेषु यत्पुण्यफलं प्रदिष्टम् ।
अत्येति तत्सर्वमिदं विदित्वा योगी परं स्थानमुपैति चाद्यम् ॥ ८:२८ ॥

vedeshu yajneshu tapahsu chaiva daneshu yat punya-phalam pradishtam
atyeti tat sarvam idam viditva yogi param sthanam upaiti chadyam

Translation

The yogi who understands this teaching transcends all the merits declared to arise from the study of the Vedas, sacrifices, austerities, and charity, and attains the supreme eternal abode.
